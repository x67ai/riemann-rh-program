### Rows 1–2 — the Markov apparatus (report `a01.md`, COMPLETE, harvested 2026-09-10 00:58 IST)
- **Identity:** both EXACT. Warsaw [4] = r5-01 (its Theorem 1, p. 462); Warsaw [3] = r5-02 (Theorems 1–3, p. 166, matching the Warsaw p. 257–258 questions one-to-one). r5-01 Theorem 4.1 = Duminy = Warsaw Theorem 1.3; r5-01 cites the Warsaw paper as a 1988 manuscript "[C-C 3]".
- **Markov LMS, the printed definition (r5-02 pp. 165–166, 169–170; r5-01 p. 463 clauses (1)–(4); the two agree):** an exceptional local minimal set X (minimal for 𝓕|U, U open saturated) such that on a transversal Cantor set C = X ∩ T the holonomy pseudogroup Γ|C is generated by a single locally homeomorphic map τ: C → C that is *essentially conjugate* (semiconjugate via a continuous surjection whose fibers are singletons or the two endpoints of a gap) to a one-sided subshift of finite type. Hurder's "generated by a subshift of finite type" is a compression of this. Standing hypotheses differ only in bookkeeping (r5-02: M closed, orientability not assumed; r5-01: M compact orientable with boundary leaves, U connected).
- **The semiproperness-dropping theorem (r5-01 Theorem 1, p. 462, verbatim in a01 §3):** M compact orientable, 𝓕 C² transversely orientable codimension one, ∂M leaves, X a Markov LMS, **L any leaf of 𝓕|X** ⟹ ℰ*(L), the ends asymptotic to X, is a Cantor set. C² is used only via Lemma 1.7 in the minimal case and is "crucial" (Theorem 3, p. 471) for LMS at level ≥ 1.
- **Ends, infinite level, leaves outside X:** r5-02 says nothing about ends (its theorems: unique contraction of relative holonomy, finitely many semiproper leaves, Lebesgue measure zero). Neither paper mentions infinite level (the only "level" sentence: r5-02 p. 170, Markov LMS "at any desired finite level"). A leaf outside X whose closure contains X is treated only as a border leaf of U (r5-01 Theorem 3, §4) — such leaves have a proper side; nothing about a non-semiproper leaf outside X.
- **Checklist for any future program theorem about K (a01 §3, recorded, not judged):** the hypotheses visibly NOT among (D1)–(D6) are L ⊂ X (the (P0)/(P1)/(P∞) trichotomy — in (P∞) Theorem 1 does not reach L at all) and the subshift condition on Γ|C. r5-02 §7 prints exceptional minimal sets that are NOT Markov (Fuchsian groups of the second kind with parabolics), so the condition is not automatic.
- **Record:** nothing contradicts it. Two sharpenings for the next program stream: Hurder's gloss is to be read as above; r5-01 p. 461 prints ℰ*(L) as ends "asymptotic to L" — a misprint for X (vision at 240 dpi).
