## Proposal (Phase 3, immutable)

### Thesis
Because the Zeta23 inertia certificate is completely blind to the depth |β−1/2| of off-line zeros, re-running it in windows of length H = T^θ (θ > 3−√6 ≈ 0.5505) around an arbitrary height yields the first structural constraints that hold uniformly down to depth 10^(−1000) — a certified "wanted poster" saying any RH counterexample must be shallow, locally sparse at every polynomial scale, and immersed in a ≥(2−1/θ−θ/3)-fraction simple-on-line environment — which simultaneously kills the dense-carrier-wave failure scenario, organizes the Λ>0 and statistics channels honestly, and identifies the cheapest new statistic (band-edge regularity of the unconditional form factor at α=1) that would shrink both the formal ceiling and the Alternative-Hypothesis world.

### Mechanism

Form G_kl = W(f_k,f_l) from the Weil explicit formula.

OUTPUT (the wanted poster). Combining as in §6, for every window I of length H = T^θ:
(a) N₀ˢ(I) ≥ (2 − 1/θ − θ/3 − ε)·N(I) — simple-and-on-line zeros in EVERY short window;
(b) N_off(I) ≤ (1/θ + θ/3 − 1 + ε)·N(I) — from N(I) ≥ N_on(I) ≥ s₁ and (a): off-line zeros (with multiplicity) cannot exceed local density κ(θ)−1; numerically ≤ 0.334·N(I) at θ=1, ≤ 0.412 at θ=0.9, ≤ 0.517 at θ=0.8, ≤ 0.662 at θ=0.7, ≤ 0.867 at θ=0.6, vacuous at θ ≤ 3−√6;

WHY THIS IS THE RIGHT REFUTATION INSTRUMENT. (1) Depth-uniformity: every classical constraint on off-line zeros (Guth–Maynard N(σ,T) ≪ T^{30(1−σ)/13}, all zero-density technology) degenerates as δ = β−1/2 → 0 and says literally nothing for δ ≪ 1/log T — exactly the Lehmer/carrier-wave regime where a counterexample would live (Farmer 2211.11671). Inertia prices a pair at depth 10^(−1000) identically to depth 0.4. (2) Every-window (not almost-all): the constraint holds at the counterexample's own height γ₀, so it directly constrains the neighborhood of any hypothetical off-line zero: at all scales γ₀^θ, θ > 0.5505, its companions are outnumbered ≥ 2:1 by certified simple on-line zeros (θ=1). (3) It kills the dense-failure scenario: Farmer's carrier-wave picture, in its off-line variant, has long stretches of zeros pushed off the line by a slow modulation; (b) forbids off-line local density 1 in any window longer than γ₀^{0.5505+ε}, and density > 1/3 at scale γ₀. (4) It closes the loop with Part I's visibility analysis: the transcript's empirical visibility thresholds required an isolation hypothesis (HR); the wanted poster CERTIFIES partial isolation (sparsity at all polynomial scales) unconditionally, making a visibility theorem under certified sparsity (milestone 6) meaningful for the first time.

CHANNEL ASSESSMENTS BUILT INTO THE PROGRAM. ### Novelty
Relative to the Zeta23 paper: all its theorems are dyadic-window (T,2T] statements; localization in window position/length is an axis the 54-hour campaign never varied (it varied bandwidth, moments, weights, and windows-as-tapers only). Relative to short-interval literature: Karatsuba (1984, H ≥ T^{27/82}) and successors give ≫ H log T critical-line zeros in short intervals but no explicit proportion near 2/3, no simplicity, and — decisively — no upper bound on off-line counts; to my knowledge NO short-interval simple-zero proportion exists at any exponent (flagged for a literature check), and no off-line local-density cap of any kind exists that is uniform in depth. Relative to zero-density (Guth–Maynard arXiv:2405.20552 and the ANTEDB successors): those bounds are vacuous for depth δ ≪ 1/log T; (b) is the first constraint of positive strength in that regime. Relative to GLSS arXiv:2503.15449/2507.06823: their 100%-simple conclusions assume full-support PCC; ours are unconditional and local. Relative to Farmer arXiv:2211.11671: converts "no reasons to doubt" from a rebuttal-essay into certified structural theorems a counterexample must satisfy, and makes his no-statistic claim a formal no-go within a precise certificate class while exhibiting the exact loophole (prime sums of length e^{c/δ}). Relative to Rodgers–Tao: adds the explicit depth→Λ quantitative bridge as a stated target rather than folklore. The refutation-program framing itself — wanted poster (structure) + bridge (Λ) + no-go (statistics) as one certified search-space-narrowing instrument — has no antecedent; the literature has "no serious counterexample-search program exists" (literature.md §9).

Additionally the localized certificate is not even available for DH/Epstein: their log-derivative coefficients are uncontrolled (DH has zeros with β > 1, so −F′/F coefficients grow polynomially; Epstein class# > 1 similarly), so the prime-side mean-value step (Prop 5.6) fails for them — the certificate consumes an input those objects violate, which is why they may (and do) have off-line zeros our theorem would forbid for ζ.
- **Alternative Hypothesis (bandwidth-1 indistinguishability)** — Confronted head-on rather than evaded: the program's own assessment is that AH survives the 2/3 theorem (the 256-law is an AH-flavored certified witness). The localized first theorem uses only bandwidth-one data and therefore cannot and does not claim to kill AH. - **Bandwidth-one certificate ceiling 0.6818287** — No claimed constant exceeds it: the localized constants 2−1/θ−θ/3 ≤ 2−1/c*_1 = 0.6725 < 0.68183. ### First theorem
THEOREM (Localized certificate; the wanted poster). For every θ ∈ (3−√6, 1] and ε > 0 there exists T₀(θ,ε) such that for all T ≥ T₀ and every H with T^θ ≤ H ≤ T, writing I = (T, T+H], N(I) for nontrivial zeros with ordinate in I counted with multiplicity, N₀ˢ(I) for simple zeros on Re s = 1/2, N_d(I) for distinct zeros, and N_off(I) for zeros with Re ρ ≠ 1/2 counted with multiplicity:
(a) N₀ˢ(I) ≥ (2 − 1/θ − θ/3 − ε)·N(I);
(b) N_off(I) ≤ (1/θ + θ/3 − 1 + ε)·N(I);
(c) N_d(I) ≥ ((3 − 1/θ − θ/3)/2 − ε)·N(I);
uniformly over the window position T, and — the point — uniformly over the depths |β−1/2| ∈ (0,1/2) of whatever off-line zeros exist. Refinement: with the Montgomery–Taylor window, 1/θ + θ/3 improves to 1/c*_θ, c*_θ = √2·tan(θ/√2)/(1+(θ/√2)tan(θ/√2)).
COROLLARY (anti-clustering, depth-uniform): there is no height γ₀ and window of length ≥ γ₀^{0.5506} in which more than a fraction (1/θ+θ/3−1+ε) of zeros are off the critical line; in particular a hypothetical counterexample's off-line companions have local density ≤ 1/3+ε at scale γ₀ and its γ₀^θ-neighborhoods carry a certified majority of simple on-line zeros.

### Failure modes

- Hidden window-position dependence in the prime-side cross terms: an integration-by-parts boundary term in 𝓜[μ,P_X] or the ends E1/E2 proportional to T rather than H would shrink the admissible θ-range; mitigation is a smooth-in-position window weight, but if that interacts badly with the exact Poisson identity the constants degrade (route survives with worse θ₀).
- Literature preemption partially undiscovered: a short-interval simplicity or off-line-cap result may exist (Karatsuba-school, Ivić, or recent BGSTB follow-ups such as arXiv:2603.28104 'zeros in a narrow vertical box'); must be checked before claiming (a)-novelty — though the depth-uniform off-line cap (b) has no plausible antecedent since inertia-based counting is new to the 2026 paper.
- Scope honesty: the wanted poster constrains OFF-LINE failure scenarios only; a 'carrier-wave' near-failure that keeps all zeros on the line with pathological spacings is untouched by (b), and AH survives — the program says so explicitly, but a reader wanting counterexample-EXCLUSION will find only density constraints, never exclusion (the method is insensitive to o(N) off-line zeros, per the paper's own §1.5).
- M4 may be hard: edge regularity of F at α=1 could secretly require length-T^{1+ε} information (the atom at the edge may be exactly as inaccessible as F beyond the band); the LP-re-run then only prices an unprovable theorem.
