## Proposal (Phase 3, immutable)

### Thesis
Because the Zeta23 inertia certificate is completely blind to the depth |β−1/2| of off-line zeros, re-running it in windows of length H = T^θ (θ > 3−√6 ≈ 0.5505) around an arbitrary height yields the first structural constraints that hold uniformly down to depth 10^(−1000) — a certified "wanted poster" saying any RH counterexample must be shallow, locally sparse at every polynomial scale, and immersed in a ≥(2−1/θ−θ/3)-fraction simple-on-line environment — which simultaneously kills the dense-carrier-wave failure scenario, organizes the Λ>0 and statistics channels honestly, and identifies the cheapest new statistic (band-edge regularity of the unconditional form factor at α=1) that would shrink both the formal ceiling and the Alternative-Hypothesis world.

### Mechanism
OBJECTS. Fix θ ∈ (3−√6, 1], a window I = (T, T+H] with H = T^θ, and bandwidth λ = θ−ε′ < θ, L = λ·log(T/2π), X = e^L = (T/2π)^λ. Take the paper's Gabor family f_k(u) = φ(u)e^{−iτ_k u} on the grid τ_k = T + 2πk/L, but now with d = ⌊HL/2π⌋ ≈ λ·N(I) frequencies covering only I (the Gabor–Poisson identity Σ_k φ̂(γ−τ_k)φ̂(γ′−τ_k) = LΦ(γ−γ′) is grid-translation-invariant — Poisson.lean proves it for arbitrary offset T — so nothing in the zero side cares where the window sits). Form G_kl = W(f_k,f_l) from the Weil explicit formula.

ZERO SIDE (already window-parametric in the repo): Â = P + Q + tail exactly as in Prop 4.1/ZeroBlockData: P ⪰ 0 from on-line points in I′ = (T−D₀, T+H+D₀], rank P ≤ s₁+s₂, tr P ≤ N_on(I′) via the sampling identity; each off-line pair {ρ, 1−ρ̄} with ordinate in I′ a signature-(1,1) block with n₊(Q) ≤ p_I, REGARDLESS of depth — this is the depth-blindness that classical zero-density methods lack. Rank–trace at c = 2 (Lemma R, formalized, provably tight): 3s₁ + 4s₂ + 4p_I ≥ 4·tr Â − ‖Â‖²_F.

PRIME SIDE, localized (the only genuinely new work): tr G̃ over the short grid = aL·N(I)(1+o(1)) — the μ-density integral localizes trivially (μ ≈ l/2π is flat across I), and the P_X contribution is O(L√X) = O(l·T^{λ/2}) = o(Hl) since λ < 2θ. For the second moment, the diagonal prime term gives (H/π)·Σ_{n≤X}Λ(n)²n^{−1}g(log n) ≈ HL³/6π, while the off-diagonal is bounded by the SAME window-independent Montgomery–Vaughan estimate O₁ ≪ L²X as in Prop 5.6 (MV with δ_n = 1/(2n) bounds the bilinear form by the diagonal Σ Λ(n)²·n·n^{−1} ≈ XL with no window factor; this is visible in the Lean file PPOffDiag.lean:40, whose bound 16·C_MV·(∫Φ²)·Σ_{n≤X}Λ(n)² contains no T). Admissibility: L²X = o(HL³) ⟺ X = o(HL) ⟺ λ < θ. Tail: choose the free parameter D₀ = T^{θ/4+ε} (Prop 4.2 allows any D₀ ∈ [T^{λ/4+ε}, T^{1−ε}]), giving θ₀ ≪ l·T^{λ/2−θ/2−2ε} → 0 and boundary count N(I′∖I) ≪ D₀·l = o(Hl). Hence, unconditionally, tr Ĝ = N(I)(1+o(1)) and ‖Ĝ‖²_F = (κ(λ)+o(1))·N(I), κ(λ) = 1/λ + λ/3.

OUTPUT (the wanted poster). Combining as in §6, for every window I of length H = T^θ:
(a) N₀ˢ(I) ≥ (2 − 1/θ − θ/3 − ε)·N(I) — simple-and-on-line zeros in EVERY short window;
(b) N_off(I) ≤ (1/θ + θ/3 − 1 + ε)·N(I) — from N(I) ≥ N_on(I) ≥ s₁ and (a): off-line zeros (with multiplicity) cannot exceed local density κ(θ)−1; numerically ≤ 0.334·N(I) at θ=1, ≤ 0.412 at θ=0.9, ≤ 0.517 at θ=0.8, ≤ 0.662 at θ=0.7, ≤ 0.867 at θ=0.6, vacuous at θ ≤ 3−√6;
(c) N_d(I) ≥ ((3−1/θ−θ/3)/2 − ε)·N(I). With the Montgomery–Taylor window (ThmD machinery at bandwidth θ), 1/θ+θ/3 improves to 1/c*_θ.

WHY THIS IS THE RIGHT REFUTATION INSTRUMENT. (1) Depth-uniformity: every classical constraint on off-line zeros (Guth–Maynard N(σ,T) ≪ T^{30(1−σ)/13}, all zero-density technology) degenerates as δ = β−1/2 → 0 and says literally nothing for δ ≪ 1/log T — exactly the Lehmer/carrier-wave regime where a counterexample would live (Farmer 2211.11671). Inertia prices a pair at depth 10^(−1000) identically to depth 0.4. (2) Every-window (not almost-all): the constraint holds at the counterexample's own height γ₀, so it directly constrains the neighborhood of any hypothetical off-line zero: at all scales γ₀^θ, θ > 0.5505, its companions are outnumbered ≥ 2:1 by certified simple on-line zeros (θ=1). (3) It kills the dense-failure scenario: Farmer's carrier-wave picture, in its off-line variant, has long stretches of zeros pushed off the line by a slow modulation; (b) forbids off-line local density 1 in any window longer than γ₀^{0.5505+ε}, and density > 1/3 at scale γ₀. (4) It closes the loop with Part I's visibility analysis: the transcript's empirical visibility thresholds required an isolation hypothesis (HR); the wanted poster CERTIFIES partial isolation (sparsity at all polynomial scales) unconditionally, making a visibility theorem under certified sparsity (milestone 6) meaningful for the first time.

CHANNEL ASSESSMENTS BUILT INTO THE PROGRAM. Λ-channel: since Rodgers–Tao gives Λ ≥ 0, a certified Λ ≥ 10^{−N} is logically equivalent to ¬RH; concretely it requires a certified non-real zero of some H_t, t > 0 (a finite argument-principle/interval-arithmetic computation, Polymath15's effective machinery reused in reverse — feasible IF one knows where to look), and no independent shortcut exists: backward heat flow from Λ ≤ 0.2 data is exponentially ill-posed, and forward flow of an RH-true configuration keeps all zeros real for every t > 0. So the honest role of the Λ-channel is the quantitative bridge: an off-line zero of depth δ at height γ₀ forces Λ ≳ c·δ²/polylog(γ₀) via the pair-attraction ODE dy/dt ≈ −1/y of the flow (exact exponent to be pinned in milestone 6; the qualitative statement Λ > 0 ⟺ such a zero exists is Rodgers–Tao + de Bruijn) — converting any future find into a certified strictly-positive Λ. Statistics channel: Farmer is right for zero-statistics — any functional of zeros below height T is trivially consistent with both worlds; the formalizable small no-go (milestone 5) exhibits two ZeroConfigs in the repo's abstract configuration language agreeing below T, satisfying all certified constraints, one RH-true and one not; the only genuine influence channel is prime sums of length e^{c/δ} (to resolve depth δ), computationally void for δ ≪ 1. AH assessment: the Alternative Hypothesis fully survives the new theorems — the 256-periodic ceiling law (marks {1,2}, near-CUE band data, simple fraction 0.68183 > 2/3) is itself an AH-flavored world consistent with every theorem in the artifact, including the localized ones (which consume only bandwidth-one data). AH forces the form factor to be 2-periodic, so F_AH(1+u) = 1−u+o(1) versus F_GUE = 1 on (1,2); the discriminators are (i) the band-edge derivative/regularity of F at α = 1⁺ and (ii) the Wiener long-run average of F, which under AH equals the sum of squared atoms of the half-integer spacing measure — to be tested against the RH-conditional Carneiro–Milinovich–Ramos window [0.9303, 1.3208] (whether GLSS Part II arXiv:2507.06823 already computes this average must be checked — flagged). The cheapest constraint provable with current technology is edge regularity of the unconditional BGSTB complex-zero form factor AT α = 1 (no atom at the band edge): it is exactly the free row j = N that the 256-law exploits (edge atom of mass 0.826, D(1) = 0.824), it is listed in the repo as an extension hook, and it requires only mollified/second-moment information at Dirichlet-polynomial length exactly T — not length T^{1+ε}.

### Novelty
Relative to the Zeta23 paper: all its theorems are dyadic-window (T,2T] statements; localization in window position/length is an axis the 54-hour campaign never varied (it varied bandwidth, moments, weights, and windows-as-tapers only). Relative to short-interval literature: Karatsuba (1984, H ≥ T^{27/82}) and successors give ≫ H log T critical-line zeros in short intervals but no explicit proportion near 2/3, no simplicity, and — decisively — no upper bound on off-line counts; to my knowledge NO short-interval simple-zero proportion exists at any exponent (flagged for a literature check), and no off-line local-density cap of any kind exists that is uniform in depth. Relative to zero-density (Guth–Maynard arXiv:2405.20552 and the ANTEDB successors): those bounds are vacuous for depth δ ≪ 1/log T; (b) is the first constraint of positive strength in that regime. Relative to GLSS arXiv:2503.15449/2507.06823: their 100%-simple conclusions assume full-support PCC; ours are unconditional and local. Relative to Farmer arXiv:2211.11671: converts "no reasons to doubt" from a rebuttal-essay into certified structural theorems a counterexample must satisfy, and makes his no-statistic claim a formal no-go within a precise certificate class while exhibiting the exact loophole (prime sums of length e^{c/δ}). Relative to Rodgers–Tao: adds the explicit depth→Λ quantitative bridge as a stated target rather than folklore. The refutation-program framing itself — wanted poster (structure) + bridge (Λ) + no-go (statistics) as one certified search-space-narrowing instrument — has no antecedent; the literature has "no serious counterexample-search program exists" (literature.md §9).

### Obstruction evasion

- **DH/Epstein filter** — Out of scope, and honestly so: the program claims structure/refutation constraints, never full RH. Additionally the localized certificate is not even available for DH/Epstein: their log-derivative coefficients are uncontrolled (DH has zeros with β > 1, so −F′/F coefficients grow polynomially; Epstein class# > 1 similarly), so the prime-side mean-value step (Prop 5.6) fails for them — the certificate consumes an input those objects violate, which is why they may (and do) have off-line zeros our theorem would forbid for ζ.
- **Alternative Hypothesis (bandwidth-1 indistinguishability)** — Confronted head-on rather than evaded: the program's own assessment is that AH survives the 2/3 theorem (the 256-law is an AH-flavored certified witness). The localized first theorem uses only bandwidth-one data and therefore cannot and does not claim to kill AH. Milestone 4 designs the cheapest AH-killers: band-edge regularity of F at α=1 (within length-T technology) and the Wiener average of F against the CMR interval (RH-conditional).
- **Bandwidth-one certificate ceiling 0.6818287** — No claimed constant exceeds it: the localized constants 2−1/θ−θ/3 ≤ 2−1/c*_1 = 0.6725 < 0.68183. Localization in window position is orthogonal to form-factor bandwidth, so the ceiling does not obstruct it. The only ceiling-attacking milestone (M4) goes through an added edge constraint at α=1, which the formalized ceiling explicitly does NOT cover (the law's row j=N is free; constraining it is a listed extension hook in the repo).
- **lemmaR_tight (two-moment certificate exhausted)** — The linear algebra is reused verbatim, not improved; tightness caps the constants H(θ), not the ability to run the certificate in new windows. No claim of separating on-line doubles from deep pairs is made anywhere.
- **Dimension cap d = λN** — Respected per window: d ≈ λN(I) with λ < θ, and the theorem is only claimed for θ > 3−√6 > 1/2 where the certificate is non-vacuous; the vacuity below θ = 0.5505 is stated as the theorem's own boundary.
- **κ(λ) = 1/λ+λ/3 ≥ 2/√3 (0.8453 cap at any bandwidth)** — No proportion above 0.8453 is claimed at any point of the program; the wanted-poster constants are all ≤ 0.6725.
- **Bombieri small-support Weil positivity (unconditional cones prove nothing)** — The mechanism is not a positivity proof on a cone: it is a two-sided counting certificate (inertia + rank–trace) whose output includes UPPER bounds on off-line counts — a direction small-support positivity cannot produce. To the campaign residue 'every route's first substantive step was Weil positivity in disguise': our first step is the already-proven rank–trace certificate; the NEW leverage is localization in window position/length plus the refutation reading (upper-bounding N_off), neither of which is a positivity statement.
- **Bombieri–Garrett ~94% pseudo-Laplacian cap** — No pseudo-Laplacian or automorphic-spectrum route is used; out of scope.
- **Parity problem** — No sieve-only step anywhere; the arithmetic inputs are EF + Chebyshev + Montgomery–Vaughan mean values, all already formalized.
- **Conrey–Li (de Branges positivity fails)** — No de Branges positivity condition is assumed; the de Branges δ-tilt appears only as unmined residue and is not on the critical path.
- **Rodgers–Tao Λ ≥ 0** — Absorbed as the organizing fact of the Λ-channel: since Λ ≥ 0, certifying Λ ≥ 10^{−N} is equivalent to certifying ¬RH via a complex zero of some H_t, t>0; the program therefore does NOT propose a standalone Λ-computation (backward flow is exponentially ill-posed — stated as a no-go) and instead supplies the depth→Λ bridge as a payoff formula for any future find.
- **Per-pair quadratic integrality pricing is FALSE (G1–G4)** — Only the mass-linear rank–trace inequality is used; no per-pair quadratic pricing appears.
- **Pontryagin negative-index route empty** — Not used; the positive-index dual (the paper's own mechanism) is used throughout.
- **Radziwill mollifier limitations / Conrey–Li / Levinson ceilings** — No mollifier-length-dependent step is used; mean values enter only through MV at length ≤ T^θ.

### First theorem
THEOREM (Localized certificate; the wanted poster). For every θ ∈ (3−√6, 1] and ε > 0 there exists T₀(θ,ε) such that for all T ≥ T₀ and every H with T^θ ≤ H ≤ T, writing I = (T, T+H], N(I) for nontrivial zeros with ordinate in I counted with multiplicity, N₀ˢ(I) for simple zeros on Re s = 1/2, N_d(I) for distinct zeros, and N_off(I) for zeros with Re ρ ≠ 1/2 counted with multiplicity:
(a) N₀ˢ(I) ≥ (2 − 1/θ − θ/3 − ε)·N(I);
(b) N_off(I) ≤ (1/θ + θ/3 − 1 + ε)·N(I);
(c) N_d(I) ≥ ((3 − 1/θ − θ/3)/2 − ε)·N(I);
uniformly over the window position T, and — the point — uniformly over the depths |β−1/2| ∈ (0,1/2) of whatever off-line zeros exist. Refinement: with the Montgomery–Taylor window, 1/θ + θ/3 improves to 1/c*_θ, c*_θ = √2·tan(θ/√2)/(1+(θ/√2)tan(θ/√2)).
COROLLARY (anti-clustering, depth-uniform): there is no height γ₀ and window of length ≥ γ₀^{0.5506} in which more than a fraction (1/θ+θ/3−1+ε) of zeros are off the critical line; in particular a hypothetical counterexample's off-line companions have local density ≤ 1/3+ε at scale γ₀ and its γ₀^θ-neighborhoods carry a certified majority of simple on-line zeros.
STATUS OF INPUTS: every input is proven and formalized — EF for C_c² test functions (WeilEF/), grid-translation-invariant Gabor–Poisson (Poisson.lean), block structure and inertia for arbitrary windows (ZeroBlockData, ZeroSide.lean), rank–trace (RankTrace.lean), MV with the window-independent off-diagonal bound (PPOffDiag.lean), short-interval Riemann–von Mangoldt from the local count N(t+1)−N(t) ≤ A₀log(t+3) already in PaperInputs. The ONLY new work is re-running the prime-side moment evaluation (Props 5.5–5.7) with window length H as a parameter: main terms localize because μ is flat across I and the diagonal prime term scales linearly in H; the binding check is O₁ ≪ L²X = o(HL³) ⟺ λ < θ, and the tail/boundary bookkeeping with D₀ = T^{θ/4+ε} (allowed by Prop 4.2's stated D₀-range), N(I′∖I) ≪ D₀·l = o(Hl). ### Failure modes

- Hidden window-position dependence in the prime-side cross terms: an integration-by-parts boundary term in 𝓜[μ,P_X] or the ends E1/E2 proportional to T rather than H would shrink the admissible θ-range; mitigation is a smooth-in-position window weight, but if that interacts badly with the exact Poisson identity the constants degrade (route survives with worse θ₀).
- Literature preemption partially undiscovered: a short-interval simplicity or off-line-cap result may exist (Karatsuba-school, Ivić, or recent BGSTB follow-ups such as arXiv:2603.28104 'zeros in a narrow vertical box'); must be checked before claiming (a)-novelty — though the depth-uniform off-line cap (b) has no plausible antecedent since inertia-based counting is new to the 2026 paper.
- Scope honesty: the wanted poster constrains OFF-LINE failure scenarios only; a 'carrier-wave' near-failure that keeps all zeros on the line with pathological spacings is untouched by (b), and AH survives — the program says so explicitly, but a reader wanting counterexample-EXCLUSION will find only density constraints, never exclusion (the method is insensitive to o(N) off-line zeros, per the paper's own §1.5).
- M4 may be hard: edge regularity of F at α=1 could secretly require length-T^{1+ε} information (the atom at the edge may be exactly as inaccessible as F beyond the band); the LP-re-run then only prices an unprovable theorem.
- M3 may hit a structural wall: averaging over window positions may not beat 3−√6 because the MV off-diagonal bound is already position-independent — the bet fails cleanly but consumes effort.
- The heat-flow bridge exponent in M6 (Λ ≳ δ²/polylog γ) is recalled dynamics folklore, not verified literature — the exact statement must be derived or found (Polymath15 machinery); if the true bridge is weaker (e.g. exponential loss in height), the Λ-channel payoff shrinks to a qualitative remark.
- Numerics risk: at T = 5·10^4 the certificate is strictly negative (Remark 5.9 — convergence 1−O(1/log T)), so Experiment 1 measures trends and depth-uniformity, not asymptotic constants; over-reading it would be wishful — the deliverable is the frontier's SHAPE, with the honest caveat that finite-T exclusion thresholds are far from κ(θ)−1.
