## Proposal (Phase 3, immutable)

### Thesis
The de Branges kernel family E_a(z) = xi(a - iz) is not prime-computable at any fixed tilt a > 1/2, but its JETS in a at the critical tilt a = 1/2 are - the first jet is exactly a mixed xi.xi' explicit-formula form whose prime side is already formalized in Zeta23/XiPrime - and compressing the jet family yields a linked two-matrix certificate whose new invariant, the mixed moment tr(G G') plus the exact derived-zero constraint P' >= sum (m_j - 1) v_j v_j^dagger, is multiplicity-sensitive: precisely the invariant lemmaR_tight proves the current method lacks.

### Mechanism
OBJECTS. For a = 1/2 + delta let E_a(z) = xi(a - iz), E_a^#(z) = xi(1-a-iz), and K_a(w,z) = [E_a(z) conj(E_a(w)) - E_a^#(z) conj(E_a^#(w))]/(2 pi i (conj(w) - z)) the de Branges kernel. Krein-Langer: K_a has exactly kappa_a negative squares, kappa_a = N(beta > a) WITH multiplicity - the invariant the Weil form provably lacks (transcript Part I: an m-fold off-line zero gives ONE negative square of W; lemmaR_tight: on-line double = off-line pair). The campaign's blocker (tx_048): compressions of K_a at fixed a > 1/2 need xi on the lines Re s = a, 1-a (Riemann-Siegel), not primes. THE TWO-PARAMETER FAMILY AND THE KEY IDENTITY. Do not fix a: expand K_a in the tilt at the critical point. At a = 1/2 the numerator vanishes identically on the diagonal (functional equation), and the FIRST JET is: d/da K_a |_{a=1/2}(w,z) = [xi'(1/2 - iz) conj(xi(1/2 - iw)) + xi(1/2 - iz) conj(xi'(1/2 - iw))]/(pi i (conj(w) - z)) - the MIXED xi.xi' kernel. The transcript's recorded-but-undeveloped delta-tilt identity 'd/da log|xi(a+it)/xi(1-a+it)| at a = 1/2 equals 2 pi nu_X' is precisely the diagonal w = z of this jet divided by |xi|^2: the jet, unlike K_a itself, is EXPLICIT-FORMULA DATA. Higher jets bring xi'', xi''', ...: the j-th tilt-jet of the de Branges kernel is a mixed form in (xi, xi', ..., xi^(j)), and each layer has a Weil-type explicit formula - the j=1 layer is ALREADY FORMALIZED (Zeta23/XiPrime/ExplicitFormula, prime term J, Gram bridge, entry errors). Since kappa_a jumps by exactly m_rho as a crosses beta_rho, the multiplicity distribution of zeros is encoded in the jets of the kernel family at a = 1/2; the jets are prime-computable even though K_a at fixed a is not. This is the arithmetic de Branges principle: PRIME-COMPUTE THE JETS, NOT THE KERNEL. COMPRESSED AVATAR. On the paper's Gabor family f_k (window phi, bandwidth L = lambda log(T/2pi), d = floor(LT/2pi)), form the two Gram matrices G_{kl} = W_xi(f_k, f_l) (the paper's matrix) and G'_{kl} = W_{xi'}(f_k, f_l) (the XiPrime matrix). The compressed avatar of the tilt-jet is the pencil G + tG' together with the MIXED MOMENT tr(G_hat G_hat') - a third prime-computable moment beyond the exhausted pair (tr, Frobenius). Zero side (via the exact Poisson identity, applied to both kernels): tr(G_hat G_hat') = sum_{rho, sigma} m_rho m'_sigma |L Phi(gamma_rho - conj(gamma_sigma))|^2-normalized + tail, a mixed Fejer sum over (xi-zero, xi'-zero) pairs. WHY MULTIPLICITY BECOMES VISIBLE - THE TWO LINK CONSTRAINTS (exact algebra, not asymptotics): (L1) Derived zeros: if xi has a zero of multiplicity m >= 2 at rho (on OR off the line), xi' has a zero of multiplicity exactly m-1 at the SAME point, so the atom in G' sits on the SAME evaluation vector v_rho as the atom in G: P' >= sum_{multiples} (m_j - 1) v_j v_j^dagger in the PSD order, and each multiple FORCES a coincident-point contribution ~ m(m-1) |L Phi(0)|^2 to the mixed Fejer sum. (L2) Rolle: Xi(t) = xi(1/2 + it) is real, so between consecutive distinct on-line xi-zeros there is an on-line xi'-zero; combined with N_{xi'}(T,2T) = N_xi(T,2T) + O(log T) (formalized zero-count comparison), this pins the on-line xi'-population against the on-line xi-population. An off-line pair of SIMPLE zeros forces neither. So the degenerate pair of lemmaR_tight (on-line double vs off-line pair, both charging 4 in the old certificate) splits: the double is FORCED to buy mixed-moment mass, the pair is not. CERTIFICATE. Apply the rank-trace machinery (rank_trace_mult_k, already stated for arbitrary real weights) to H_t = G_hat + t G_hat': on-line xi-points of multiplicity m carry weight >= m + t(m-1) (by L1, plus nonneg other xi'-mass), distinct on-line points cost rank 1, pairs of either object spend the n_+ budget; the three data values tr H_t = (1+t)N(1+o(1)), ||H_t||_F^2 = ||G_hat||_F^2 + 2t tr(G_hat G_hat') + t^2 ||G_hat'||_F^2 (all unconditionally computable - the cross term by the First Theorem, the outer two by the paper and by XiPrime), and the LP over integer multiplicities with charge k_c(m + t(m-1)) yield: an upper bound on the total multiplicity excess sum_rho (m_rho - 1) = N - N_d (both on- and off-line multiples are forced by L1 into the mixed moment), hence N_d >= (5/6 + eta)N; and an upper bound on the on-line multiple mass s_{2+} whenever the computed c_x is below the forced-coincidence threshold - the first multiplicity-specific (rather than distinctness-aggregate) unconditional bounds by any compression method. WHY IT SHOULD WORK: every analytic ingredient is a re-run of proven technology (Poisson twice, MV for the mixed off-diagonal - MV is bilinear in two arbitrary sequences, tail for both kernels); the only new analysis is one mixed prime-diagonal evaluation; the only new algebra is a linked two-matrix rank-trace lemma that the repo's real-weight k_c-form was explicitly engineered to accommodate; and the discrimination signal (forced coincident atoms) is a rank-one POSITIVE contribution, immune to the phase-cancellation disease that killed per-pair pricing. LONG HORIZON: iterate jets (xi^(j) towers, j-fold links m -> m-j), reconstructing the depth-and-multiplicity distribution of zeros from primes - an arithmetic canonical-system (de Branges chain) whose Hamiltonian jets at the critical tilt are Lambda(n)-sums; and a phase-side branch: control ||sin(pi(N(t)-N(s)))/(pi(t-s)) - warped-sinc(N_X)||_HS by the unconditional L2 phase approximation int |S - S_X|^2, transferring inertia between the true (multiplicity-visible) de Branges kernel and R4's prime-computable warped sinc - rehabilitating, with error control, the operator the campaign discarded because it 'is not a compression of W': it is a compression of K_{1/2}, which is the right object.

### Obstruction evasion

- **DH/Epstein filter** — Out of scope for full RH, and says so: the horizon goals are proportions, multiplicity-specific bounds (N_d, s_{2+}-mass), and refutation channels, never RH itself. Consistency check: for Davenport-Heilbronn the method self-destructs honestly - the prime-side mean-value step (Prop 5.6 analog) fails because DH coefficients grow too fast, so both G and G' are uncomputable and the joint certificate is empty; for Epstein (class number >1) the same. Nothing in the mixed-moment route pretends to consume Euler-product-at-every-prime beyond L2 means, so no RH-completeness is claimed or implied.
- **Alternative Hypothesis (bandwidth-1 pair data cannot distinguish GUE from half-integer worlds)** — The certificate no longer reads only xi pair-correlation data: tr(G G') is a CROSS correlation between xi-zeros and xi'-zeros, a data type outside the AH ambiguity class as formulated (AH concerns the single-object form factor). We do not claim to refute AH; but note the joint data is exactly the kind AH analysis has never constrained - an AH-consistent world must now also supply a derivative-zero configuration satisfying Rolle interlacing and derived-zero coincidences, a rigidity absent from pure pair-correlation modeling. Milestone 4 (joint ceiling LP) will determine quantitatively how much the link constraints shrink the adversary polytope.
- **Bandwidth-one certificate ceiling 0.6818287 (PairCeiling, formalized)** — Evaded by leaving the ceiling's data class, exactly along the escape route the formalization itself declares out of scope ('certificates reading only bandwidth-one [xi] form-factor data, configuration-by-configuration'). tr(G G') is not a functional of the xi form factor: the 256-periodic law has no xi'-layer, and no bandwidth-one xi-configuration data determines the cross moment. To re-trap this certificate one must build a JOINT (xi, xi')-law satisfying the analytic links (derived zeros at every multiple point, Rolle interlacing of the real function Xi, matched counting functions) - Milestone 4 constructs that LP deliberately, so either the ceiling moves up (room confirmed) or we obtain a new formalized no-go; both are payoffs.
- **lemmaR_tight (two-moment rank-trace certificate exhausted; on-line double = off-line pair)** — This is the obstruction the design targets head-on rather than evades. lemmaR_tight says the invariant set (tr, Frobenius, block structure, n_+) of ONE matrix cannot separate an on-line double from a tight off-line pair. The mixed moment tr(G G') is a new invariant outside that set, and the derived-zero link makes it multiplicity-sensitive: a double FORCES a coincident xi'-atom on the same evaluation vector (exact algebra, not asymptotics), a pair of simple zeros forces nothing. The joint tightness question (is the enlarged invariant set exhausted?) becomes a new, formalizable lemma - Milestone 2/4.
- **Dimension cap d = lambda*N (nothing below lambda=1/2, 100% only at lambda=1)** — Fully respected: the joint certificate is still a compression certificate on the d-dimensional Gabor space, so it certifies at most lambda_1*N points; we operate at lambda near 1 and target proportions strictly below 1 (N_d beyond 5/6, s_1 beyond 0.6818). No claim of 100% or of anything at lambda <= 1/2.
- **kappa(lambda) = 1/lambda + lambda/3 >= 2/sqrt(3): trace+Frobenius certificates cap at 0.8453 at any bandwidth** — That cap is proved for certificates consuming exactly two moments of a single matrix. The joint certificate consumes five computable quantities (tr G, ||G||_F^2, tr G', ||G'||_F^2, tr GG') plus two exact link constraints; the 0.8453 bound's derivation (Cauchy-Schwarz on one moment pair) does not apply to the enlarged system. The honest counterpart: Milestone 4 computes the NEW cap of the enlarged system by the same LP-law technology, before we over-invest.
- **Bombieri small-support unconditional Weil positivity (small cones prove nothing)** — Not used. No step asserts positivity of the Weil form on any cone. The forms G, G' are indefinite and treated by inertia; the first substantive step is an unconditional IDENTITY (the mixed moment evaluation), not a positivity statement. Where positivity appears (PSD atoms on-line, P' minorant) it is per-zero structure valid unconditionally.
- **Bombieri-Garrett ~94% pseudo-Laplacian spectral cap** — Out of scope: no self-adjoint operator is constructed and no zeros are claimed to be eigenvalues of a pseudo-Laplacian. The de Branges kernel enters only as bookkeeping (Krein-Langer negative-square counts and their jets), never as a spectral realization of zeros; the certificates are finite-dimensional inertia statements.
- **Parity problem (sieve-only routes)** — Not a sieve argument: inputs are explicit-formula mean values (Montgomery-Vaughan bilinear bounds, unconditional moments), which already sit outside the parity-limited axiom class; parity is orthogonal to this route's bottleneck (interference control in signed cross terms).
- **Conrey-Li counterexamples to de Branges positivity conditions** — Conrey-Li kill sufficient-for-RH POSITIVITY HYPOTHESES on specific de Branges spaces built from xi (and its chi_4 analogue xi(., chi_4) — their §3.2; the F(W)-side space is analytic in the UPPER HALF-PLANE, not entire — w-05b p. 119, source-verified in results/w-05b-vision-pass.md). This program assumes no such hypothesis, conditional or otherwise. Inequivalence check on record (FETCH-LIST-RESPONSE.md §4): at a = 1, TRP(1) holds while CL's (3.1) fails for that same E_1 — the falsified hypotheses are strictly stronger than RH. It uses only the unconditional Krein-Langer counting identity - E_a's kernel has exactly kappa_a = N(beta>a) negative squares WITH multiplicity, true whether or not RH holds - and then extracts prime-computable JETS of that kernel at a=1/2 (the mixed xi.xi' forms). Direction of inference is opposite: not 'positivity => RH' but 'computable compressions => lower bounds on good zeros / upper bounds on multiples'. Conrey-Li constrains nothing here.
- **Rodgers-Tao Lambda >= 0 (no sub-critical margin)** — No heat-flow/deformation-toward-criticality step is used; the tilt parameter a moves in the SPATIAL (real-part) direction at scale 1/log T, not in de Bruijn-Newman time, and only its derivative at a=1/2 is consumed. Nothing requires a margin below criticality.
- **Campaign residue: 'every route's first substantive step was Weil positivity in disguise'; per-pair quadratic pricing FALSE (G1-G4); Pontryagin negative-index route empty** — First substantive step here is the unconditional mixed-moment IDENTITY tr(GG') = mixed Fejer sum = prime-side main term - an evaluation, not a positivity. The new leverage over the disguised-Weil-positivity pattern is the exact algebraic LINK between two Gram matrices (derived zeros share evaluation vectors; Rolle interlacing of the real function Xi), which no single-form statement contains. Per-pair quadratic pricing is not revived: all pricing remains mass-linear via rank_trace_mult_k (already stated for arbitrary real weights m + t(m-1)); the cross moment enters as a linear budget constraint, not a per-pair charge. The negative-index counting route is not used in the upper-bound direction anywhere; inertia is used only in the certified (lower-bound) direction, as in the paper. Also honest about the known mimicry limit: an off-line pair at depth y -> 0+ with adjacent xi'-mass fakes a double continuously, so improvements target multiplicity-specific and N_d-type counts where the adversary must fit VALUES of the new data, not per-configuration separation at depth 0.

### First theorem
THEOREM (unconditional mixed Montgomery theorem for xi and xi'). Fix lambda in (0,1), a C^3 window phi with supp phi = [-L/2, L/2], L = lambda*log(T/2pi), and the critical-density Gabor family f_k(u) = phi(u)e^{-i tau_k u}, tau_k = T + 2pi k/L, 0 <= k < d = floor(LT/2pi). Let G_{kl} = W_xi(f_k, f_l) and G'_{kl} = W_{xi'}(f_k, f_l) be the Gram matrices of the Weil forms of xi and of xi' (both objects and their explicit formulas are formalized: Zeta23/WeilEF and Zeta23/XiPrime/ExplicitFormula), normalized as in the paper (G_hat = G/(aL^2), same for G_hat'). Then, as T -> infinity: (i) [zero-side identity] tr(G_hat G_hat') = sum over pairs (rho a zero of xi, sigma a zero of xi', both in the window) of m_rho * m'_sigma * |L*Phi(gamma_rho - bar gamma_sigma)|^2/(aL^2)^2 + O(theta-tail), Phi = (phi^2)^hat, an exact consequence of the aliasing-free Poisson sampling identity applied to both kernels, with the tail controlled by Prop 4.2 run for each kernel (both zero sets lie in the open strip - formalized for xi' in XiPrime); and (ii) [prime-side evaluation] tr(G_hat G_hat') = c_x(lambda; v) * N(T,2T) * (1 + O(E_T)) unconditionally, where c_x(lambda; v) = 1/lambda_1 + lambda_1 * (1/3 + j(lambda; v)), the 1/3-analogue coming from the mixed prime diagonal (T/pi) * sum_{n <= X} Lambda(n) * w_J(n) * g(log n)/n with w_J(n) the explicit coefficient of the formalized xi'-prime term (XiPrime/ExplicitFormula/PrimeTermJ.lean; w_J(n) = Lambda-weight times an explicit 1 + O(log n / log T) correction), g = phi^2 * phi^2 (convolution), and j(lambda; v) the resulting explicit window integral; equivalently: the mixed complex-zero form factor F_{xi,xi'}(alpha) of BGSTB type satisfies int_{-lambda}^{lambda} (lambda - |alpha|) F_{xi,xi'}(alpha) d alpha = (explicit value), unconditionally, for every lambda < 1 (endpoint lambda = 1 admissible iff all secondary terms stay o(main), expected as in Remark 6.1; fallback lambda < 1 fixed). STATUS OF INPUTS: every ingredient exists and is proven - the Weil EF for xi (formalized), the xi' EF with Gram bridge and entry errors (formalized), the exact Poisson identity (formalized), Montgomery-Vaughan (formalized, bilinear in two arbitrary coefficient sequences, so it applies verbatim to the mixed off-diagonal sum_{n<>m} Lambda(n) w_J(m)(...)/(log n - log m)), and the tail estimate for both kernels. ATTACK SKETCH: expand tr(GG') = sum_{k,l} W(f_k,f_l) W'(f_l,f_k); insert both explicit-formula densities nu_X = mu + Pi_X + P_X and nu'_X = mu' + P'_X; the Poisson identity collapses the (k,l)-sums to L^2 |Phi(tau - tau')|^2 exactly (no aliasing since supp phi + supp phi is within [-L, L]); evaluate the six bilinear pieces exactly as Props 5.5-5.7 with the pair (mu, P_X) x (mu', P'_X): archimedean x archimedean gives the 1/lambda_1 term, prime x prime diagonal gives the mixed L^3-term, prime x prime off-diagonal is absorbed by MV, cross terms mu x P' by one integration by parts against the cosine oscillation. The only genuinely new computation is the mixed prime diagonal - one explicit Euler-Maclaurin evaluation. A referee can check every step against the corresponding single-object step in the paper's Section 5.

### Failure modes

- Signal-to-background failure: the forced coincident-atom contribution of one on-line multiple to tr(G_hat G_hat') is O(1) in N-units, but so is the generic (sine-kernel-level) xi.xi' clustering background; if the numerically measured ratio R is near 1, the joint LP gains ~0 and the program degrades to Milestone 1 (still a new unconditional theorem) plus Milestone 4 (a no-go).
