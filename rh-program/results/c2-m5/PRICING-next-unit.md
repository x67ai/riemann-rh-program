A cone element is w with w ≥ 0, ŵ ≥ 0, supp w ⊂ [−L, L], where ŵ(ξ) := ∫ w(u) e^{−iuξ} du, and the test is g := w / cosh(u/2). ## §1 Candidate R1 — mandatory repair 1: the Sector-I confinement theorem as a THEOREM of the field

### §1.1 The mechanism, computed — and the finding that fixes the theorem's honest shape

**(a) What a cone certificate at (t, y) is.** A cone element w excludes a zero orbit at (t, y) iff the orbit's share alone exceeds the budget: 4(ŵ ∗ μ_y)(t) > B(w) (C2 line 16: "the region {V > 1} is unconditionally zero-free", V(t, y; L) = sup{orbit share : w ∈ Σ_L, B(w) ≤ 1}). Since ŵ ≤ ŵ(0) and μ_y has mass 1, the orbit share is at most 4ŵ(0) = twice the pole term — the continuous form of Fejér's a₁ ≤ 2a₀ for nonnegative cosine polynomials, the constraint that has capped the de la Vallée Poussin method since 1899 (zoo III.9, BARRIER-ZOO.md line 220: "The 3-4-1 inequality is Fejér-square positivity — the identical 1899 generator behind classical zero-free regions, edge-capped for 125 years"). The depth enters only through the peak μ_y(0) = 1/sin(πδ) of a kernel of width ≍ δ (PRICING.md §1.3: the cone "sees depth only through 1/cos(πy)"), and the height enters only through the archimedean cost a(τ) ≈ (1/2π)log(t/2π) =: ℓ/2π per unit of ŵ-mass placed near ±t. For the height-t family w = p(u)(a₀ + a₁cos tu + a₂cos 2tu) with p spread over |u| ≲ 1/δ this gives a certificate iff ∫p/p(0) ≳ [(a₁ + a₂)ℓ − 2π a₀|a(0)|]/(2(a₁ − a₀)), and ∫p/p(0) ≤ 2/δ because cosh(yu)/cosh(u/2) ≤ 2e^{−δ|u|} kills p beyond |u| ≈ 1/δ — so certificates exist only for δ ≲ 4(a₁ − a₀)/((a₁ + a₂)ℓ): 0.8/ℓ for the 3-4-1 polynomial, at most 2/ℓ at the Fejér limit a₁ → 2a₀ (derivation in the pricing agent's working; the constants are the ones printed in §0). That is the killer's layer {1 − σ ≤ C/log t} (C2 line 70) with its mechanism made explicit, and it shows the design parameter L is **pinned at ≍ 1/δ by the strip-positivity factor cosh(yu)/cosh(u/2)** — bandwidth beyond 1/δ buys nothing (KICKSTART 10(h): "record in the zoo where each proof class's parameter is pinned and by what cone/positivity condition").

**(b) Two proof routes for general w, and why only one is cheap.** For arbitrary w ∈ Σ_L the certificate inequality must be refuted for all w, not for a family. Route (α), configuration-free: bound B(w) from below by the archimedean functional alone. Splitting ŵ into its part on |τ| < τ₀ = 6.31 (where a < 0) and the rest gives B(w) ≥ (2 − I₋)ŵ(0) + m·ℓ′/π with I₋ := ∫_{a<0}|a| and m the ŵ-mass within t/4 of t, and the orbit share is ≤ 4m/sin(πδ) + 4ŵ(0)·tail. **Computed: I₋ = 2.2415 > 2** (`budget_floor_run.log` line "I_minus (both sides) = 2.241523 -> 2 - I_minus = -0.241523"). So the trivial floor is negative by 0.24ŵ(0), and route (α) needs a **budget-floor lemma** κ := inf_{w∈Σ_∞} B(w)/ŵ(0) > 0 with κ explicit; the theorem's constant then reads C ≈ 4(1 + 0.24/κ) (pricing agent's assembly). The Fejér family gives the upper bound κ ≤ 0.136 (minimum at T = 11, log line "min over the Fejer family: T = 11 B/what(0) = 0.13597"), and the cone's LP duality shows what a proof of κ > 0 must contain: a dual certificate is a pair (ψ ≥ 0 on the zero line, φ ≥ 0 on the prime line) with (2 − κ)δ₀ + a = ψ + φ̂/2π, and because a(0) < 0 while φ̂(0) = ∫dφ ≥ 0 for every finite positive φ, **no finite-mass prime measure and no finite-variation perturbation of the mean zero density can certify any κ > 0** — the dual object must be a genuine infinite zero configuration with positive prime measure and pole weight below 2, which is precisely the truncated-Hamburger existence set PRICING.md §1.1(v) named as the honest object of M1 `[novelty: single-check]`. Route (α) with explicit constants is therefore an M1/M3-grade unit (≥ 2 slots, uncertain), and with a non-explicit κ (compactness plus a Chebyshev bound, standard) a 1-slot lemma with a non-explicit height threshold. **Route (β), ζ-anchored:** use B(w) ≥ Z(w) = 2∫ŵΨ and compare kernels pointwise: since the orbit share is 2∫ŵ(s)[μ_y(t − s) + μ_y(t + s)]ds, the inequality **μ_y(t − s) + μ_y(t + s) ≤ Ψ(s) for all s** implies orbit share ≤ Z(w) ≤ B(w) for every cone element at every bandwidth — no floor, no smallness regime, one line. Near s = t the condition is 1/sin(πδ) ≤ min Ψ, i.e. the hypothetical orbit's Poisson peak must not exceed ζ's own smoothed on-line density; away from t it is automatic (the tails of μ_y are ≤ 4πδe^{−π|t−s|} against Ψ(s) ≥ 1/cosh(π·dist(s, zeros))).
