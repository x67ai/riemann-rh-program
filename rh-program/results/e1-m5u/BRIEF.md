## The question (FORMULATION §5.1, verbatim, the object of this slot)
Fix ζ's datum Λ, a bandwidth L, a height T with α := L/log(T/2π) ∈ [½, 1), a window [T, T + W]; ν_ζ ∈ 𝒦_L(Λ) for every L (§1.1(b)). **M5-U (uniqueness on the window):** is every ν ∈ 𝒦_L(Λ) equal to ν_ζ on [T, T + W] (marks included)? **M5-D:** is d_{T,W}(ν) > 5/6 for every ν ∈ 𝒦_L(Λ)? The YES shape: "for every configuration ν of RvM density with ν ≠ ν_ζ on [T, T + W], the even distribution E_ν − E_{ν_ζ} is nonzero on (−L, L)" — an uncertainty principle for positive integer-atomic measures of logarithmic density: the difference of two such measures with a spectral gap (−L, L) vanishes on every window whose height is below 2πe^{2L}. The NO shape: an explicit ν ∈ 𝒦_L(Λ), ν ≠ ν_ζ on a window at α ∈ [½, 1), with d_{T,W}(ν) ≤ 5/6 — an exact ghost. Inputs the YES would need, none on the record (§5.1): (i) a gap theorem for INFINITE, INTEGER-weighted measures with the positivity ν ≥ 0 used; (ii) a localization in height.
