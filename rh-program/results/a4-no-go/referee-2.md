## 3. Findings — MAJOR

### Major 1 — the pair-channel closure is claimed for "the gate's entire admissible class," but the proved coverage has a gap the paper itself concedes elsewhere

Headline 1.4(8): *"is closed for the gate's entire admissible class: proved for all single-pair configurations to depth w <= 2.8 ... and all multi-pair configurations to w <= 0.82 unconditionally (w <= 1 modulo one certified crowding constant ...)"*; Section 4.3 is titled *"The pair-interference channel: closed for the admissible class"* and its Consequence paragraph repeats *"extends from the atom-only class to the gate's entire admissible class."*

The paper's own Section 2.4 defines the admissible class with pair depths *"in the family (0, 1] plus deep probes {1.5, 2}"* (matching SPEC 3.3 seed 5 / SPEC 6, where the deep probes are dictionary columns). A column with **two or more pairs at mixed depths, at least one beyond w = 1** (e.g. one probe at w = 1.5 plus a pair at w = 0.5, plus atoms) is admissible but is covered by none of Theorems 4.6 (single pair), 4.7 (all depths <= 1), or 4.8 (pair-only, one common depth) — only by the 8/9 backstop (Theorem 4.9) and heuristic pricing. The paper concedes exactly this in 8.3: *"(O2) Single-pair depths in (0.45, 1.0) at unrestricted mass, and multi-pair beyond the family, are covered by numerics plus the 8/9 backstop only."* The Consequence paragraph's dismissal — *"deep multi-pair beyond the family ... none of it LP-relevant"* — contradicts Section 2.4, which places the deep probes inside the class.

**Fix:** in 1.4(8), 4.3's title line, and the Consequence paragraph, replace "the gate's entire admissible class" with the honest enumeration already present: closed for all single-pair columns at every gate depth, all multi-pair columns within the R5 depth family (the deepest ~20% modulo one certified crowding constant), and equal-depth pair-only columns at every depth; mixed-depth multi-pair columns involving the deep probes rest on the unconditional 8/9 backstop plus converged pricing (O2).

### Major 2 — headline 1 attributes eps in {0.002, ..., 0.10} to the 940 pre-registered records

Headline 1.4(1): *"940 LP records spanning mark alphabets W in {2,...,8}, ladder constants C_led in {10, 100, 1000}, tolerances eps in {0.002,...,0.10}, all fuzz modes ..."*

Verified against `runs/scans_N64.json` directly: the 900 N = 64 grid records span eps in **{0.02, 0.05, 0.10}** only (and the 40 N = 128 records carry no eps axis of their own). The eps = 0.002/0.005/0.01 points are the **separate, labeled, beyond-SPEC tight-eps run** (`runs/tighteps_N64.json`, 8 records; RUN-REPORT deviation 6 labels them). Section 4.4's "The grid" paragraph states the axes correctly; headline 1 contradicts it and inflates the pre-registered scope. (The statements elsewhere that "delta_0 = 0 across eps in 0.002–0.10" — 8.3, 9.1 — are fine as statements about the union of runs; the raw tight-eps delta_0 values are 1.0–2.8e-13.)

**Fix:** in headline 1, "tolerances eps in {0.02, 0.05, 0.10} (plus labeled tight-eps runs down to 0.002)".
