## §1 Re-derivations at the line

1. **The truncated zeta F_X (l. 13, BRIEF task 3) — ✓.** For σ > 1, ζ_P(s) = ∫_{1⁻}^∞ u^{−s} dN(u). Split at X and write N = T + E on (X, ∞) with T(u) = ρ(u − 1) + 1, dT = ρ du: ∫_X^∞ u^{−s}ρ du = ρX^{1−s}/(s − 1); ∫_X^∞ u^{−s}dE = −E(X)X^{−s} + s∫_X^∞ E(u)u^{−s−1}du (integration by parts; boundary term at ∞ vanishes when E = O(u^{σ−ε})). Hence ζ_P = F_X + R_X with F_X exactly as on l. 13 and R_X(s) = s∫_X^∞ E(u)u^{−s−1}du. (The BRIEF's form ρsX^{1−s}/(s − 1) − (ρX + E(X))X^{−s} is the same function: ρsX^{1−s}/(s − 1) − ρX^{1−s} = ρX^{1−s}/(s − 1).)
