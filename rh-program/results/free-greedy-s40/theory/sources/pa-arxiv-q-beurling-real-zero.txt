    <title>Zero free regions for Dirichlet series</title>
    <updated>2011-01-06T12:19:06Z</updated>
    <link href="https://arxiv.org/abs/1101.1199v1" rel="alternate" type="text/html"/>
    <link href="https://arxiv.org/pdf/1101.1199v1" rel="related" type="application/pdf" title="pdf"/>
    <summary>In this paper, we are interested in explicit zero-free discs for some Dirichlet series and we also study a general Beurling-Nyman criterion for $L$-functions. Our results generalize and improve previous results obtained by N. Nikolski and by A. de Roton. As a concrete application, we get, for example, a Beurling-Nyman type criterion for the Siegel zero problem.</summary>
