- **Lagarias 1999** (Forum Math. 11, 295–312; abstract via web search, not yet at the page): a g-integer set with the Delone property (consecutive gaps bounded above and away from zero) has unique factorization; those contained in ℤ have as g-primes all but finitely many rational primes plus finitely many composites. - The simplest continuous example, for the record: dN = δ₁ + ρ dx on [1, ∞) has ζ_c(s) = (s − 1 + ρ)/(s − 1), N(x) = 1 + ρ(x − 1) exactly, prime density (1 − x^{−ρ})/log x ≥ 0, and its only zero at s = 1 − ρ (to the right of ½ when ρ < ½). S8 is the integer-level discretization of this template.
