WHAT THE THEOREMS SAY, and what they do not (binding trust vocabulary, D-R3 / D-R8):
  * `<name>_check : checkW1Floor <name> <name>_floor = true := by decide +kernel` — the kernel
    evaluated the integer checks C1–C11 (Checker.lean) on the literal and found them true.  That is
    a statement about integers.  For the eight ζ transcripts the ζ conclusion is `cert_of_checkW1`
    (Soundness.lean) MODULO the two displayed hypotheses H-ENCL (the producers' enclosures are true)
    and H-AP (the rectangle argument principle for ζ) — neither is certified here.  The ledger
    sentence for each (D-R6) is "no zeros of ζ in R, kernel-checked modulo H-ENCL and H-AP
    (producers untrusted)" — never "RH verified in R".
  * For the two f_DH live-fire transcripts (`mpDH`, `arbDH`) there is NO theorem about f_DH at all:
    they are checker-level true-positive firing tests (D-R8); the kernel verdict says only that the
    checker's arithmetic accepts their rows.
  * `posMP_rej_check`, `posARB_rej_check : checkW1 … = false` — the positive controls (a box
    STRADDLING the critical line around ρ₁ ≈ ½ + 14.13i, deliberately violating C2) are rejected by
    the kernel, exactly as by both Python checkers (acceptance-report.md §2).
  * Nothing here is "fully machine-checked".  No `native_decide` anywhere.  `#print axioms` on every
    `_check` theorem: `[propext]` for the ten `checkW1Floor` instances, no axioms for the two
    `checkW1` rejections (results/d1-m1/recon_lean_instances.log).
