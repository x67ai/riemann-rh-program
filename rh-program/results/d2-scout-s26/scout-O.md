### P5 — Monoid and blueprint squares: Deitmar's monoid schemes, Lorscheid's blueprints

*Nearest killed class and exact difference:* nearest K1 (F₁-squares), different formalism (monoids with a base-change functor −⊗_{F₁}Z).
**(a) Four items.**
- *Deitmar — item 1 collapses:* base change is F_A ⊗_{F₁} Z = Z[A] (Deitmar p. 3 [Q26]); the only monoid A with Z[A] = Z is the trivial one **(computed from [Q26])**, so the only F₁-model of Spec Z is the absolute point Spec F₁, whose square over Spec F₁ is itself: the square is the diagonal. Finite-type monoid schemes are toric: "Let X be a monoid scheme such that X ⊗_{F₁} Z is a connected, separated and flat scheme of finite type. Then X ⊗_{F₁} Z is a toric variety over Z" (Lorscheid, "F₁ for everyone", Thm. 2.5, p. 14 [Q27], citing Deitmar 2008).
- *Lorscheid 2014 — item 1 EXHIBITED as a topological surface, nothing more:* the diagnosis at the page: "in the approaches towards F₁-geometry so far … the self-product Spec Z ×_{F₁} Spec Z is either isomorphic to Spec Z itself or infinite-dimensional over Z" (1204.3129 p. 2 [Q28]); the construction: "Proposition 4.1.
