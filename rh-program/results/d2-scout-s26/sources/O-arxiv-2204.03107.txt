                                                                              Abstract
                                                  We define the concept of a Bi-operad. We develop the homotopy theory
                                               of Bital-Sets and of 8-Bi-operad. We develop a geometry of generalized
                                               schemes based on the spectra of distributive monochromatic bi-operads.


                                          1    Introduction
                                             It was Dan Kan who developed the language of Categories, and of
                                          Simplicial Sets based on ﬁnite-linearly-ordered sets, to give a combinational
                                          approach to homotopy. Dan Quillen axiomatized this approach in his language
                                          of Model Categories. Operads were discovered by Boardman and Vogt in
                                          connection with homotopy invariant algebraic structures, and by May in con-
                                          nection with iterated loop spaces. Ieke Moerdijk (with collaborators I. Weiss, C.
                                          Berger, D.C. Cisinski, G. Heuts, V. Hinich, E. Hoﬀbeck) generalized categories
                                          and operads to a theory of “Coloured-Operads”, leading to a generalization
                                          of simplicial sets to “Dendroided Sets”, based on ﬁnite trees, and developed
                                          the homotopy theory of 8Operads generalizing 8Categories.
                                          While categories and simplicial sets have a dual opposite, there is no opposite of
                                          an operad. Some algebraic structures (such as Hopf-algebras and bi-algebras)
                                          are not controlled by an operad since they involve both operations and “co-
                                          operations”. The purpose of this paper is to deﬁne a notion of “Bi-Operad”,
                                          having both operations and co-operations, and of “Bital-Sets”, based on ﬁnite
                                          - “Bi-trees”, and sketch the homotopy theory of 8Bi-Operads generalizing the
                                          Dendroidal and the Simplicial stories.
                                          Having an opposite for Bi-operads and Bital-Sets we can speak of the self-dual
                                          Bi-operads and Bital-sets having an involution. Finally, returning to the clas-
                                          sical “monochromatic” case of having one colour or object, we can speak of the
                                          distributive Bi-operads.
                                          It was Alexander Grothendieck who develop the modern language of algebraic
                                          geometry, the schemes, based on the spectra of commutative-rings. But com-
                                          mutative rings are Not adequate for Arithmetical Geometry (The “integers”
                                          at a real or complex place of a number ﬁeld do not form a sub-ring; the integers Z
