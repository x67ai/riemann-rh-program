### IV.20 The finite-rank target kill (no surface over a field, and no target of finite Q-rank, can carry ζ's diagonal row) — NEW, Session 35 (β-shapes, `results/beta-shapes-s35/NOTE.md` §2.3; entered 2026-09-30, Session 36)

- **STATEMENT.** Let (X, Y, c) be a pair in the sense of the SPEC (`results/f1-spec-s29/SPEC.md`) over a base object B of A2's class, with A5 (deg(Γ_1 ∩ Γ_n) = Λ_B(n)), A7 (c(Γ_n) the graph of φ_n) and A9 (Σ_{n ≠ 1 : c(Γ_n) = D} Λ_B(n) = κ (Δ · D)_Y). **Theorem R:** if the Q-linear span G of the diagonal row {(Δ · c(Γ_n))_Y : n ≥ 2} is finite-dimensional, then B has finitely many residue characteristics; conversely dim_Q G ≤ |char(B)| always, so dim_Q G < ∞ exactly when char(B) is finite (A9's one fixed κ is used; A7 only names the fibers of c). Hence over Spec Z, and over every number ring, NO target whose intersection form is Z-valued, Q-valued, valued in a finite-rank subgroup of R, or defined on a finite-rank Num(Y) — in particular no surface over a field in Weil's form, of any genus, with or without the Hodge index theorem or a Lefschetz identity — satisfies A5 + A7 + A9. Companion: **Lemma F** — A9's finite fiber sums force every prime endomorphism φ_p to have infinite order in End_β(B), so every β with End_β(B) = {id} (terminal, subterminal, Durov's square, β = F₁) fails A9 by a divergent fiber (T1, T2).
