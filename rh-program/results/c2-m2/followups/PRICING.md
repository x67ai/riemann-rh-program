### §3(a) Required versus attainable accuracy of P_X(g_t) — and the correction this pricing makes to the record's "e^{L*}"

**The object.** By clause 8 (note lines 353–357) and `EF_lit_zetaZeroConfig`, for k = g = f ⋆ f̃ (C^∞, support [−L, L]):

— the prime sum is FINITE and the identity is exact (the explicit formula is formalized for ζ; `WeilEF/Main.lean` 270). ### §3(b) The two-sided form

**EITHER — the impossibility THEOREM, with its class named.** Two classes, two statements, both refutation-shaped:

* **(E) Estimate class: "uniform-in-height bounds on the short prime sum".** Statement: for the fixed test class g_{t,L} and X = e^{L}, sup over t in any window [T, T + H] of |P_X(g_t) − (its t-independent part)| tends, as H → ∞, to the ℓ¹ norm Σ_{n ≤ X}Λ(n)n^{−1/2}|w_n| of the coefficients (the trivial bound), because the phases {p^{−it}} over the primes p ≤ X are jointly equidistributed on the torus (Kronecker's theorem on the Q-linear independence of {log p} — `[recalled, unverified at the page; a textbook theorem, and the M6 note must either cite it at the page or prove the finite version by Dirichlet's simultaneous approximation, which is a page]`). Size: one slot (the statement, its proof for finite exponential sums with Q-independent frequencies, its instance for the primes, the numbers at (t, δ, L) of §3(a)); zoo entry: Group IV, "the Kronecker sharpness of the trivial bound for localized prime sums" — KILLS any brief proposing to certify a first-order datum's sign at a specific height by a t-uniform prime-side estimate (including Lindelöf-conditional ones, see the OR-branch).
