## IV.20 (new entry; heading + five bullets)

- **IV.20 The finite-rank target kill (no surface over a field, and no target of finite Q-rank, can carry ζ's diagonal row) — NEW, Session 35 (β-shapes, `results/beta-shapes-s35/NOTE.md` §2.3)**
- **STATEMENT.** Let (X, Y, c) be a pair in the sense of the SPEC (`results/f1-spec-s29/SPEC.md`) over a base object B of A2's class, with A5 (deg(Γ_1 ∩ Γ_n) = Λ_B(n)), A7 (c(Γ_n) the graph of φ_n) and A9 (Σ_{n ≠ 1 : c(Γ_n) = D} Λ_B(n) = κ (Δ · D)_Y). **Theorem R:** if the Q-linear span G of the diagonal row {(Δ · c(Γ_n))_Y : n ≥ 2} is finite-dimensional, then B has finitely many residue characteristics; conversely dim_Q G ≤ |char(B)| always, so dim_Q G < ∞ exactly when char(B) is finite (A9's one fixed κ is used; A7 only names the fibers of c). Hence over Spec Z, and over every number ring, NO target whose intersection form is Z-valued, Q-valued, valued in a finite-rank subgroup of R, or defined on a finite-rank Num(Y) — in particular no surface over a field in Weil's form, of any genus, with or without the Hodge index theorem or a Lefschetz identity — satisfies A5 + A7 + A9. Proof: A9 makes each κ(Δ · D)_Y the logarithm of a positive integer N_D (Lemma F); the logarithms of the primes are Q-linearly independent (unique factorization); a finite-dimensional span of such logarithms has a finite union of prime supports; the fiber of c(Γ_𝔪) contains 𝔪, so every residue characteristic of B occurs in some support. Companion: **Lemma F** — A9's finite fiber sums force every prime endomorphism φ_p to have infinite order in End_β(B), so every β with End_β(B) = {id} (terminal, subterminal, Durov's square, β = F₁) fails A9 by a divergent fiber (T1, T2). Rung 1 is untouched: a curve over F_q has one residue characteristic, its fiber products are q^{N_N}, and Weil's Z-valued form passes (E3 Theorem 3.4; Milne pp. 9–10). Consequence for the free slot β: it is narrowed to targets whose diagonal-row span has infinite Q-dimension — real-valued forms with (Δ · c(Γ_p))_Y = (1/κ) log N_p, N_p ∈ pZ — with Num(Y) of infinite rank and the Hodge index available only in a regularized form (the SPEC's A8′, A11′, A13′).
- **KILLS / RETURNS.** Any brief proposing a target for W*(Spec Z) that is a surface over a field, an algebraic surface with Z-valued intersection numbers, a finite-rank Néron–Severi group, or a finite-h Lefschetz identity with integer traces; any brief presenting Deligne-style rationality (finitely many eigenvalues whose traces are integers — a characteristic polynomial over Z, (H-Lef_Z)) as available on a square of Spec Z (finitely many transcendental eigencharacters with non-injective c — the sub-shape (D4-fin) of NOTE §3.4 — is NOT killed); any brief reading "the archimedean place" (Borger p. 5) as anything less than a diagonal row of infinite Q-rank. NOT killed (guard): (D4)-shaped targets with real-valued forms of infinite Q-rank (Arakelov-type values; regularized generators); the theorem is RH-blind and is not an S1 filter — it fires on every base with infinitely many residue characteristics whether or not RH holds for it (V.4 reading in NOTE §2.3 DH CHECK).
- **EXECUTABLE TEST.** For any proposed (β, Y, c): (1) write the value group of Y's intersection form and the Q-dimension of the span of {(Δ · c(Γ_n))_Y}; finite ⇒ dead by Theorem R. (2) compute the order of φ_p in End_β(B) for one prime: finite ⇒ dead by Lemma F. (3) check that (Δ · c(Γ_p))_Y = (1/κ) log N_p with p | N_p for the first few primes — a necessary condition, computable from the proposal's own pairing before any generator is discussed.
