## SEED N1 — `ly-infinity`: Lee–Yang functions of the primes (the Bohr lift as the change of variables zoo III.15 asks for) and infinite-rank Fourier quasicrystals

**Idea (orchestrator's derivation, to be re-derived and attacked).** Give every prime its own variable z_p (Bohr's lift: p^{−s} ↦ z_p). (1) For a finite set of primes and c_p := p^{−1/2}, Q(z) := Π_p (1 − c_p z_p) has no zeros in the closed unit polydisc, so each Blaschke factor (z_p − c_p)/(1 − c_p z_p) has modulus < 1 inside, and therefore P_±(z) := Π_p (1 − c_p z_p) ± Π_p (z_p − c_p) has NO zeros with all |z_p| < 1 and none with all |z_p| > 1: a Lee–Yang polynomial. Restricted to the Kronecker line z_p = p^{−it} it is f_±(t) = A(t) ± P^{−it} conj A(t), A(t) = Π_p (1 − p^{−1/2−it}) = Σ_{n | P} μ(n) n^{−1/2−it}, P = Π p — a Dirichlet polynomial all of whose zeros are REAL, for every finite set of primes, by multivariate stability alone. More generally det(1 − Z U), Z = diag(z_p), U unitary, is Lee–Yang, and the secular function det(1 − P^{1/2−s} U) = Σ_{n | P} μ(n) det(U_{S(n)}) √n · n^{−s} has all zeros on Re s = ½, with functional equation from Jacobi's complementary-minor identity. (2) The product form is exactly where the Euler product enters: the Bohr lift of a non-multiplicative Dirichlet polynomial (Davenport–Heilbronn's pieces) is not a product and need not be stable — the zoo I.1 filter acts at the axiom level. (3) The positivity acts in the fugacity-type variables (z_p) and the zeros live on a line inside the torus: the variable mismatch of zoo III.15 (read it with its riders: the Newman/Ising single-variable interface was priced NO; this seed is a different, multivariate interface) is bridged by restriction to the Kronecker line, as in the 2020–2024 classification of finite-rank Fourier quasicrystals (Kurasov–Sarnak; Olevskii–Ulanovskii; Alon–Cohen–Vinzant: real-rooted exponential polynomials are restrictions of Lee–Yang polynomials — `[recalled, unverified]`: find and read them, this is the one place prior art is the point). (4) ζ is the INFINITE-rank limit: the zeros of ζ (if on the line) form a Fourier quasicrystal whose spectrum is {±k log p} with weights log p · p^{−k/2}, plus the archimedean term; the monomial P^{−it} is replaced by the archimedean phase e^{−2iϑ(t)}. The Lee–Yang class is closed under locally uniform limits (Hurwitz). **So: a sequence of Lee–Yang functions of finitely many primes converging locally uniformly to ξ (or to a function with the same zeros) would prove RH; the divergence of Σ p^{−1} on the critical line is the obstruction to the naive limit.**

**Tasks.** (a) Re-derive (1)–(4) exactly; correct them. (b) Compute: for prime sets up to p ≤ X (X up to a few hundred), the zeros of f_± and of the archimedean-phase variants (replace P^{−it} by e^{−2iϑ(t)} or by the exact χ-factor) against the true zeros of ζ in windows of height T (mpmath `zetazero`); quantify the match as a function of (X, T); decide whether any renormalization of the weights c_p (smooth cutoffs c_p(X) = p^{−1/2} w(log p / log X), exponents σ ↓ ½, Blaschke products of higher degree per prime, a non-diagonal unitary coupling U between primes) gives locally uniform convergence to a function with ζ's zeros WHILE staying in the Lee–Yang class. (c) State and try to prove the sharpest theorem you can in either direction: a convergence theorem (close G or T), or a no-go of the form "no sequence of Lee–Yang functions of product type A·e^{iφ} ± conj can converge to ξ because …" (close K). (d) Inverse design: for B ≤ 12 primes, numerically optimize a unitary U (or a general Lee–Yang polynomial) so that the zeros of det(1 − P^{1/2−s}U) match ζ's zeros in a window; examine the optimizer for arithmetic structure; report honestly whether the fit is more than parameter counting. (e) Controls: the Bohr lift of the Davenport–Heilbronn function's Dirichlet-polynomial truncations and of F_{a,q} — where exactly does stability fail? (f) Formulate, as a precise conjecture or definition, what an "infinite-rank Lee–Yang function on the Bohr torus" should be so that RH becomes "ξ is one" — a candidate new object (KICKSTART 10(n): nearest published object and the exact difference).
