(2) *The problem is stated open in print in 2002, 2005, 2007, 2008, 2013, 2023, 2024/26 and 2025* (§3, twelve verbatim statements), with exactly one solved case, Deninger's [De02] elliptic-curve solenoid (rank-1 length spectrum, no archimedean leaf). (3) *Obstructions:* (a) every closed-3-manifold model is dead — the closed-orbit lengths lie in the period group of KMNT's closed time form on M₀, which is finitely generated because H₁(M₀) is, while {log p} is Z-linearly independent (R2, a short re-derivation extending Den05 Remark 3 / [x-18] p. 3 to the preserved-leaf case; needs finitely many preserved leaves); (b) Deninger's α = 1 conformality (needed for the Np^k coefficients, Den05 p. 27) is incompatible with a finite flow-invariant transverse measure on a compact foliated space (R1), so clause (iv) must be read as the e^{−t}-scaled (type III) measure of Leichtnam 2007 or as ÁLKL's infinite-mass b-density at the preserved leaf — under the invariant reading clause (iii) is forced, not independent; (c) by (ii) every γ_p is a source in the 3-space, which removes the object from the Anosov/Axiom-A/geodesic classes and hence from every periodic-data rigidity, prime-orbit, and Ruelle-zeta-at-zero theorem reached (§1.C–D become consistency markers: entropy 1, Ruelle zeta = ζ(s) with ζ(0) = −1/2).
