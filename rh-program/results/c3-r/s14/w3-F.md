## 0. VERDICT (stated first)

**PARTIAL — with the analytic core of W3 established as a theorem in corrected form, and the statement W3 was asked to mirror shown to be false at its source.** Precisely, and without rounding:

1. **THEOREM (NO) against the literal target.** The topology-coincidence statements W3 was to reproduce "exactly" are false as written, already in the manifold case (already for a one-point transversal): [ÁLKL23] Prop. 3.2 (p. 14), Cor. 3.4 (p. 14), Cor. 4.5 (p. 19, for every compact M), Cor. 6.12 (p. 31) and its cut-manifold analog Cor. 7.11 (p. 44), together with Cor. 3.6's acyclicity clause (p. 15: S^∞(U × ℝ^l) is not even regular for any nonempty open U ⊂ ℝⁿ, n ≥ 1). Theorems 3.1–3.5 give explicit counterexamples (translated, dilated and escaping bumps) and identify the single invalid step of the source's proof (the equality ‖φ(a)‖′ = ‖a‖′ on the completion, p. 14). The text of every one of these statements and proofs is verbatim identical in arXiv v3 (June 2024), up to renumbering (§1). Since the counterexamples are constant in any parameter, **no transplant of the literal statements exists: there is nothing true to transplant.** `[novelty: single-check]`
2. **THEOREM (YES) for what the acyclicity criterion actually needs.** What [Wen03, Thm. 6.1] as quoted requires is coincidence of the topologies of two higher steps on a 0-NEIGHBORHOOD of a lower step, not on the whole lower step. This is TRUE for the symbol spectra with compact base variable (Theorem 5.1) and for the b-weighted spectra on a compact collar (Theorem 5.2), by a new mechanism — an anisotropic, weight-adapted Landau–Kolmogorov interpolation between the order-m bound on the 0-neighborhood and the order-m″ smallness (Props. 4.3–4.4) — and, **the point of W3, for their parametrized versions over any compact Hausdorff T, by the same pointwise inequality followed by sup over t** (Theorem 5.3). The inequality is uniform in the parameter; nothing about T beyond compactness enters, total disconnectedness plays no role, and the result is insensitive to whether the transversal dependence is designed as C(T; ·) or ℓ^∞(T; ·) (Theorem 5.3(b)). Hence, via [Wen03, Thm. 6.1 / Prop. 6.3 / Prop. 6.4 / Cor. 6.5] *as quoted on disk*, the parametrized model LF-spaces are acyclic, hence boundedly/compactly/sequentially retractive, complete, regular and Hausdorff (Cor. 5.4). `[novelty: single-check]`
3. **The manifold-case package the program consumes is re-secured with valid proofs:** acyclicity of I(M, L) (M compact), A(M̄), Ȧ(M̄), K(M̄), and through [ÁLKL23] §7's identifications J(M, L), K(M, L) (Theorem 6.2); the Montel halves of Cors. 4.7/6.14/6.20/6.25 (Theorem 6.3); and the exactness engine of the dual-conormal sequences ([ÁLKL23] Claim 6.43 = v3 Claim 6.46, reused verbatim for Prop. 8.8) re-proved WITHOUT the false Cor. 6.12/7.11 — bounded retractivity plus the partial extension maps suffice (Prop. 7.1). So [ALKL]'s sequences (1.3.1)/(1.3.2) and Theorems 1.3.3/1.3.6 are not damaged, and the S2 note's inventory rows I-8/I-9/I-10 stand with one correction: the third input of Claim 6.43 (Cor. 6.12) is dispensable. `[novelty: single-check]`
4. **What is CONDITIONAL (the interface with W1/W2, definitional not analytic):** the transplant theorem (Theorem 6.5) applies to the S2 note's I_τ, J_τ, Ȧ_τ, K_τ as soon as their symbol-order steps are defined by the parametrized (4.10)-type embeddings into the model spectra of §5 (hypothesis (H1)) and the (4.12)-type interleaving with the Sobolev filtration is available (hypothesis (H2)); the chart-level part of (H1) is proved here (Lemma 6.4), the gluing and the parametrized Prop. 4.3 are W2's content and are not proved here.
