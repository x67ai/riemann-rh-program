## 1. What W3 asked, exactly, and how the question split

The S2 note (§5, read verbatim this session) defines W3 as: *"topology-coincidence for the transplanted symbol/Sobolev spectra (the [ÁLKL23] Cor. 4.5/6.12/6.24 analogs), yielding acyclicity, hence all retractivities ([Wen03] Thm. 6.1 + Prop. 6.4). The mechanism (pointwise symbol-seminorm interpolation) survives sup-over-t; this is the one place a referee should press, and the one place whose failure would reopen the verdict."* Row I-8 of its §4 adds the mechanism claim: *"[ÁLKL23] Cor. 4.5's mechanism is symbol-seminorm interpolation on S^{m̄}-scales — inequalities between weighted sup-norms in ξ, pointwise in the base point, hence pointwise in t; taking sup over t preserves them. So the SAME mechanism proves the C(T)-valued coincidence."*

Read against the source, the item contains three distinct claims: (W3-lit) the whole-step coincidence statements Cor. 4.5/6.12/6.24 hold and transplant; (W3-acyc) the transplanted spectra are acyclic in the sense of the quoted [Wen03] criterion, hence boundedly/compactly/sequentially retractive, complete, regular; (W3-mech) the source's mechanism is a pointwise interpolation that survives sup over t. The adjudicated answers: (W3-lit) FALSE at source (§3); (W3-acyc) TRUE for the model spectra and their C(T; ·)-versions, conditional on the definitional interface for the S2 spaces (§4, §7); (W3-mech) the source contains NO interpolation argument — its route is the false Prop. 3.2 — but the S2 note's description was correct as a *prediction* of what the true mechanism is (§4.1–§4.3), and the sup-over-t transfer is in fact a formal property of Wengenroth's criterion needing no estimate at all (§4.4).
