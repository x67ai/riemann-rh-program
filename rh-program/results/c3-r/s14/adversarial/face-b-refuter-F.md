### Attack line (1) — the weight W: well defined, positive, l.s.c., +∞ on packets?

**Definition (my own, from (41)–(43)).** On X̊(ℂ) put ρ̂(P) := |ker(P^×|_{μ(κ(x))})| ∈ ℕ ∪ {∞} if x = η, and ρ̂(P) := +∞ if x is closed. On X̌(ℂ) put ρ̂(F_ν⁻¹P) := ρ̂(P)/ν (∞/ν = ∞). On X₀ put W([P₀,u]) := ρ̂₀(P₀)·u.

*Well defined on the colimit.* If F_ν⁻¹P = F_{ν′}⁻¹P′ then F_{ν′}P = F_νP′ in X̊(ℂ) (p. 25), and both have the same underlying point x (F does not move x, p. 22). Char 0 with finite kernels: (43) with ν_x = ν gives ν′ρ(P) = νρ(P′), i.e. ρ(P)/ν = ρ(P′)/ν′. Char 0 with an infinite kernel on one side: ζ ∈ ker(F_{ν′}P)^×|_μ iff ζ^{ν′} ∈ ker P^×|_μ, and ζ ↦ ζ^{ν′} is a surjection of μ_∞ with fibers of size ν′, so ker(F_{ν′}P)|_μ is infinite iff ker P|_μ is; same on the other side; both sides ∞. Closed x: both ∞ by definition. **Re-derived.** (Under (Tors) the infinite-kernel case is empty on the E-locus, but the definition is sound without it.)

*ℚ>0-conformality.* For q = m/n and P̌ = F_ν⁻¹P: F_{m/n}F_ν⁻¹P = F_{nν}⁻¹F_mP, ρ̂ = mρ̂(P)/(nν) = q·ρ̂(P̌), using (43) for ρ(F_mP) = mρ(P) in char 0; ∞ in char > 0. **Re-derived.**

*G-invariance.* ker((P^×∘σ)|_μ) = σ⁻¹(ker P^×|_μ), same cardinality; σ preserves the characteristic. Descends to ρ̂₀ on X̌₀(ℂ). **Re-derived.**

*Invariance under the suspension identifications.* W((P₀,u)·q) = ρ̂₀(F_qP₀)·q⁻¹u = q ρ̂₀(P₀) q⁻¹ u = W([P₀,u]). **Re-derived.** W∘φ^t = e^t W since φ^t only scales u. **Re-derived.**

*Positivity.* ρ̂ ≥ 1 (the kernel contains 1), u > 0. **Re-derived.**

*Value on packets.* By definition ρ̂ = +∞ over closed points; so W = +∞ on ⨿_p Γ^E_p = Π⁻¹(closed points), where Π: X₀ → Spec ℤ is the descended pr_{X₀} (constant on ℚ>0-orbits by p. 27). It is a genuine value, not "undefined": Deninger's ρ over char p is a class in ℚ>0/p^ℤ ((44), p. 33), so ρ·u would be well defined on Γ_p only as a point of the circle ℝ>0/p^ℤ; the choice +∞ is the unique real-valued extension compatible with l.s.c., because (see the char-p case below) any net approaching a packet point from characteristic zero has ρ → ∞. **Re-derived.** Conversely W < ∞ iff x = η and ker(P|_μ) finite — **under (Tors), iff x = η.** This is the one place admissibility is consumed, and it is exactly where §3 strikes.

*Lower semicontinuity of ρ̂ on X̊(ℂ) — done with explicit open sets, not nets.* Fix a real M and P with ρ̂(P) > M. (a) x = η, kernel finite of order N > M (or infinite — then pick any finite N > M with μ_N ⊂ ker, possible since an infinite subgroup of μ_∞ ≅ ℚ/ℤ contains μ_N for arbitrarily large N): the set U_N := ⋂_{ζ∈μ_N} ev_ζ⁻¹(B(1, ½)) is open (finitely many continuous evaluations) and contains P; on U_N every P′ has P′(ζ) ∈ μ_{ord ζ}(ℂ) ∩ B(1,½) = {1}, so μ_N ⊂ ker P′|_μ, so ρ̂(P′) ≥ N > M if P′ is generic and = ∞ if not. (b) x closed over p: ζ ∈ μ_{p^k} is a unit of ℤ̄, ζ̄ ∈ F̄_p^× satisfies ζ̄^{p^k} = 1, and (T−1)^{p^k} = T^{p^k} − 1 in characteristic p forces ζ̄ = 1, so P(ζ) = 1; choose k with p^k > M and use U_{p^k} as in (a). Hence {ρ̂ > M} is open. **Re-derived; holds on all of X̊(ℂ), no (Tors) needed.** This is the "root-of-unity rigidity" (values at ζ lie in a finite set, so equality is forced near the limit) and the "p-adic inflation" (μ_{p^∞} ⊂ kernel of every char-p point). I looked for a net breaking l.s.c. — a net P_i → P with ρ̂(P_i) < ρ̂(P) eventually — and there is none: the kernel of the limit is contained in the eventual kernels. What CAN happen is the opposite jump (ρ̂(P_i) → ∞ while ρ̂(P) finite; e.g. kernels μ_{ℓ_i} with primes ℓ_i → ∞ converge pointwise on μ_∞ to the trivial kernel on every fixed μ_N): ρ̂ is not upper semicontinuous, and that is harmless for the argument.

*Transport to X₀ in the quotient topology.* {W > a} open in X₀ iff Q⁻¹{W > a} = {(P₀,u): ρ̂₀(P₀)u > a} open in X̌₀(ℂ)_E × ℝ>0. The map π̌ × id is a continuous open surjection (product of an open map with the identity), so a subset of the target is open iff its preimage is: need {(P̌,u): ρ̂(P̌)u > a} open in X̌(ℂ)_E × ℝ>0. Cover X̌(ℂ)_E by the E-charts F_ν⁻¹X̊(ℂ)_E = F_ν⁻¹X̊(ℂ) ∩ X̌(ℂ)_E (equal by bi-invariance, Prop. 4.2), open by p. 47; F_ν × id carries chart × ℝ>0 homeomorphically onto X̊(ℂ)_E × ℝ>0 and the set to {(P,u): ρ̂(P)u/ν > a}, which is open because a product of a [1,∞]-valued l.s.c. function with a continuous strictly positive function is l.s.c. **Re-derived.** (qb-build proves the same via closedness of the complement; qb-kill via Cor. 4.4; all three agree.)

**Outcome (1): every claim about W STANDS for admissible E.** The single consumption of (Tors) — {W < ∞} = generic locus — is confirmed as the load-bearing hinge.

### Attack line (2) — the minimum step and the backward flow

Let K ⊂ X₀ be nonempty, quasi-compact (subspace topology, no separation axiom), with φ^{-t}(K) ⊂ K for t > 0. W|_K is l.s.c. (restriction) and > 0. Put m := inf_K W ∈ (0,∞]. If m = ∞ then W ≡ ∞ on K. If m < ∞ and no point attains it, then for each z ∈ K choose a real a_z with m < a_z < W(z) (any a_z > m if W(z) = ∞); the sets {W > a} ∩ K, a > m, are open in K and cover K; a finite subcover {W > a_1}, …, {W > a_n} has union {W > min a_i} ⊇ K, so inf_K W ≥ min a_i > m — contradiction. So m = W(z₀) for some z₀ ∈ K. If m < ∞ then φ^{-1}z₀ ∈ K and W(φ^{-1}z₀) = e^{-1}m < m = inf_K W — contradiction. So m = ∞ and W ≡ ∞ on K. **Re-derived.** The W = ∞ points (packets) create no problem: they are exactly where the argument ends, not where it breaks; and a flow-invariant K consisting of packet points is allowed (the packets themselves are such sets). For K = f(Y) with Y an ℝ-space and f equivariant, φ^{-t}f(Y) = f(φ_Y^{-t}Y) = f(Y). **Outcome (2): STANDS.** (The max/min slip in qb-kill's Step 1 is noted in §0; immaterial.)

It is **false** for the non-admissible class of all characters, and false for the unitary closure Y₀^{full} = X̌₀(S¹) ×_{ℚ>0} ℝ>0 as printed on p. 49 (§3.4). The only place (Tors) enters is the identification {W < ∞} = generic locus (qb-kill Thm. 5.4(4); qa-kill's e = |ker Ψ|_μ| < ∞ in Lemma 3.3); everything else is (Tors)-free. So the theorem is *exactly* co-extensive with Deninger's standing hypothesis, as qb-kill §10.1 says — but the E-free failure is not "degenerate blobs only": it is genuine packets accumulating compactly onto a (Tors)-violating unitary generic orbit.

**Which topology.** The quotient topology on X̌₀(ℂ)_E × ℝ>0 (product) under Q, read from [x-03] §10 p. 63 and [x-06] p. 11. The negative conclusions persist for every **finer** topology on X₀ (the sets {W > a} and V_p stay open; quasi-compactness gets harder); for a strictly **coarser** topology l.s.c. of W could fail and the theorem would need re-proof. The topology on X̊(ℂ) (pointwise convergence, p. 40) and the openness of π̌, F_q, Q, and of the strata F_ν⁻¹X̊(ℂ)_E (pp. 43, 47) are the only topological inputs, and each was read at the cited page.

**Compact vs quasi-compact vs non-compact.** Proved for every nonempty **quasi-compact** Y with an ℝ-action (continuity of the action on Y is not even used; only equivariance and continuity of f) — hence for every compact Y, Hausdorff or not, metrizable or not, of any dimension, with any orbit spectrum. Nothing is proved for **non-compact** Y: Y_∞ = ⨆_p ℝ>0/p^ℤ is a continuous equivariant witness with one orbit per packet, and the theorem is silent on whether a non-compact 3-dimensional lamination extends it. Compactness of the S4 object is a ledger specification (§8 S4 row), not a word in Deninger's p. 40 question; [Ghy99] is not on disk, so whether "Riemann surface lamination in the sense of [Ghy99]" forces compactness is [RU] and unasserted here.

**Which targets.** X₀^E (all admissible E) and Y₀^E := X̌₀(S¹)_E ×_{ℚ>0} ℝ>0 ⊂ X₀^E: the theorem stands. Y₀^{full} (p. 49 literal): refuted (§3.4). The conjectural X̄₀ (pp. 39–40): not addressed by the theorem (unbuilt).

**What the theorem does and does not say for S4.** It says: no compact Y with a continuous ℝ-action admits a continuous equivariant map into X₀^E meeting infinitely many packets — so the ledger's S4 (compact, target X₀ = X₀^E) is dead as a mapping problem, for every admissible E. It does not say: that S4 is dead for the E-free unitary closure (it is alive there at "arbitrary compact Y" grade, §3.4, and open at "dim-3 lamination with transverse measure" grade); that S4 is dead for non-compact Y; or that S4 is dead for X̄₀.
