### 3.3 B3: the splitting Q̄^× = μ × V
μ = μ(Q̄) is divisible (a primitive mn-th root of unity has an n-th power of order m generating μ_m). A divisible subgroup is a direct summand — **on disk**: [x-03] p. 28, "both split since µ(k) and µ(C) are divisible", and p. 53.
