### 3.1 The topology on 𝕏₀ (assignment items 1–2), settled

(a) *Chart.* X•(C) for affine X₀ = Spec R₀ carries pointwise convergence on multiplicative maps R → C (p. 40): Hausdorff (subspace of a product of copies of a valued field), metrizable. For X₀ = Spec Z, R = Z̄. At a point x over (p): κ(x)^× = μ^{(p)}, every r ∉ 𝔭_x has r̄ of finite order prime to p; so at fixed x pointwise convergence of (x, P_α^×) is convergence of P_α^×(ζ) for each ζ ∈ μ^{(p)}. ✓
(b) *Galois quotient.* X•₀(C) = X•(C)/G with the quotient topology (p. 42); π open (p. 43); G acts by homeomorphisms (Lemma 7.3) and jointly continuously (Prop. 7.5). ✓
(c) *Colimit.* X̌(C) has the inductive limit topology; X•(C) is clopen in it (Prop. 7.4 a) and each F_q is a homeomorphism (7.4 b), so every chart F_ν^{-1}(X•(C)) is clopen and F_ν maps it homeomorphically onto X•(C); the charts are nested and cover (pp. 24–25). X̌₀(C) = X̌(C)/G ≅ colim X•₀(C), π̌ open (p. 43). **Therefore closure in the colimit is chart-local (closure is local on open sets: cl_Y(A) ∩ U = cl_U(A ∩ U) for U open), and on each chart the colimit topology IS the pointwise topology transported by F_ν.** No subnet limit exists "pointwise but not in the colimit" or conversely — the only thing the colimit topology does is make each chart clopen, so a net converging into a chart is eventually inside it. This settles item 2: the worry was real in general and does not arise here. ✓
(d) *E-loci.* Subspace topologies on X•(C)_E, X•₀(C)_E; inductive limit = subspace topology on X̌(C)_E, X̌₀(C)_E because the charts are open (p. 47; re-derived: the E-charts U_ν = F_ν^{-1}(X•₀(C)) ∩ X̌₀(C)_E by backward invariance, Prop. 4.2, and cover X̌₀(C)_E). All of (a)–(c) transfer ("All preceding results in this section remain true …", p. 47). Closures in E-spaces are ambient closures intersected with the E-locus. ✓

**(L1)** closure local on open sets — proved above. **(L2)** for f continuous, open, surjective: f^{-1}(cl A) = cl(f^{-1}A), hence cl A = f(cl f^{-1}A). *Proof.* f^{-1}(cl A) is closed ⊇ f^{-1}A. ### 3.2 Route I — the packet is the fiber of a continuous flow-invariant map (every arithmetic scheme)

Let X₀ be an arithmetic scheme, E admissible, x₀ with finite residue field.
(I-1) pr_{X₀} : X̌₀(C)_E → X₀ is continuous into the Zariski topology: Lemma 7.1 (affine, p. 40), p. 42 (any arithmetic scheme, glued topology, and the quotient X•₀(C)), p. 43 (the colimit: "It suffices to show that pr_X ∘ (F_ν^{-1}|X•(C)) is continuous for each ν. Since pr_X = pr_X ∘ F_ν, this follows"), p. 47 (E-version; equivalently, the restriction of a continuous map to a subspace). It is Q₀^{>0}-invariant: Q₀^{>0} acts trivially on X₀ (p. 27), i.e. pr_{X₀}(F_q P) = pr_{X₀}(P). Hence h(P, u) := pr_{X₀}(P) is continuous on X̌₀(C)_E × R^{>0} and constant on the Q-orbits (P,u)q = (F_q P, q^{-1}u); by the universal property of the quotient topology it descends to a continuous **Π : 𝕏₀ → X₀, Π[P,u] = pr_{X₀}(P)**. Since φ^t[P,u] = [P, ue^t], Π∘φ^t = Π. ✓
(I-2) q^{-1}(Γ^E_{x₀}) = C^E_{x₀} × R^{>0}: Γ^E_{x₀} = q(C^E_{x₀} × R^{>0}) by definition (p. 38), and C^E_{x₀} × R^{>0} is q-saturated because C_{x₀} is Q-invariant (a fiber of the Q-invariant pr_{X₀}, p. 31) and X̌₀(C)_E is Q-invariant (Prop. 4.2). ### 3.4 Break attempts and necessity of the hypotheses

* *Drop (Tors).* In the un-cut suspension X̌₀(C) ×_Q R^{>0}, (R1)–(R4) go through and (R5) becomes cl(S) = π(K): with n_k := ∏_{ℓ≤k, ℓ≠p} ℓ^k → 0 in Ẑ_{(p)} (v_ℓ(n_k) = k for k ≥ ℓ; check C4), F_{n_k}P₀ → π(x, 1) and [P₀, n_k u] = [F_{n_k}P₀, u] → [π(x,1), u], a point whose Q^{>0}-isotropy is all of Q^{>0} (its R^{>0}-isotropy is Q^{>0} ⊂ R^{>0} — dense, but it is **not** a fixed point of the flow, contra referee F §5.1's phrase) and which lies in no packet. **Equality is false without (Tors).** Since every admissible E carries (Tors), this is a scope fact, not an escape.
* *Coarser topology on 𝕏₀.* Would void Π's continuity (Route I) and q's openness (Route II). Not an escape: the quotient topology is the sourced reading (§3.1 e). Recorded as the one hypothesis a reader must accept.
* *Unbounded denominators / Galois-created limits / other strata.* A net F_{r_α}P₀ converging in X̌₀(C)_E converges into some open chart U_ν, so its prime-to-p denominators eventually divide ν ((R2)) — no escape; Galois nets are absorbed by (L3); other strata are excluded by Route I (cl(γ) ⊂ Π^{-1}((p))). No counterexample exists to the statement as scoped.
* *Consistency with Cor. A.2 (non-Hausdorff) and Theorem A.* A compact circle dense in a strictly larger closed set, with uncountably many disjoint such circles, is impossible in a Hausdorff space and possible here precisely because 𝕏₀ is not Hausdorff along the packets. Referee O's audit went further (its Cor. O.7: cl{z} = Γ^E_p for every packet point z, so 𝕏₀ is not even T₁ there).
