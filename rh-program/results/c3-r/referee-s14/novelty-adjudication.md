**Decision.** An irreducible T₁ space with two points is not Hausdorff, so Prop. 10.3 + Remark exhibit, in print, a non-Hausdorff T₁ suspension-shaped quotient by the same Q₀^{>0}-action — the adelic shadow Y of X₀ under the map r of §9/§10 — and Deninger uses that failure to prove H⁰_F = R. Hence PARTIAL: phenomenon anticipated, statement new. Sweep O's severity MAJOR is upheld for the *record wording* (adjudication §4 item 3 "New negative structural datum"; probe B Cor. A.2; probe B §9 item 2): each must place pp. 49, 63, 64, 76 in front of the claim and state the increment as (i) X₀ itself, (ii) localized in a single packet, (iii) no periodic orbit closed, (iv) the explicit witness. Probe B's Cor. A.2 already cites pp. 48–49 and calls the result "the topological face of Deninger's own remark" — the missing anchor there is Prop. 10.3 (p. 64). The standing conflict with [r3s-08] Thms 2.2.8/2.2.9 ("homeomorphism", "closed R₊-orbits", pp. 16–18, re-read) is already on the books as W11; the record should say "in conflict with the wording of", which probe B's dated §3 block already does.
