### N4 — Haar-average road and DQ-M (measured trace formula for a continuum of closed orbits)

*(c) Measured trace formulas with non-isolated fixed sets — exist, but not for orbit continua of foliated flows:*
- Álvarez López–Kordyukov, *Lefschetz distribution of Lie foliations*, arXiv:math/0703753 (downloaded; pp. 3–5 and §10.1 p. 32 read): Thm 1.3, p. 4: "Suppose that every fixed point of φ' is leafwise simple. Then L_dis(F) = pr_{2*}(ε · Λ'_{Fix(φ')})" where "Fix₀(φ') is a C^∞ manifold of dimension equal to codim F … So Λ defines a measure Λ'_{Fix₀(φ')} on Fix₀(φ')" — a Lefschetz distribution whose geometric side integrates a **transverse invariant measure over a continuum of fixed points** (for a transverse Lie-group action); p. 5: "We hope to prove elsewhere another version of Theorem 1.3 with a more general condition on the fixed points … what is needed is another version of the Lefschetz theorem of [14], which holds for more general fixed point sets when the transverse measure is C^∞". But for flows (codimension one, §10.1, p. 32): "Assume that all closed orbits of φ are simple."
- Heitsch–Lazarov, *A Lefschetz theorem for foliated manifolds*, Topology 29 (1990) 127–162 [located by search W24; text not read]: fixed-point sets are submanifolds transverse to the foliation, measured by a holonomy-invariant transverse measure; Álvarez López–Kordyukov arXiv:0801.4628 (abstract, C4 [5]) asks for the topological Λ-Lefschetz number in general.
- Lazarov, *Transverse index and periodic orbits*, GAFA 10 (2000) 124–159: zbMATH review 1443218 (read via the zbMATH API): "assumes that the action's periodic orbits are isolated and nondegenerate".
- All foliated-flow trace formulas on disk assume isolated/non-degenerate closed orbits: [x-22] §2, p. 6, immediately before Theorem 2.2: "We assume that all compact orbits γ of the R-action are non-degenerate"; [r3s-21] Theorem 2: "Assume that the closed orbits γ of the flow φ^t acting on S = L ×_{q^Z} R₊^* are non degenerate"; [r3s-17]/arXiv:2402.06671 abstract: "Assume the closed orbits of φ are simple"; [r3s-19] p. 13: "Then the closed orbits are isolated if we take the periods in…".
So the 9.4 note's sentence "no published trace formula admits a continuum of periodic orbits with a transverse measure on the continuum" is correct for **closed orbits of foliated flows**, but the measured-fixed-set technology (Heitsch–Lazarov; ALK Thm 1.3) exists for **fixed sets of leaf-preserving maps/transverse actions**, and DQ-M should be positioned relative to it: the packet base B_p lies in the *leaf* direction of X₀ (the flow is transverse to the leaves; the packet circles are flow-lines), so it is neither the Heitsch–Lazarov "fixed set transverse to F" case nor the ALK "dimension = codim F" case — the note should say this explicitly, because it is the reason none of the cited formulas applies.
