Products of the per-prime Tate curves of absolute geometry carry no correspondence calculus for the Weil explicit formula

Connes and Consani's June-2026 absolute geometry of $\SpecZ$ produces, for every prime $p$, a complex elliptic curve: the Tate curve $E_p = \mathbf{C}^\times/p^{\mathbf{Z}}$, a rectangular torus with period lattice $\mathbf{Z} \oplus \mathbf{Z}\cdot i\log p/(2\pi)$. A natural proposal is to assemble a correspondence calculus for the Weil explicit formula — the long-missing ``square of $\SpecZ$'' — from the $(p,q)$-graded family of classical abelian surfaces $E_p \times E_q$, importing Castelnuovo–Severi positivity fiber by fiber. We prove that the proposal is empty. The rigidity that does the first half of the work is not new and is not claimed here: for distinct primes, $\operatorname{Hom}(E_p, E_q) \neq 0$ if and only if $\log p \cdot \log q \in 4\pi^2\mathbf{Q}$ (Theorem 1), a transcendence coincidence impossible under the four-exponentials conjecture; unconditionally, each prime has at most one exceptional partner prime (Theorem 2), so among $E_2\times E_3$, $E_2\times E_5$, $E_3\times E_5$ at most one surface carries a nonzero correspondence group (Theorem 3). \textbf{Theorems 1–3 are a specialization to primes of J. Winkelmann's rigidity for the curves $\mathbf{C}^\times/\lambda^{\mathbf{Z}}$, from April 2002}; what is added here is the identification of the group $\operatorname{Hom}(E_p, E_q)$, its rank and the isogeny degree, none of which appears in his paper. The new results are the consequences. Off this conjecturally empty matching, $\operatorname{NS}(E_p \times E_q)$ has rank $2$: there are no graph classes and no diagonal class, every divisor class acts as zero on $H^1$, and the Castelnuovo–Severi inequality is vacuous (Theorem 4). On the diagonal, $\operatorname{End}(E_p) = \mathbf{Z}$ unconditionally — the CM alternative $(\log p)^2 \in 4\pi^2\mathbf{Q}$ is refuted by Gelfond–Schneider — and the complete intersection profile of the graphs $\Gamma_m$ consists of universal integers independent of $p$ (Theorem 5). No assignment of correspondence groups, composition data, and intersection pairings on the family realizes the required calculus (Theorem 6): three independent obstructions, each individually fatal. The family-level kill is unconditional; the paper carries a single open transcendence rider, whose negation is a published conjecture of D. Bertrand — the weak form of the four-exponentials conjecture — and which is consumed by none of the theorems.
