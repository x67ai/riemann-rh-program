Products of the per-prime Tate curves of absolute geometry carry no correspondence calculus for the Weil explicit formula

Connes and Consani's June-2026 absolute geometry of $\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf{Z}$ produces, for every prime $p$, a complex elliptic curve: the Tate curve $E_p = \mathbf{C}^\times/p^{\mathbf{Z}}$, a rectangular torus with period lattice $\mathbf{Z} \oplus \mathbf{Z}\cdot i\log p/(2\pi)$. A natural proposal is to assemble a correspondence calculus for the Weil explicit formula — the long-missing “square of $\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf{Z}$” — from the $(p,q)$-graded family of classical abelian surfaces $E_p \times E_q$, importing Castelnuovo–Severi positivity fiber by fiber. We prove that the proposal is empty. For distinct primes, $\mathrm{Hom}(E_p, E_q) \neq 0$ if and only if $\log p \cdot \log q \in 4\pi^2\mathbf{Q}$ (Theorem 1), a transcendence coincidence impossible under the four-exponentials conjecture; unconditionally, each prime has at most one exceptional partner prime (Theorem 2), so among $E_2\times E_3$, $E_2\times E_5$, $E_3\times E_5$ at most one surface carries a nonzero correspondence group (Theorem 3). Theorems 1–3 specialize to primes a rigidity of Winkelmann for the curves $\mathbf{C}^\times/\lambda^{\mathbf{Z}}$; what is added here is the identification of the group $\mathrm{Hom}(E_p, E_q)$, its rank and the isogeny degree. Off this conjecturally empty matching, $\mathrm{NS}(E_p \times E_q)$ has rank $2$: there are no graph classes and no diagonal class, every divisor class acts as zero on $H^1$, and the Castelnuovo–Severi inequality is vacuous (Theorem 4). On the diagonal, $\mathrm{End}(E_p) = \mathbf{Z}$ unconditionally — the CM alternative $(\log p)^2 \in 4\pi^2\mathbf{Q}$ is refuted by Gelfond–Schneider — and the complete intersection profile of the graphs $\Gamma_m$ consists of universal integers independent of $p$ (Theorem 5). No assignment of correspondence groups, composition data, and intersection pairings on the family realizes the required calculus (Theorem 6): three independent obstructions, each individually fatal. The family-level kill is unconditional; the paper carries a single open transcendence rider, whose negation is a published conjecture of D. Bertrand — the weak form of the four-exponentials conjecture — and which is consumed by none of the theorems.
