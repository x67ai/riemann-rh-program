## 11. THE SEPARATE FACT-CHECK — Farmer–Gonek–Lee and the constant 0.8051

### What the report claims

`results/arxiv/novelty-check.md`, lines 135–137 and 780–782:

> the history line quotes **Farmer–Gonek–Lee's published distinct-zeros constant 0.8051**, which
> collides …
> * **Disambiguate 0.8051** wherever it appears: A4's finite-N matched-budget corner
>   (3N − B₁(1+ε))/(2N) at ε = 0.05 is *not* Farmer–Gonek–Lee's published distinct-zeros constant,
>   which is also 0.8051 to four decimals.

### How I ran this down

The trap here was real and I nearly fell into it. The obvious primary source is arXiv:0803.0425 —
and it is the **wrong** document. That preprint has only two authors (Farmer and Gonek, v1, 4 Mar
2008; no later version was ever posted), and its Corollary 1.4 reads "the number of distinct zeros
of the zeta-function in [0,T] is larger than (0.6544 + o(1))N(T)". Grepping it for `0.805` returns
nothing. Anyone stopping there would have written REFUTED, wrongly.

The three-author paper is the **published** JLMS version, which is not on arXiv. It *is* in this
program's own corpus.

### Commands

```
curl -sSL "http://export.arxiv.org/api/query?id_list=0803.0425"        # 2 authors, 2008, 0.6544
curl -sSL -H "User-Agent: Mozilla/5.0" "https://arxiv.org/pdf/0803.0425v1" -o fg08.pdf
pdftotext -layout fg08.pdf fg08.txt ; grep -n "0\.805\|8051" fg08.txt   # -> no hits
curl -sSL "https://api.crossref.org/works?query.bibliographic=Farmer+Gonek+Lee+Pair+correlation+zeros+derivative+Riemann+xi-function+Journal+London+Mathematical+Society&rows=4"
WebFetch https://academic.oup.com/jlms/article-abstract/90/1/241/2939424
ls fetched*/r-12a-farmer-gonek-lee-2014*.pdf
pdftotext -layout "fetched-r2/r-12a-farmer-gonek-lee-2014-pair-correlation-zeros-xi-prime-jlms90.pdf" fgl14.txt
grep -n "0\.805\|8051" fgl14.txt
```

### Raw evidence — the published paper, read directly

`fetched-r2/r-12a-farmer-gonek-lee-2014-pair-correlation-zeros-xi-prime-jlms90.pdf`, 29 pages,
PDF Title field `doi.10.1112/10.1112/jlms/jdu026`. Its own first-page masthead:

```
J. London Math. Soc. (2) 90 (2014) 241-269      (C) 2014 London Mathematical Society
                                                doi:10.1112/jlms/jdu026

          Pair correlation of the zeros of the derivative
                   of the Riemann ξ-function

            David W. Farmer, Steven M. Gonek and Yoonbok Lee
```

The constant, in the published statement of Corollary 1.4 (text line 144):

```
  Corollary 1.4.  Assume RH. The number of distinct zeros of the zeta-function in [0, T]
is greater than 0.8051N(T) for all sufficiently large T.

  Note, however, that Bui and Heath-Brown [2] have recently shown by a different method
that the number of distinct zeros is greater than 0.84665N(T).
```

and its proof, at the end of the paper (text line ~2020):

```
Proof of Corollary 1.4.  In [10], Farmer proved the formula
       N_d(T) >= ( 2^{-1} β_0 + 2^{-5} β_5 + Σ_{n=1}^{4} 2^{-n-1} β_n ) N(T),
where β_j = liminf_T N_s^{(j)}(T) / N^{(j)}(T) … Conrey [4] has shown that β1 > 0.79874,
β2 > 0.93469, β3 > 0.9673, β4 > 0.98006, β5 > 0.9863, and by Corollary 1.3 we now have
β1 > 0.8584. … Bui and Heath-Brown [2] showed that this holds on RH alone. Therefore, we find
that
  N_d(T) >= (0.7037/2 + 0.9863/32 + 0.8584/4 + 0.93469/8 + 0.9673/16 + 0.98006/32) N(T)
          = 0.8051 N(T).
```

I recomputed that sum: 0.35185 + 0.030821875 + 0.2146 + 0.11683625 + 0.06045625 + 0.030626875 =
**0.80519125**, which the authors print as 0.8051 (truncation, not rounding — 0.80519 would round
to 0.8052). So the published constant is exactly the four-decimal string "0.8051".

Independent corroboration from a second peer-reviewed paper — Chirre, Gonçalves and de Laat,
arXiv:1810.08843 (= [CGdL20], already in the A4 bibliography), immediately after their Corollary 3
on N_d(T):

```
   Using the pair correlation approach, the best previous result known is due to Farmer, Gonek and
Lee [24] with constant 0.8051. By a different technique, assuming RH, Bui and Heath-Brown [4]
improved the constant to 0.8466.
```

and their reference `[24] D. Farmer, S. Gonek and Y. Lee, Pair correlation of the zeros of the
derivative of the Riemann ξ-function, J. Lond. Math. …`. As a reliability check on that secondary
report, their "0.8466" for Bui–Heath-Brown matches BHB13's own printed κ_d ≥ 0.84665 (read off the
on-disk `fetched/w-11`), so CGdL are quoting others' constants accurately. But the verdict below
rests on the FGL paper itself, not on CGdL.

Journal coordinates, independently: Crossref gives `Journal of the London Mathematical Society`,
volume 90, issue 1, pages 241–269, published 2014-06-03, DOI 10.1112/jlms/jdu026, authors
David W. Farmer, Steven M. Gonek, Yoonbok Lee; the OUP article page gives the same and the same
abstract.

Finally, the collision itself. A4's `paper.md` line 365, Corollary 4.4:

```
at the shipped matched values (N = 64, eps = 0.05, B1 = 84.71214548308222) these are 0.8050957
and 0.6101914
```

0.8050957 → 0.8051 at four decimals. FGL14's constant → 0.8051 at four decimals. They agree to
four decimals and are unrelated quantities (A4's is a finite-N LP corner of a bandwidth-one model;
FGL's is an asymptotic RH-conditional lower bound for the proportion of distinct zeros of ζ).

### Verdict — CONFIRMED

The report is right, and this is one of its claims that primary-source checking upholds. The
constant 0.8051 genuinely appears in Farmer–Gonek–Lee, it is a **published** result (JLMS 2014, not
in the 2008 arXiv preprint, where the same corollary reads 0.6544), and what it counts is:
**a lower bound, assuming RH, for the number of distinct zeros of the Riemann zeta-function with
ordinates in [0, T], as a proportion of N(T)** — i.e. N_d(T) > 0.8051 N(T) for all sufficiently
large T. The disambiguation sentence the executing session was told to write is warranted, and
should be written.

Two things the executing session must get right when it writes that sentence:

1. **Cite the published version, three authors, 2014.** Citing arXiv:0803.0425 for this constant
   would be a factual error: that preprint has two authors and the number 0.6544 there.
2. FGL's 0.8051 is **superseded**. FGL themselves note in the same paragraph that Bui–Heath-Brown
   get 0.84665 by a different method, and CGdL20 (already cited in A4) get 0.8477 on RH / 0.8486 on
   GRH. So the sentence should say "Farmer–Gonek–Lee's 2014 pair-correlation lower bound for the
   proportion of distinct zeros" rather than anything suggesting it is the current record.

### Suggested disambiguation sentence (drop-in)

```
The coincidence is numerical only: the constant 0.8051 also appears in the literature as
Farmer-Gonek-Lee's RH-conditional pair-correlation lower bound N_d(T) > 0.8051 N(T) for the
proportion of *distinct* zeros of zeta [FGL14, Cor. 1.4] (since superseded by 0.84665 in [BHB13]
and 0.8477 in [CGdL20]). The value 0.8050957 here is a finite-N LP corner of the bandwidth-one
model at N = 64, eps = 0.05, B1 = 84.71214548308222, and has no relation to it.
```

```
the Riemann xi-function*, J. London Math. Soc. (2) **90** (2014), no. 1, 241-269;
doi:10.1112/jlms/jdu026. (The two-author 2008 preprint arXiv:0803.0425 is an earlier version whose
Corollary 1.4 gives 0.6544, not 0.8051; cite the published version for this constant.)
```
