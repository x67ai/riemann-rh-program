--- BANDWIDTH ROLE ---
The bandwidth L = λ·log(T/2π), λ ≤ 1, is the support of the window φ (supp φ = [−L/2, L/2]), and it enters at four distinct load-bearing points. (1) Explicit-formula gate: W(f_k, f_l) uses the test k = f_k ∗ g̃_l with support ⊆ [−L, L], so ONLY prime powers n ≤ X = e^L = (T/2π)^λ appear on the prime side; "bandwidth one" (λ = 1) = Dirichlet polynomials of length T = pair-correlation test functions with Fourier support in [−1,1] in Montgomery's normalization. (2) The binding constraint λ ≤ 1 is Proposition 5.6's off-diagonal treatment: the second moment tr G̃² contains Σ_{n≠m} a_n a_m·(oscillating)/(log n − log m), which is NOT evaluated but only BOUNDED by the Montgomery–Vaughan generalized Hilbert inequality (Lemma 5.2, constant 3π/2, spacing δ_n⁻¹ ≤ 2n), giving |O1| ≪ L²X; the diagonal main term is TL³/6π, so the bound is admissible iff X ≪ TL, i.e. λ ≤ 1. For X ≫ T·log T the off-diagonal terms dominate and their EVALUATION would require Hardy–Littlewood prime-pair asymptotics Σ_m Λ(m)Λ(m+h) — equivalently Montgomery's pair-correlation conjecture for |α| > 1 (paper §7.5(a)). So it is a diagonal-only evaluation with MV absorbing the off-diagonal into the error; the arithmetic information beyond the diagonal is thrown away. (3) Dimension cap: the Gabor family at critical density has d = ⌊LT/2π⌋ = λ₁N members, so every rank/positive-index certificate is ≤ λ₁N (Prop 7.4): the method certifies nothing for λ ≤ 1/2 and its a priori ceiling is 100% only at λ = 1; combined with ‖Â‖²_F ≥ (tr Â)²/d the two-moment certificate is ≤ (2 − 1/λ₁)N. (4) Higher moments tr G̃^k are unconditionally available only in the Rudnick–Sarnak range kλ < 2, so at the operating point λ ≈ 1 only k = 1, 2 exist — precisely the two moments the rank–trace certificate consumes. The tail estimate (Prop 4.2) also carries a bandwidth factor X^{1/2} = T^{λ/2} (from e^{L|Im γ_ρ|}, |Im γ_ρ| < 1/2), harmless for λ ≤ 1 but fatal past λ = 2. Finally the PairCeiling development makes "bandwidth-one data" a formal notion: certificates (c₀, r) reading only the form-factor measure on [0,1] plus mean density plus integrality — and proves the 0.6818287 ceiling for ALL of them, so the 2/3 ↔ 0.6725 ↔ 0.6818 gap is intrinsic to bandwidth ≤ 1, not to the linear algebra.
--- LIMITATIONS ---
* No bearing on RH in either direction (paper §1.5): the certificate is a lower bound only; the inputs (functional equation, explicit formula, mean values of length-≤T Dirichlet polynomials) are insensitive to o(N) off-line zeros and are satisfied by Davenport–Heilbronn functions and Epstein zeta functions of class number > 1 for which RH-analogues are FALSE. Any route to RH itself must use an input that these counterexamples violate (Euler product/multiplicativity beyond L²-means).
* λ ≤ 1 is essential and provably expensive to pass: for X ≫ T log T the off-diagonal prime sums require Hardy–Littlewood-strength prime-pair information (≡ pair-correlation conjecture for α > 1). The method certifies nothing at all for λ ≤ 1/2 (Prop 7.4 cap: certificate ≤ (2−1/λ₁)N ≤ 0).
* The rank–trace certificate is provably tight (Lean TightMult.lemmaR_tight): given only tr, ‖·‖²_F, the block structure, and n₊, an on-line double zero and an off-line pair at depth → 0 are indistinguishable (both charge 4), so 'two thirds' cannot be pushed to 'all' by better linear algebra on the same two moments; the extremal configuration ((2/3)N orthonormal simples + (1/6)N doubles/deep pairs) realizes all constraints.
* Bandwidth-one ceiling (Lean PairCeiling, hypothesis EnclOK only): NO certificate (c₀, r) of the Theorem-B type, valid configuration-by-configuration against bandwidth-one data, certifies simple-zero proportion > 0.6818287 + 2.55·10⁻⁶(|r′(1)| + ∫|r″|) (r(1) = 0). So even the optimal-window 0.6725 is within 0.0094 of a hard wall; 0.70/0.80/0.90 need Fourier support ≈ 1.04/1.26/1.70.
* Higher unconditional moments are useless: available only for kλ < 2 (Rudnick–Sarnak), and for λ ∈ (1/2, 1) the only accessible extra moment (k = 3, λ < 2/3) does not improve the Christoffel-function bound; for λ ≤ 1/2 many moments exist but the dimension cap voids them. Even granting ALL moments (full Hardy–Littlewood, HL*(k₀, λ) for all k₀): proportion → 1 of simple zeros on the line, but 'RH itself is out of reach of the mechanism' — o(N) exceptional zeros remain invisible.
* Degree-one method: for an individual L-function of degree m the usable bandwidth is Λ < 1/m and the certificate 2 − 1/c with c = Λ/(1+Λ²/3) is vacuous for m ≥ 2 (GL(2): c = 6/13 < 1/2, nothing for any window).
* Slow convergence / no finite-T content: the certified ratio approaches its limit like 1 − O(1/L) = 1 − O(1/log T); numerically at all accessible heights (T ≤ 10⁶) the flat-window certificate is ≈ +0.40 to +0.42, well below 2/3·N — Theorems A–E are strictly T → ∞ statements; also the constants c(λ) are effective but the log log T/log T loss at λ = 1 is intrinsic to the endpoint.
* Orthonormalisation is blocked: the Gram matrix M of the Gabor family has ≍ (w/L)d tiny eigenvalues; controlling W on those directions would need bounds on P_X over windows of length 1/L, i.e. Lindelöf-type information — the argument deliberately works in non-orthonormal coordinates where only inertia (coordinate-free) survives.
* The zero-side second moment cannot be read termwise: a pair at depth |β−1/2| contributes ≍ X^{|2β−1|} to ‖Â‖²_F, so no unconditional termwise bound exists — forcing the prime-side detour and the loss of all off-diagonal zero-pair information.
--- EXTENSION HOOKS ---
+ Off-diagonal prime sums: Prop 5.6 BOUNDS Σ_{n≠m} a_n a_m(...)/(y_n − y_m) by Montgomery–Vaughan instead of evaluating it. Any partial evaluation (e.g. averaged Hardy–Littlewood for Λ-correlations, known on average à la Montgomery–Soundararajan/Goldston) feeding a two-moment certificate at effective λ slightly > 1 would break the 0.6818 ceiling; the paper quantifies the payoff curve (support 1.04 → 0.70).
+ The moment→count machinery is already laid out (§7.5 d-f): n₊ ≥ (1 − Λ_m(0))d via Christoffel functions of the moment sequence d⁻¹tr(G̃/ℓ₁)^k. Proving HL*(4, λ) (fourth moment = sine-kernel value 13/4, encoding Σ(Λ∗Λ)(m)(Λ∗Λ)(m+h) asymptotics for |h| ≤ X²/T) yields 13/18 ≈ 0.7222 unconditionally. This is the sharpest concrete conjecture-to-constant hook in the artifact.
+ Family averaging (Remark 7.2(iii)): Lemma 3.2 is linear in tr and ‖·‖²_F, so both commute with averaging over families; orthogonality of characters restores bandwidth Λ* = 1 for q-averaged Dirichlet families, promising 2/3 of zeros on the line on average over χ mod q with T as small as a power of log q. Missing ingredient is explicitly named: a Gevrey-class taper making the Prop 4.2 tail work at tiny T. Concrete, unstarted project.
+ Under RH the third trace is a theorem (triple correlation) and the cubic-weight LP gives N_d ≥ 0.85082; the same LP framework (weights in span{m, m², m³, 1_{m=1}}, Schur–Horn majorisation constraint) generalizes to any new trace input — a template for converting each new correlation theorem into a zero-count.
+ The ceiling theorem itself is an extension target: it covers certificates valid CONFIGURATION-BY-CONFIGURATION against bandwidth-one data. Certificates exploiting positivity constraints that the 256-periodic law violates but true zeta satisfies (e.g. F(α) ≥ 0 for |α| > 1 — used by Chirre–Gonçalves–de Laat under RH to reach 0.6792 — or lower bounds on F beyond [−1,1], or non-configurationwise/average validity) are explicitly OUTSIDE the ceiling's scope. Making any F(α)-positivity for |α| > 1 unconditional (for the complex-zero form factor of [BGSTB24]) would immediately re-open the SDP route.
+ Window optimization for ξ′ (and ξ^(j)): the quartic window 1 − (7/100)(2s)² − (51/200)(2s)⁴ is an ad hoc polynomial, not the solution of the ξ′-analogue of the variational problem (7.3); solving the corresponding Euler–Lagrange equation should push 0.86864 toward (but below) the RH-conditional 0.8825, and combining with Wu's 0.86957 on-line count is a nontrivial merge.
+ Sylvester/inertia beyond two moments: Prop 4.5's inequalities (s₁ ≥ 2n₊^θ(G̃) − N, N_d ≥ n₊^θ(G̃)) are configuration-free — they hold for ANY 1−ρ̄-symmetric locally finite multiset with log-density. Any new spectral lower bound on n₊ of a structured Hermitian matrix (operator-norm information, eigenvalue rigidity, band-matrix theory for the specific kernel Φ(τ_k − τ_l)-against-ν_X) plugs in directly without touching the arithmetic.
+ The Lean LinAlg core (von Neumann trace inequality, both directions of Sylvester inertia, rank–trace, Weyl — namespace RHLinalg, contributed to the ecosystem) plus the abstract SeamMult/Assembly layers are engineered so that a new zero-side inequality or a new prime-side asymptotic can be swapped in; the k_c-form of Lemma R (rank_trace_mult_k) is stated leak-free for arbitrary real weights m_j‖v_j‖², i.e. already accommodates non-integer 'effective multiplicities' from imperfect sampling — a hook for windows beyond critical density or multi-scale families.
+ Hybrid q-T uniformity (Remark 7.2(i)): H(1/(1+ϑ)) for q ≤ T^ϑ, positive for ϑ < √6/3 ≈ 0.816 — stated as unchecked; carrying out the uniform error terms would give the first unconditional positive-proportion critical-line result uniform in conductor by this method.
+ The 256-periodic extremal law itself (exact-rational LP over marked point configurations with marks in {1,2}) is a new combinatorial object: the LP dual certificates characterize exactly which linear functionals of bandwidth-one data are saturated; studying its structure (why p₀ = 0.68182868746, why near-CUE rows) may reveal which additional constraint (three-point positivity? Fourier-positivity of higher correlations?) is cheapest to add to move the ceiling.

==================== read:paper-limits ====================
--- BANDWIDTH ROLE ---
Bandwidth enters at exactly four load-bearing points. (1) Support of the window: supp φ = [-L/2, L/2], L = λl, so Weil's formula for k = f∗g̃ (supp ⊂ [-L,L]) sees only prime powers n ≤ X = e^L = (T/2π)^λ — the certificate reads Montgomery's F(α) only for |α| ≤ λ ≤ 1 (Remark 5.10 makes this identification exact: tr G̃² ↔ ∫_{-λ}^{λ}(λ-|α|)F(α)dα = λ+λ³/3). (2) The Montgomery-Vaughan step (Lemma 5.2 with δ_n⁻¹ ≤ 2n, giving off-diagonal O₁ ≪ L²X in Prop 5.6) is what makes the prime-side second moment unconditional, and it collapses for X ≫ Tl: beyond λ = 1 the off-diagonal requires Hardy-Littlewood prime-pair correlations, equivalently Montgomery's conjecture for α > 1 (§7.5(a)). λ = 1 itself is admissible only because every secondary term is o(main) even at X ≍ T (Remark 6.1). (3) Dimension: the test family has d = ⌊LT/2π⌋ = λ₁N+O(1) elements at the critical Gabor density h = 2π/L, and rank/positive-index certificates can never certify more than d points (Prop 7.4 Cap) — so the same bandwidth that limits the prime side also caps the certifiable proportion at λ₁·100%, and makes the certificate vacuous for λ ≤ 1/2. For higher-degree L-functions the forced normalized bandwidth Λ* = 1/degree puts GL(2) below the vacuity threshold (Remark 7.2). (4) The ceiling: the PairCeiling development shows that the bandwidth-one data itself (form-factor measure on [0,1], i.e. masses s_j at j/256 within 3·10⁻⁴⁰ of CUE, plus edge mass) is consistent with a genuine configuration law whose simple fraction is 0.68182868746..., so within bandwidth one no certificate of the paper's linear type can exceed ≈0.6818287 — the binding constraint is not the linear algebra (Lemma 3.2 is tight) nor the window (Montgomery-Taylor is optimal, Theorem D + [CCLM17]) but the bandwidth of the arithmetic input. Higher moments do not help within the bandwidth: tr G̃^k is computable only in the Rudnick-Sarnak range kλ < 2 (§7.5(e)).
--- LIMITATIONS ---
* Hard ceiling within the method's data class: no certificate (c₀,r) reading only bandwidth-one pair data (form-factor/pair-correlation with Fourier support in [-1,1] plus mean density plus multiplicity integrality), valid configuration-by-configuration, certifies simple-on-line proportion > 0.6818287 + 2.55·10⁻⁶·(|r′(1)|+∫|r″|) — because an explicit 256-periodic law of marked configurations realizes near-CUE pair data with simple fraction exactly 0.68182868746.... 2/3 is within 0.016 of this cap. IMPORTANT scope caveat: the ceiling as formalized rules out certificates that are (i) linear in the form factor, (ii) with weight r ∈ C¹, r′ of bounded variation (the error budget scales with |r′(1)|+∫|r″|, so wilder r escapes the stated bound only by blowing the error term), and (iii) valid against every configuration sharing the data (in particular against the law's average). It does NOT rule out: arguments using arithmetic information specific to ζ beyond the shared data (mollifiers/Levinson-Conrey machinery, moments of ζ, zero-density, amplification); higher-correlation (k≥3) inputs, which the 256-law was not built to match; bandwidth > 1; family averaging; nonlinear functionals of the spectral data; or, of course, anything about disproving RH.
* λ ≤ 1 is essential and not an artifact: for X ≫ Tl the off-diagonal prime sums need Hardy-Littlewood-strength prime-pair information (= Montgomery pair correlation for α > 1). The quantified cost of going further: proportions 0.70/0.80/0.90 need Fourier support ≈ 1.04/1.26/1.70 (Remark 1.1).
* Prop 7.4 (Cap): the whole architecture (rank/index of a d-dimensional compression) certifies at most d = λ₁N points; nothing for λ ≤ 1/2; hence 100% is the absolute ceiling even with all moments, and the method structurally 'does not distinguish two thirds from all' — it produces lower bounds only, insensitive to o(N) off-line zeros.
* The inputs (functional equation, explicit formula, mean values of length-≤T Dirichlet polynomials) are satisfied by Davenport-Heilbronn and Epstein zeta functions for which RH is FALSE (§1.5); therefore no refinement of these inputs alone can prove RH. The paper states flatly for the conditional moment program: 'RH itself is out of reach of the mechanism' (§7.5(f)) — even HL*(k₀,λ) for all k₀ gives only proportion 1 of simple on-line zeros (a density-one statement), not RH.
* Higher moments are unconditionally unavailable: tr G̃^k evaluable only for kλ < 2 (Rudnick-Sarnak); for λ ∈ (1/2,1) only k ≤ 3 (and only λ < 2/3), and odd moments do not improve the Christoffel bound; for λ ≤ 1/2 where many moments exist, the Cap makes them useless. So the two-trace certificate is provably the best unconditional one in this family.
* The linear algebra is exhausted: Lemma 3.2 is tight (lemmaR_tight, kernel-checked equality case), Prop 4.4 is sharp in all three parts at the Montgomery extremal configuration (2/3 simple + 1/6 doubles/deep pairs), and the window optimization is closed (Montgomery-Taylor optimal by [CCLM17 Cor 14]). No slack anywhere except the arithmetic input.
* Degree-one method: individual GL(2) L-functions give c = 6/13 < 1/2, i.e. literally nothing, whatever the window; positive output needs normalized bandwidth Λ* > 1/2 (Λ* > 3-√6 = 0.5505 for a nonvacuous on-line proportion at degree 1).
* Orthonormalizing the test family (to get genuine eigenvalue counts rather than inertia) is blocked: it would require control of P_X on intervals of length ≍ 1/log T, i.e. Lindelöf-type information (Remark 7.1(ii)).
* Effectivity is asymptotic-only: the certificate is strictly negative at all computationally accessible heights (Remark 5.9, §8 tables); convergence is 1-O(1/log X).
* In Lean, the ceiling theorems carry the one hypothesis EnclOK (the 256 enclosures, produced by external 70-digit interval arithmetic; certificate JSON not in the repository — only its sha256), so the 0.6818287 figure is machine-checked modulo that finite computation; the paper's Remark 1.1 rounds it to 0.68185 and does not prove it in the text.
--- EXTENSION HOOKS ---
+ The 0.0152 gap between 2/3 (resp. 0.67250 with the optimal window) and the 0.68182868746 cap is genuine room WITHIN bandwidth one: the paper's certificate is not extremal against the 256-law. Closing it means optimizing (c₀, r) against all bandwidth-one configurations — an infinite-dimensional LP whose dual is the law; the Lean stability machinery (Stability.lean/Ceiling.lean, configuration-free) is exactly the verification harness for any improved certificate.
+ Quantified bandwidth targets: pair-correlation input on support [0, 1.04] would already beat the cap (0.70); 1.26 → 0.80, 1.70 → 0.90. Any partial unconditional information beyond α = 1 (e.g. averaged Hardy-Littlewood, almost-all-h prime-pair asymptotics, or the α > 1 lower bounds of Goldston-Montgomery type) plugs directly into O₁ of Prop 5.6 — the sole obstruction is one off-diagonal bilinear form over prime pairs with |log(n/m)| small.
+ The fourth-moment lever: HL*(4,λ) (a single additive-correlation asymptotic Σ_m(Λ*Λ)(m)(Λ*Λ)(m+h), |h| ≤ X²/T) yields 13/18 = 0.7222 via the Christoffel function Λ₂(0;1) = 5/36 — a concrete, well-posed conjecture strictly weaker than full pair correlation with extended support. The general moment-to-count machine (§7.5(d): n₊/d ≥ 1-Λ_m(0), Chebyshev-Markov-Stieltjes) is set up and waiting for any new moment input; it also invites SDP/LP duality analysis (cf. + Family averaging (Remark 7.2(iii)): both tr and ‖·‖²_F are linear, so the whole certificate commutes with averaging over families; orthogonality of characters restores Λ* = 1 for {χ mod q}, promising 2/3 on-line/simple for almost all χ with T as small as a power of log q. Same lever could give GL(2) families (where individuals are hopeless) a positive proportion.
+ The rank-trace inequality is tight only for the listed invariants (tr, ‖·‖_F, block structure, tr P₁ ≤ s₁): lemmaR_tight is an invitation to add invariants. Candidates visible in the setup: the operator norm of Ĝ (an off-line pair of depth y contributes eigenvalues ≍ ±X^{|y|}... self-pair term ≍ X^{|2β-1|}, p.11 remark — any unconditional upper bound on λ_max(Ĝ) would penalize deep off-line pairs and split the 'flat charge 4'), localized/windowed traces (tr against several windows simultaneously = a matrix-valued moment problem), and traces against shifted grids (the pair blocks' individual traces are currently unconstrained).
+ The two levels of integrality (m-1)² ≥ 0 and (m-1)(m-2) ≥ 0 are used; §7.5(g) shows the cubic level (LP over span{m, m², m³, 1_{m=1}}) works under RH via Schur-Horn majorization. A systematic majorization calculus for higher integrality polynomials against available (or conjectured) traces is a mechanizable research direction — the Lean LinAlg layer (von Neumann, Sylvester, Weyl) is the reusable core.
+ ξ′ and beyond: the two-trace transfer to ξ′ (0.85838/0.92919 unconditional, formalized) suggests iterating to ξ^{(k)} (Farmer-Gonek program: proportions →1 as k→∞) and asking whether the rank-trace device transfers with quantitative gain per derivative; the quartic window v(s) = 1-(7/100)(2s)²-(51/200)(2s)⁴ shows window re-optimization is object-specific (the ThmD variational problem changes with the density J).
+ The Kreĭn-space reading (Appendix C.3, Bombieri [Bom00], Yoshida [Yos92]): W on the full space is positive iff RH; the paper extracts what TWO moments of ANY finite compression certify. The open structural question it isolates: what functional-analytic invariant of the compression sequence (beyond moments — e.g. the full spectral measure of G̃, its operator-norm growth, or the Toeplitz/Gabor structure of M^{-1/2}GM^{-1/2} conditional on Lindelöf) is both unconditionally computable from primes and sensitive to a single off-line zero. Remark 7.1(ii) pinpoints the exact analytic threshold: bounds for P_X on windows of length 1/log T.
+ The hybrid regime q ≤ T^ϑ (Remark 7.2(i)): claimed to give H(1/(1+ϑ)) uniformly (positive for ϑ < √6/3 ≈ 0.816) modulo checking uniformity of error terms — a low-risk verification project with new content (proportion results uniform in conductor).
+ The extremal-law construction itself (exact-rational LP over N-periodic marked configurations, interval-arithmetic enclosures, kernel-checked row certificates) is a reusable template for computing ceilings of OTHER certificate classes: adding a third-moment row set to the LP would compute the exact ceiling of the two-trace + triple-correlation method (conditionally relevant per §7.5(g)), quantifying precisely how much each new arithmetic input is worth before attempting to prove it.

==================== lean:ceiling ====================
--- BANDWIDTH ROLE ---
Bandwidth-one enters in three coordinated places. (1) In the certificate's VALUE functional v = c₀ + ∫₀¹ r(x)·x dx: the weight r is only defined/hypothesized on [0,1] and is paired against the density x dx — the GUE form-factor value on the band |α| ≤ 1, the only region where Montgomery-type unconditional information exists. (2) In the VALIDITY pairing c₀ + Σ_{j=1}^N (S j/N)·r(j/N) ≤ p: the certificate interrogates the configuration's form factor only at frequencies j/N ∈ (0,1] — nothing about S(j) for j > N (frequencies > 1) enters any hypothesis or conclusion, so the adversarial law is completely unconstrained above the band edge, and constrained to within 3·10⁻⁴⁰ of CUE strictly inside it. The closed-band edge j = N is deliberately left free (the LP puts a large atom of mass ≈ 0.826 there), which is what makes p₀ = 0.6818… < 1 achievable while remaining CUE-consistent in the open band. (3) In the parent method (PrimeSideA), the same limitation appears as the mollifier length cap: Setting.lam = λ ∈ (0,1], X = (T/2π)^λ; the Montgomery–Vaughan generalized Hilbert inequality controls mean values only for Dirichlet polynomials of length ≤ T, i.e., λ ≤ 1, and this is precisely why the paper's certificates factor through the band-[0,1] pairing that the ceiling then caps. The ceiling is thus the formal statement: the information content of bandwidth-one pair-correlation data (open-band form factor + edge mass + simple-point fraction, processed linearly) saturates at 0.68182868746….
--- LIMITATIONS ---
* The cap binds only the exact certificate format X: a scalar c₀ plus a LINEAR pairing of one weight r against the pair-correlation measure on [0,1], compared to the simple-point fraction. It is an impossibility theorem for this format, not for the analytic problem: approaches not factoring through this pairing (moments of ζ, zero-density, non-linear positivity, Levinson-type with different observables) are formally untouched.
* Higher bandwidth is outside scope by construction: no hypothesis constrains S(j) for j > 256 (frequencies > 1), and the value functional stops at 1. A certificate exploiting form-factor information on (1, Δ] (which would require new mean-value theorems for Dirichlet polynomials of length T^Δ, Δ > 1 — the known fundamental obstruction) simply does not satisfy the theorem's shape and is not bounded by it.
* Multi-linear / higher-correlation functionals are outside scope: the configuration class Y records ONLY the pair form factor S and the simple fraction p. Certificates pairing with triple correlations, k-level densities, or joint distributions would need a richer adversarial law matching those observables too; nothing here shows the same law (or any law) defeats them.
* The numeric cap is NOT uniform over certificates: the bound is p₀ + 2.5431316e-6·(|r′(1)| + ∫₀¹|r″|), degrading linearly in the certificate's second-variation mass; at fixed N = 256 a certificate with ∫|r″| ~ 10⁴ would only be capped near 0.707. Pinning rough certificates requires larger N, but the law (and possibly p₀(N)) must be re-derived by a new LP at that N — the dependence of the LP value on N is not formalized.
* Certificates with r(1) < 0 escape the signed cap and only receive the weaker unsigned bound with +0.82395317·|r(1)|; the headline number 0.6818287 requires r(1) ≥ 0 (signed theorem) or r(1) = 0 (decimal instance as stated in the CeilingLaw256 docstring).
* Regularity boundary of X: r must be genuinely C¹ on [0,1] (HasDerivAt everywhere on Icc, g continuous); r′ may fail differentiability only on a countable set and r″ must be interval-integrable. Certificates with corner points in r (jump in r′) or distributional components are outside the formal class (though approximable at the cost of ∫|r″| → large).
* Trust boundary: EnclOK — that the actual LP law's form factor lies in the 256 recorded enclosures — is a displayed hypothesis verified only by external 70-digit interval arithmetic on a JSON file identified by sha256 and 'available from the authors'; it is not in the repository and not replayable from the artifact alone. Likewise p₀ = 1 − a_N being the law's simple fraction, and the law being the LP OPTIMUM, are paper-side claims (p enters the Lean theorems as a free variable).
* One-sidedness: the formalization proves the ceiling (upper bound via one adversarial law) but NOT tightness — no formalized matching certificate/LP-dual showing 0.68182868746 is attained; nor any formalized statement that the paper's Theorem B/D certificates actually satisfy hypothesis (vi) at this law (that bridge is a paper-level remark; PairCeiling has no comparator trusted-statement file).
* The adversarial law uses marks in {1,2} only (simple or double points). This suffices for the ceiling (any admissible configuration works as an adversary), but means the ceiling does not quantify what certificates could say about higher multiplicities or about the distinct-zeros functional of Theorem C — those would need separate laws/LPs.

==================== lean:certificate ====================
--- BANDWIDTH ROLE ---
Bandwidth enters at exactly three places, and it is the binding constraint everywhere. (1) The test family: the taper phi(u) = rho((L/2 - |u|)/w) has support EXACTLY [-L/2, L/2] with L = lambda*log(T/2pi) (Defs.lean, Params.phi), so the explicit-formula prime side P_X involves Lambda(n)/sqrt(n) only for n <= X = e^L = (T/2pi)^lambda - a Dirichlet polynomial of length T^lambda. The standing assumption Params.Valid forces 0 < lambda <= 1, and all headline theorems run lambda < 1 then lambda -> 1^-. lambda <= 1 is what unconditional mean-value technology (the Montgomery-Vaughan generalized Hilbert inequality, Zeta23/MV/, feeding the second-moment computation of thm:traces) can control: the second moment is ||G-hat||_F^2 <= (kappa(lambda) + o(1))*N with kappa(lambda) = 1/lambda + lambda/3 (kfun, FinalMult.lean), where 1/lambda is the diagonal/delta term and lambda/3 the form-factor integral. The certificate constant is 2 - kappa(lambda), increasing in lambda on (0,1], so the bandwidth cap lambda <= 1 yields exactly kappa(1) = 4/3 and the constants 2/3 (Thm A/B) and 5/6 (Thm C). (2) The tail: theta_0 ~ X^{1/2} log(4T)/D0^2 with D0 = sqrt(T) (TailInputs), and the error field calE contains X log l/(T l) + T^{lambda/2 - 1} - both need lambda bounded (lambda < 2 suffices here; the moments are the real constraint). (3) The ceiling: PairCeiling formalizes that the limitation is not an artifact of this proof - EVERY bandwidth-one certificate (weight r supported on the form-factor variable in [0,1], i.e., correlation information up to Dirichlet-polynomial length T) certifies at most 0.6818287 + 2.55e-6*(|r'(1)| + int|r''|) of simple zeros against the exhibited 256-periodic law. Two structural corollaries worth recording: unconstrained in lambda, H(lambda) = 2 - 1/lambda - lambda/3 is maximized at lambda = sqrt(3) with value 2 - 2/sqrt(3) ~ 0.8453, and by AM-GM kappa(lambda) >= 2/sqrt(3) > 1 for ALL lambda > 0 - so even granting arbitrary-length mean-value theorems, this second-moment certificate can never certify proportion 1; bandwidth relaxation alone caps at ~0.845.
--- LIMITATIONS ---
* Provable saturation of the certificate (lemmaR_tight, TightMult.lean): the inequality 2c*tr(P+Q) - ||P+Q||_F^2 <= sum_j k_c(m_j) + c^2*b is an EQUALITY on configurations of orthonormal on-line atoms (integer m_j <= c) plus b pair-blocks of eigenvalue c. No further inequality consuming only (tr G-hat, ||G-hat||_F^2, integer atoms on vectors of norm <= 1, n_+(Q) <= p) can improve the constants; at c=2 an on-line double zero and an off-line pair at depth -> 0 have identical certificate price (4).
* Depth blindness: an off-line pair {rho, 1-conj(rho)} enters ONLY through the signature of its hyperbolic block 2m(xx^T - yy^T), i.e. n_+ <= 1, regardless of |beta - 1/2|. No norm information on off-line evaluation vectors is used or usable in this scheme; the method cannot localize or weigh off-line zeros, only bound the count p from above.
* Second-moment barrier independent of bandwidth: kappa(lambda) = 1/lambda + lambda/3 >= 2/sqrt(3) ~ 1.1547 > 1 for all lambda > 0, so the certified proportion 2 - kappa is < 0.8453 always; certifying proportion 1 (RH) via this trace/Frobenius certificate is impossible for ANY Dirichlet-polynomial length, because the lambda/3 form-factor term is contributed by the zeros themselves (near-CUE randomness), not by technique deficiencies.
* Bandwidth-one ceiling (PairCeiling): within lambda <= 1 the entire class of certificates of this shape is capped at 0.6818287 + 2.55e-6*(|r'(1)| + int|r''|) for the simple-zero proportion, so the headroom above Theorem D's 0.67250 is < 0.01; further progress within bandwidth one is nearly exhausted. Caveat: the ceiling instance carries the one displayed hypothesis EnclOK (256 integer enclosures produced by interval arithmetic OUTSIDE Lean, sha256-pinned); the analytic stability inequality itself is fully proved.
* Proportion-only, asymptotic-only output: all results are liminf/epsilon-forms over dyadic windows (T,2T]; the effective constants c(lambda), loglog corrections of the paper are deliberately NOT formalized (Statement.lean: 'The effective c(lambda) forms are not stated'). No individual-zero statement, no zero-density statement off the line beyond the p-count.
* The certificate cannot refute RH either: off-line pairs only decrease the left side of every inequality; there is no mechanism by which the certificate could certify the EXISTENCE of an off-line zero.
* The Cauchy-Schwarz (non-multiplicity) route saturates strictly lower (1/2 and 3/4 instead of 2/3 and 5/6): the rank bound r in rank_trace_ineq leaks c^2/4 per atom; the multiplicity-aware k_c-form is the exact repair, and it is exactly tight (see item 1) - the improvement chain rank -> multiplicity-aware ends here.
* The window variational problem is solved exactly (Montgomery-Taylor c1* = sqrt(2) tan(1/sqrt 2)/(1 + tan(1/sqrt 2)/sqrt 2)): no better taper/window exists within the one-window second-moment family; ThmD is the endpoint of that direction.

==================== lean:analytic ====================
--- BANDWIDTH ROLE ---
The bandwidth is λ = P.lam with L = λ·log(T/2π), X = e^L = (T/2π)^λ; the constraint is the field lam_le_one : P.lam ≤ 1 in Params.Valid (Defs.lean:196) and in every prime-side Facts structure (PrimeSideB.lean:359, ThmD/Traces.lean:39, ThmE/TracesChi.lean:77). It enters through three distinct mechanisms. (1) SUPPORT OF TEST FUNCTIONS: the explicit formula requires tsupport f,g ⊆ [−L/2, L/2], so the Weil test k = f⋆g̃ has support in [−L,L] and the prime side sees only prime powers n ≤ X = e^L (prime_summand_eq_zero, ExplicitFormula.lean:315) — the Dirichlet-polynomial length is exactly e^L. (2) WHERE λ ≤ 1 BITES ANALYTICALLY: the binding error is the off-diagonal prime-pair term 𝒪₁ of 𝓜[P_X,P_X], bounded via MV with min-gap weights δ_n = 1/(2n) by 16·C_MV·(∫Φ²)·Σ_{n≤X}Λ(n)² ≈ L·(X·L) = L²X (PPOffDiag.lean:40) — pure absolute values, zero cancellation extracted from Σ_{n≠m}Λ(n)Λ(m)/√(nm)·kernel(log n − log m). This must be o of the main term (TL/2π)(ℓ₁²+L²/3) ≈ Tl³; at λ = 1, L²X = l²·T/2π and the ratio is 1/l → 0 (barely); for any λ > 1, L²X = l²T^λ ≫ Tl³ and the whole second-moment computation collapses. Concretely the regime lemma requires X ≤ T (eventually_X_le_T, used at PrimeSideB.lean:479) and ℰ_T = w/L + (l²+X)log l/(Tl) + T^{λ/2−1} → 0 (calE_tendsto_zero needs lam_le_one, Main.lean:149) — the middle term diverges for λ > 1. No single lemma 'fails at the boundary' λ = 1 itself (the certificate takes λ → 1⁻ by continuity of cStar, cStar_continuousOn); the failure for λ > 1 is that the MV bound on 𝒪₁ and the ends/cross bounds are no longer o(main term). (3) THE CEILING: PairCeiling formalizes that this is not an artifact of the error analysis — the entire information content available at bandwidth ≤ 1 (equivalently, pair-correlation form factor known only on [−1,1]) caps every certificate of this shape at ≃ 0.68183 simple-zero proportion, 0.009 above the achieved 0.6725. The variational file quantifies the value of more bandwidth: cStar λ is increasing (2 − 1/c*_λ would reach 3/4 near λ ≈ 1.706 and → 1 only as λ → ∞), so every unit of bandwidth beyond 1 has an explicit payoff; obtaining it requires genuine prime-correlation input.
--- LIMITATIONS ---
* Formalized ceiling (PairCeiling): no certificate (c₀, r) of the Theorem-B/rank-trace type with bandwidth-one data — r ∈ C¹[0,1], r′ of bounded variation — valid against all admissible configurations can certify a simple-zero proportion above 0.6818287 + 2.55·10⁻⁶·(|r′(1)| + ∫|r″|); the obstruction is an explicit adversarial 256-periodic near-CUE form-factor law, kernel-checked. The method therefore provably cannot approach proportion 1 (RH-strength) without leaving the bandwidth-one certificate class.
* The zero side 'contains no arithmetic' (paper §4, formalized as ZeroConfig): all zero-side inequalities hold for arbitrary reflection-invariant locally finite multisets with N(t+1)−N(t) ≪ log t. This is a strength (modularity) and a fundamental weakness: any conclusion derivable in this framework must also hold for the adversarial law, so RH itself is out of reach of the abstract layer by construction; new arithmetic must enter through the PaperInputs boundary.
* Off-diagonal prime pairs are bounded in absolute value via MV (δ_n = 1/(2n)); pushing the polynomial length past X = T (λ > 1) would require asymptotics for shifted convolutions Σ Λ(n)Λ(n+h) (Hardy–Littlewood/additive divisor), which are unproven — this is the classical wall, and the formalization localizes it to exactly one lemma-chain (O1_bound → prop_PP → tr2).
* Lemma R is proved TIGHT (lemmaR_tight, ZeroSide/TightMult.lean): using only tr(P+Q), ‖P+Q‖_F², rank bounds and n₊ bounds, the rank-trace certificate cannot be improved. Escaping requires new spectral functionals of the Gram matrix (e.g. tr Ĝ³ / eigenvalue-distribution information), for which no prime-side evaluation exists at bandwidth 1 beyond the second moment.
* The explicit formula is formalized ONLY for C_c² test functions with support ⊆ [−L/2, L/2] (compact support is load-bearing: prime sum finite, EL truncation in the pole term). Gaussians, Schwartz functions, or the test functions of Li-coefficient/Weil-positivity computations with noncompact support are outside the proved class and would need a new limiting argument with tail control on the zero sum (ZeroSummability gives absolute convergence only via (1+|r|)^{-2} decay from compact support + C²).
* Results are proportional and dyadic (windows (T,2T], liminf ε-form): no information about individual zeros, no zero-density estimates off the line, no bound of the form N(T) − N₀(T) = o(N(T)); off-line zeros are only counted, never excluded.
* Theorem E is for FIXED primitive χ mod q > 1 (constants and T₀ depend on q; no q-uniformity is claimed), so no application to q-aspect problems as formalized.
* Deviation from the paper (documented, PrimeSideA.lean header): error constants C and thresholds T₀ may depend on λ (fixed-λ reading, sufficient because the endpoint is taken by λ → 1⁻ continuity); the paper's uniform-in-λ constants are not formalized.
* MV is proved with constant 13 (diagonal) / 26 (bilinear), not the optimal 3π/2 — harmless here (∃C form), but any application where the MV constant is load-bearing (e.g. explicit density estimates) would need the sharper constant.
* The variational optimality of v*_λ (that cStar λ = sup_v cFun λ v) is NOT formalized — only cFun λ v*_λ = cStar λ. The upper-bound half of the Montgomery–Taylor problem lives in the paper/PairCeiling, not in ThmD.
--- EXTENSION HOOKS ---
+ ZeroConfig + PaperInputs seam (Defs.lean, Hypotheses.lean): the cleanest plug-in point for new mathematics. Any new arithmetic input (e.g. a proven correlation estimate for Λ, a sixth-moment bound, a zero-density theorem) can be added as a new Prop field and consumed by the abstract assembly; conversely any theorem about abstract reflection-invariant configurations proved elsewhere instantiates immediately at ζ, Dirichlet L, ξ′ via the existing seams (zetaZeros, ThmE, XiPrime).
+ The EF machinery (WeilEF/: rectangle contour, good-heights pigeonhole, Landau partial fractions, ZeroSummability, plus the normalization chain ExplicitFormula.lean) is a reusable template for Weil-type explicit formulas of any completed L-function with a functional equation — already re-instantiated twice (Dirichlet, ξ′/ξ). Extending EF_lit from C_c² to a weighted/Schwartz class (adding a tail-control lemma to ZeroSummability) is the concrete prerequisite for formalizing Weil's positivity criterion or Li coefficients on this codebase; W(f,f) and the ν_X-integral identity are already defined and proved equal for compact support.
+ Pair correlation with larger support: the prime-side pipeline (Mform bilinear expansion, shear+Fubini in PP.lean, the 8-term MV decomposition in PPKernel/PPOffDiag) is exactly Montgomery's F(α) computation for |α| ≤ 1. A proven shifted-convolution input would replace O1_bound by a main term; the file boundaries (O1_bound → prop_PP field of Facts → tr2) make the required interface explicit: one EvBound field with X > T. The λ-dependence is otherwise generic — FactsD keeps λ symbolic throughout.
+ The variational layer (ThmD/Functional.lean, cRatio = λ₁a²/(b+λ₁²J), window-general FactsD): any new window enters through three moments (a, b, J); supports experimenting with other profiles (the quartic XiPrime window already demonstrates this), multi-parameter or complex-modulated windows. A genuinely two-variable certificate (using off-diagonal Gram data beyond ‖Ĝ‖_F²) would need a new functional here plus a new prime-side trace — the tightness theorem says this is the ONLY way forward within bandwidth 1.
+ LinAlg/RankTrace + TightMult: the Hermitian-matrix certificate layer (von Neumann, Sylvester inertia both directions, Weyl, posIndex calculus) is self-contained (namespace RHLinalg) and could host stronger certificates: higher-moment inequalities (tr Ĝ³), interlacing/majorization arguments, or block-resolved versions; lemmaR_tight precisely characterizes what invariants are exhausted.
+ PairCeiling/Stability.lean is a general-purpose tool for proving ceilings: for ANY proposed new certificate family, instantiating ceiling_stability against candidate adversarial laws gives a machine-checked upper bound on what the family can certify — a negative-space search instrument for method design. The EnclOK/decide/RowCert infrastructure (integer enclosures + kernel verification, external certificate with recorded sha256) is a reusable pattern for importing interval-arithmetic computations into Lean proofs.
+ The Gabor-Poisson lemma (Poisson.lean) is proved for arbitrary continuous even compactly supported windows with quadratic Fourier decay and arbitrary grid offset T: supports multi-grid systems, refined grids (h = 2π/L is what makes the frame tight — changing the density trades tr P against rank bounds), or vector-valued/多window Gabor frames, none of which are used by the paper.
+ MV/Spacing.lean already contains stronger unexploited lemmas (spacing_four σ=4, two_point) sufficient for third/fourth-moment off-diagonal analyses; the Preissmann–Lévêque eigen-identity route would formalize sharper MV constants (3π/2 or Preissmann's) if ever load-bearing.
+ XiPrime/ demonstrates the transfer-to-derivative pattern (Farmer–Gonek–Lee): the same rank-trace device applied to ξ^{(k)} for higher k, or to other differentiations/convolutions of ξ, is a mechanical extension; the coefficient-system files (XiPrime/Transfer) show how to re-derive the needed EF variant.
+ Theorem E's fixed-q formalization invites a q-uniform version (constants tracked in q): the EvBound/EventuallyAt error discipline used throughout makes the T-dependence explicit already; repeating it for q would formalize proportions on the critical line uniformly in conductor — relevant to Levinson-type results for families.
