## §1 Question 1 — does Theorem R1 say anything at height 0?

**What R1 needs.** Clause 2 of R1 (note §2, "domination clause") excludes a cone certificate at (t, y) under (D_{t,y}): 2[μ_y(t − s) + μ_y(t + s)] ≤ Ψ(s) + Ψ(−s) for all s ≥ 0, whose local content is 2/sin(πδ) ≤ Ψ(t) (note §0.4 D1); clause 3 (note §3) supplies (D_{t,y}) for ζ from a window minimum of Ψ over |s − t| ≤ 4, stated for t ≥ 28, with the gap condition Theorem G; the layer corollaries (note §6) need min Ψ > 2. The mechanism is that "a hypothetical orbit puts two zeros at height t, each carrying the depth weight μ_y(0) = 1/sin(πδ) … and the on-line zeros near t must cover both" (note §0.4 D1). On rung 3 the note transfers the theorem "verbatim with the degree-2 archimedean functional and Ψ_K" and states the layer as {1 − σ < (2 + o(1))/log(t√20/2π)} — "Stated, not proved" (note §8.1 rung 3).

**The real zero's version of (D).** For the two-point orbit at t = 0 the folded comparison of note §2's proof reads: share = 2∫_ℝ ŵμ_y = 4∫_0^∞ ŵ(s)μ_y(s)ds against 2∫_ℝ ŵΨ_K = 2∫_0^∞ ŵ(s)[Ψ_K(s) + Ψ_K(−s)]ds, so the sufficient domination condition is

  (D_{0,y})  2μ_y(s) ≤ Ψ_K(s) + Ψ_K(−s) for all s ≥ 0,  with local content 1/sin(πδ) ≤ Ψ_K(0)

— a factor 2 weaker than the complex orbit's 2/sin(πδ) ≤ Ψ(t), because the real orbit has two points instead of four.

**The numbers at D = −20 (`siegel_setup_run.log`, last block).** Ψ_K is computed from the 40 ζ-zeros and the 58 L(χ)-zeros (tails beyond 80 are below e^{−π·70}). **Ψ_K(0) = 1.210·10⁻³** (dominated by the first L(χ) zero: 1/cosh(π·2.3589) = 1.2·10⁻³); the maximum of Ψ_K on |s| ≤ 4 is 1.0014, attained at s = 2.360, the first L(χ) zero. So the on-line share 2∫ŵΨ_K near s = 0 is three orders of magnitude too small to dominate a real zero's share in any cone test, at every depth: **R1's mechanism does not apply at height 0, for a structural reason — there is no on-line density at height 0 to dominate with.** The first L(χ_D) zero sits at τ₁ ≈ 2.36 for |D| = 20 and, by the smooth count (T/2π)log(qT/2πe) ≈ 1 [shape recalled; comparison only], at τ₁ ≍ 1/log|D| for large |D|, so Ψ_K(0) ≍ Σ_τ 1/cosh(πτ) is O(1) at best and never ≥ 2 — clause 2 is vacuous at t = 0 on every rung of the ladder, not only at D = −20. ### §2.2 The pole cap — why one real zero is uncertifiable, and two are not

**Lemma C (pole cap).** For every w ∈ Σ_∞ and every y ∈ [0, ½): 2(ŵ ∗ μ_y)(0) ≤ 2ŵ(0), with equality only in the limit y → ½. *Proof.* w ≥ 0 gives |ŵ(ξ)| ≤ ŵ(0) for every ξ (note §0.1, first bullet); μ_y ≥ 0 with ∫μ_y = 1 (Lemma P(i)–(ii)); so (ŵ ∗ μ_y)(0) = ∫ŵ(s)μ_y(s)ds ≤ ŵ(0)∫μ_y = ŵ(0). As y → ½, μ_y → δ₀ (C2 line 16) and the share tends to 2ŵ(0). ∎ Consequently, for every cone element,

  margin(δ) = 2(ŵ ∗ μ_y)(0) − B_K(w) = −Arch_K(w) − 2∫μ_y(s)[ŵ(0) − ŵ(s)]ds ≤ −Arch_K(w),  Arch_K(w) := ∫ŵ(s)a_K(s)ds,

so **a cone certificate against a single real zero exists only if the archimedean part of the budget is negative for that element.** The pole term 2ŵ(0) — exactly the share a zero at s = 1 would have — is the ceiling of a real zero's share; the identity says the pole pays for one real zero and no more. That is Landau's argument in cone dress: in the note's own words, divided by 2 the identity for the de la Vallée Poussin element "is the classical partial-fraction identity Σ_ρ Re 1/(s − ρ) − Re ζ′/ζ(s) = Re[1/(s − 1) + 1/s] + …" (note §4, Remark after Lemma E), and a single real zero's term 1/(σ − β) never exceeds the pole's 1/(σ − 1). `[novelty: single-check]` for the cone-wide form (every element, every bandwidth); the mathematics is classical.

**The archimedean thresholds (`planted_real_zero_run.log` line "thresholds").** A_K(r) is even and increasing in |r| (Re ψ(x + iy) increases in |y| for fixed x > 0, as ∂_y Re ψ = −Im ψ′ = Σ_n 2(x + n)y/|x + n + iy|⁴ > 0), so a_K = μ_0 ∗ A_K is even and increasing in |s| with its minimum at s = 0 (checked on the grid for D = −20: "a_K minimal at s = 0.000"); and a_K(s; D) = a_K(s; 1) + log|D|/2π. Hence: **A_K ≥ 0 pointwise iff |D| ≥ D₀ := exp(2 log π − ψ(¼) − ψ(¾)) = 2003.75; a_K ≥ 0 pointwise iff |D| ≥ D₁ := exp(2 log π − ∫μ_0(r)[Re ψ(¼ + ir/2) + Re ψ(¾ + ir/2)]dr) = 500.94.** For |D| ≥ D₁, Arch_K(w) = ∫ŵa_K ≥ 0 for every w ∈ Σ_∞ (ŵ ≥ 0), and by Lemma C **no strip-positive cone certificate, at any bandwidth, excludes a real zero of ζ_K at any depth δ ∈ (0, ½].** For |D| < D₁ there are cone elements with Arch_K(w) < 0 (a narrow ŵ concentrated where a_K < 0), and whether they certify at a given δ is a computation. At D = −20: A_K(0) = −0.7332, a_K(0) = −0.5126, a_K < 0 exactly on |s| < 1.540 with negative mass 0.8129 (log line "D = -20: A_K(0) = …").
