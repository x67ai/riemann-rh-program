## The question, exactly
Let k = F_q and S a smooth geometrically connected curve over k (complete unless a step needs affine S; say which). Weil's proof of RH for S (the Castelnuovo–Severi / Hodge-index route on the surface S ×_k S) has two halves: **(Z) the zero-dimensional half** — intersection numbers of Frobenius graphs with the diagonal, (Γ_{Fr^n} · Δ) = N_n = #S(F_{q^n}); **(Q) the quadratic-form half** — the self-intersections (Δ·Δ) = 2 − 2g, (Γ_Fr · Γ_Fr) = q(2 − 2g), the fiber numbers, and the Castelnuovo–Severi inequality (D·D) ≤ 2d₁d₂ (Hodge index on the surface), which together force |N₁ − q − 1| ≤ 2g√q. Borger's Λ_S-formalism defines a second "square": by the premise of 0906.3146 §1.2 (p. 7, for Spec Z: "the space Spec Z ×_{Spec F₁} Spec Z must be defined to be the Witt space W*(Spec Z)"), S ×_{F₁^S} S := W_S*(S), the Witt space of the E-typical Witt vectors over S (E = the closed points). **Decide, on rung 1 and with proofs: which of (Z) and (Q) is expressed on W_S*(S) — in the host's own terms (its components, their scheme-theoretic intersections, their arithmetic degrees) — and which lives only on S ×_k S.** Scout O's prediction (HARVEST line 32): (Z) is expressible and (Q) is not. Record, for III.20's four items (doubled object; external generator; closed output condition; tower/rationality), which survive the passage from S ×_k S to W_S*(S).
