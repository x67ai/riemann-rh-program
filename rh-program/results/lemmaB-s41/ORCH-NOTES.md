**O9 — a one-block Chebyshev identity for S8 and why crude bounds do not close it [worked by the orchestrator; steps to be re-derived by any unit that uses it] (17:07 IST 2026-10-01).**
For ρ < ½ (p₁ = 1 + 1/(2ρ) > 2) and I = (X, 2X]: the identity log = Λ ∗ 1 on the free monoid gives
  θ_P(X, 2X] = Σ_{n∈I} log n − Σ_{p^k ≤ 2X/p₁} log p · N(X/p^k, 2X/p^k]
(the terms with p^k > 2X/p₁ vanish except k = 1, n = p ∈ I: a window (y, 2y] with 2y < p₁ holds no g-integer). The right side involves N on I itself — where the construction gives the ONE-SIDED bound N(I) ≥ ρX − E(X) − ½ for free — and N at scales ≤ 2X/p₁ < X, i.e. the PAST. So a lower bound for the g-primes of the next dyadic block needs only upper bounds for E on the past: this is the natural induction step, and it uses the never-undershooting property exactly where a Chebyshev argument needs a lower bound.
Main term: with the Mertens sum Σ_{p^k≤y} log p/p^k = log y + c₁ + o(1) (for the template c₁ = −1/ρ exactly, since ∫_1^y (1 − u^{−ρ})du/u = log y − (1 − y^{−ρ})/ρ), the main part is ρX·[log X − log(2X/p₁) − c₁] = X·(1 + ρ·log(p₁/2)) — of the right size (θ_P(X, 2X] ≈ X).
Error: −Σ_{p^k ≤ 2X/p₁} log p·ΔE(X/p^k, 2X/p^k]. Only an UPPER bound for E at the points 2X/p^k is needed (E ≥ −½ handles the other end). The crude bound E ≤ B gives an error ≤ B·ψ_P(2X/p₁) ≈ 4ρB·X/(1 + 2ρ), which beats the main term only if B ≲ 1/(4ρ) — false already at 10⁴ (B grows like log²). With the measured MEAN of E (≈ 0.06·log x at π/16) in place of the sup, the error is ≈ mean(E)·4ρX — below X at 10⁷ but growing like log X. So the identity is an exact balance between terms of size X·log X; the induction closes only if the bookkeeping carries the mean of E as part of the main term (a second-order Mertens statement), not as an error. **For U3:** this is the precise form in which "the feedback" enters a Chebyshev-type argument; the open step is a self-consistent bound for the weighted mean Σ_p log p·E(2X/p) of E over the g-primes of the past.
