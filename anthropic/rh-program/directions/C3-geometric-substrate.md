### FIRST DELIVERABLE — the Part B non-instantiability lemma (cheap, provable today per the sweep; referee-evaluable)

Adopted verbatim in substance from the wave-2 arithmetic-dynamics interface [V:arithmetic-dynamics scout first_interface] and the closure order ("commission the Part B DH non-instantiability lemma … as the slot's first deliverable — cheap, referee-evaluable now" [V:grossmann-sweep2.json revised_shortlist #1]).

**Definition (polarized Frobenius system).** Let S = (K, (Ω, ν), (|·|_ω)) be a proper adelic curve in the Chen–Moriwaki sense (arXiv:1903.10798; "proper" = the product formula holds) [V:arithmetic-dynamics scout; corpus q-13a]. A polarized Frobenius system on S is:
(i) a fibered square X with a nef adelic Q-line bundle L̄ in the Yuan–Zhang calculus (arXiv:2105.13587 v9 / Annals Studies 223);
(ii) a family of correspondences {Ψ_n}_{n≥1} on X with **Ψ_m ∘ Ψ_n = Ψ_mn for all m, n, and Ψ_p primitive exactly at the primes p**, whose degrees against the two fibers reproduce the degree/counting data of an L-series D(s) attached to S.

**LEMMA B (target statement).** The Davenport–Heilbronn function and the Epstein zeta functions of class number > 1 admit **no** polarized Frobenius system over any proper adelic curve. Mechanism: the composition law at every pair of primes, plus primitivity, forces the counting data to organize as an Euler product — equivalently forces −D′/D(s) = Σ Λ_D(n) n^(−s) with **Λ_D supported on prime powers and Λ_D(n) ≥ 0** (nonnegativity because the relevant degrees are intersection numbers of effective correspondences against a nef polarization, which the Yuan–Zhang calculus makes ≥ 0). DH provably violates BOTH halves: Λ_DH(6) = +1.9364 ≠ 0 (support off prime powers) and Λ_DH(12) = −0.7629 < 0 — both already computationally verified to high precision in this program (DH coefficients from a_n = (1, κ, −κ, −1, 0) mod 5, κ = 0.2840790438… derived as a surd to 41 digits; FE verified to 5.6e−40) [V:ccm-dh-filter.json experiment_summary]. Epstein, class number h > 1: the function is a SINGLE CM-point evaluation of an Eisenstein torus period — breaking the full class-group orbit is exactly what destroys Euler factorization (Hecke's formula; branch-native anatomy per the BZSV scout) [V:bzsv scout S1/key_objects]; the concrete coefficient witness Λ_E(n) < 0 for a specific class-number-2 form is NOT yet computed and is Numerics item N1.

**Proof obligations (the referee-evaluable skeleton, in order):**
1. Formalize "degrees against the two fibers reproduce the counting data of D(s)" (the one place the definition needs sharpening; the char-p model — degrees of graph-of-Frobenius powers on C × C reproducing point counts — is the template).
