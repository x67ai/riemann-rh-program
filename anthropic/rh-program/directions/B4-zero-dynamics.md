## Proposal (Phase 3, immutable)

### Thesis
The de Bruijn-Newman heat flow acts on the explicit formula as an exact Gaussian reweighting Lambda(n) -> Lambda(n)e^{-sigma*(log n/log T)(1 - log n/log T)} at the diffusive time scale t = 4*sigma/log^2 T, so the entire (formalized, instantiation-agnostic) rank-trace certificate machinery runs on H_t for every sigma, producing a one-parameter family of unconditional realness-and-simplicity theorems R(sigma) = 1 - J_+(sigma) (R(0) = 2/3) whose monotone zero-transport couplings convert the parent method's provable depth-blindness into the first depth-resolved unconditional constraints on off-line zeros of zeta.

### Mechanism
COORDINATES AND OBJECTS. H_t(z) = int_0^infty e^{tu^2} Phi(u) cos(zu) du, H_0(z) = (1/8) xi(1/2 + iz/2); zeta zero rho = beta + i gamma corresponds to z = 2 gamma - 2i(beta - 1/2), so depth delta = beta - 1/2 becomes height y = 2 delta off the real z-axis. Forward flow (t increasing) obeys dH/dt = -H'': real zeros repel (Dyson-type, velocity 2 sum' 1/(x_j - x_k)), complex conjugate pairs attract the axis and a pair at height y dies (lands on the axis) at time ~ y^2/2; backward flow reverses both. Two monotone transport maps: real zeros stay real FORWARD; complex zeros stay complex BACKWARD. Local spacing at height x is ~ 4 pi / log(x/4pi), so the intrinsic relaxation scale of the flow at height T is t ~ 1/log^2 T. THE DIFFUSIVE SCALING: set L = log(T/2pi) and t = +/- 4 sigma / L^2 with sigma >= 0 fixed as T -> infinity. THE MULTIPLIER (the engine): the heat flow multiplies the mode of frequency u (z-side) by e^{t u^2}. In gamma-coordinates (z = 2 gamma) the Riemann-Siegel term n oscillates at local frequency omega_n = theta'(gamma) - log n, and the flow multiplier relative to the carrier (n=1, omega_1 = theta'(gamma) = (1/2)log(gamma/2pi) ~ L/2) is m_n = e^{(t/4)(omega_n^2 - omega_1^2)} = e^{(t/4) log n (log n - L)} = e^{-/+ sigma alpha (1 - alpha)}, alpha = log n / L in [0,1] (minus for forward t = +4 sigma/L^2). Hence H_t has an explicit-formula density nu^{(t)} = mu_t + P_X^{(t)} whose 'von Mangoldt' coefficients are Lambda_t(n) = Lambda(n) e^{-sigma alpha(1-alpha)} (1 + o(1)) — the flow is EXACTLY a Gaussian-in-log reweighting of the prime side, trivial at alpha = 0 (density) and alpha = 1 (band edge), maximal at alpha = 1/2. Consistency checks: (i) at sigma -> infinity backward this reproduces quantitatively the Rodgers-Tao mechanism for Lambda >= 0 (fixed forward flow time would force F(alpha) -> 0 on (0,1), contradicting the unconditional Fejer mass 1/3); (ii) forward damping = rigidification matches Dyson repulsion toward the local lattice; backward amplification (e^{+2 sigma alpha(1-alpha)}) = clustering matches attraction/collisions. THE CERTIFICATE FAMILY: run the entire Zeta23 pipeline on H_t. Zero side is instantiation-agnostic (ZeroConfig needs only reflection symmetry + local finiteness + log-density — H_t qualifies for every t); Lemma R, inertia, Poisson sampling, tail bound apply verbatim (the strip widens to |y| <= 1 + O(sigma/L^2): harmless in the X^{1/2}-tail). Prime side: first moment ~ N (flow correction O(sigma) per window); second moment kappa_sigma(lambda) = 1/lambda_1 + (2/lambda^2) int_0^lambda (lambda - alpha) alpha e^{-2 sigma alpha(1-alpha)} d alpha (Montgomery-Vaughan handles the off-diagonal since |Lambda_t| <= e^{sigma/4} Lambda; bandwidth wall lambda <= 1 untouched). Output at lambda -> 1: real-simple proportion of H_t zeros >= R(sigma) = 1 - J_+(sigma), J_+(sigma) = 2 int_0^1 alpha(1-alpha) e^{-2 sigma alpha(1-alpha)} d alpha; distinct proportion >= 1 - J_+(sigma)/2; complex-pair count p(sigma) <= (J_+(sigma)/2) N. THE DEPTH DICTIONARY (what sigma buys that sigma = 0 provably lacks): the sigma = 0 certificate is depth-blind (formalized: an off-line pair enters only through its signature, at flat price 4, indistinguishable from an on-line double — lemmaR_tight). The flow converts depth to survival time: a zeta pair at depth delta = a/L survives forward until t ~ 2 delta^2, i.e. sigma_death ~ a^2/2. Combining the forward certificate's complex-pair bound with a per-pair survival lemma (M2) gives the DEPTH-RESOLVED unconditional bound #{zero pairs with beta - 1/2 >= a/log T, gamma in (T,2T]} <= (J_+(a^2/2)/2 + o(1)) N — a continuous one-parameter sharpening of the depth-blind p <= N/6, with values 1/6 at a -> 0, 0.0317N at a=3, 0.0041N at a=5, ~ 2N/a^4 for large a, obtained from the SAME bandwidth-one arithmetic re-read along the flow. ### First theorem
THEOREM (flow certificate at diffusive scale). Define H_t(z) = integral_0^infty e^{tu^2} Phi(u) cos(zu) du (standard de Bruijn-Newman normalization, H_0(z) = (1/8)xi(1/2 + iz/2)); for a dyadic window let L = log(T/2pi), and for fixed sigma >= 0 set t_T = 4*sigma/L^2. Then, unconditionally, for every sigma >= 0 and eps > 0, for all large T: N_t^{rs}(T,2T) >= (R(sigma) - eps) * N_t(T,2T), where R(sigma) = 1 - J_+(sigma), J_+(sigma) = 2*integral_0^1 alpha(1-alpha) e^{-2 sigma alpha(1-alpha)} d alpha. ### Failure modes

- Sign/normalization error in the multiplier: the entire design keys off the local-frequency computation m_n = e^{(t/4)((theta'-log n)^2 - theta'^2)} = e^{-sigma*alpha(1-alpha)}. The Gamma-factor chirp and the carrier renormalization were done at stationary-phase level; my attempted reconciliation with the recalled Polymath15 B_t exponent shape did not close (their exact exponents were garbled in memory). Cheap kill switch: numerics item (2) measures F_t(alpha)/F_0(alpha) directly at T ~ 10^3-10^4 and falsifies the formula in an afternoon.
- The effective explicit formula for H_t at the lambda -> 1 endpoint may carry Gamma-mixing/saddle errors that are not o(main): the flowed 'von Mangoldt' coefficients are only defined up to the effective-approximation error, and at bandwidth 1 the margins are thin (the parent paper's Remark 6.1 endpoint admissibility must be re-verified with the extra e^{sigma/4}-inflated constants). Mitigation: prove M1 first at fixed lambda < 1 (constants degrade smoothly: R(sigma,lambda) = 2 - 1/lambda - J(sigma,lambda)); the endpoint is polish, not substance.
- The per-pair survival lower bound (M2's dynamical lemma) may be false as stated because of interference: clusters of zeros could herd a deep complex zero to the axis faster than y^2/2. The crude velocity bound |dy/dt| <= C*(local log-density) is only marginally sufficient at diffusive depths, and the campaign's Part-I experience (no configuration-free visibility theorem) warns that configuration-free dynamical lemmas can be impossible. Fallback: state the depth-resolved bound for H_t complex-zero counts only (unconditional), and the zeta transfer under a mild local-density hypothesis.
- KKL-style results at fixed t > 0 (all but finitely many zeros of H_t real and simple) might already imply near-trivial versions of the forward statements at fixed t; if their methods secretly uniformize down to t ~ 1/log^2 T, the novelty of M1's forward branch shrinks to the explicit rate R(sigma). (Verified to my knowledge they do not uniformize — their arguments are asymptotic in x at fixed t — but this must be checked against Ki-Kim-Lee 2009 and successors.)
- The joint-sigma LP (M3) may show the flow rows are redundant against the 256-law-type adversaries (the flow-invariance of the edge atom at alpha = 1 is a warning sign), in which case the ceiling stays at ~0.6818 for zeta-proportions and the program's zeta-side payoff reduces to the depth-resolved bound and the equivalences. This outcome is still a publishable no-go extension (a 'flow-augmented ceiling'), but the headline shrinks.
- Novelty risk on the depth-resolved bound: Selberg-type critical-zone density bounds N(1/2 + a/log T, T) << N e^{-ca} (recalled, unverified constants) would beat J_+(a^2/2)/2 ~ 2N/a^4 for large a; the new content would then be confined to small/moderate a (where 1/6 -> continuous improvement lives), the pairing structure, and the joint simplicity statement. Requires a careful literature pass (Selberg 1946; Fujii; Goldston-Suriajaya narrow-box papers, incl. arXiv:2603.28104).
