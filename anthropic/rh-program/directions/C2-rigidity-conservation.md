## Proposal (Phase 3, immutable)

### Thesis
Read the explicit formula not as one quadratic form but as an infinite exact conservation-law system graded by bandwidth, whose prime side is a POSITIVE measure (Lambda(n) >= 0 — the input the entire L2/Weil-positivity industry provably never uses and Davenport-Heilbronn violates), and build the field around two invariants: the defect-cost function V(t,delta;L) of a termwise-positive "strip cone" (which already contains de la Vallee Poussin's zero-free region as one degenerate element), and the class-separation modulus of the signed exact system, where a single off-line zero costs total variation e^{delta L} that no admissible on-line configuration can pay — native single-defect sensitivity, not proportions.

### Mechanism
OBJECTS AND AXIOMS. An admissible datum is a pair (pi, A): pi = sum_n Lambda(n) n^{-1/2} (delta_{log n} + delta_{-log n}) a POSITIVE measure with Chebyshev growth pi([0,x]) <= C e^{x/2} (axiom P — this is the (S1) input), and A the archimedean functional A(g) = ghat(i/2)+ghat(-i/2) + Int ghat(tau) mu_inf(tau) dtau, mu_inf(tau) = (2pi)^{-1} Re psi(1/4+i tau/2) - (log pi)/2pi (axiom FE). An admissible configuration is a locally finite multiset Z in the closed strip |Im z| <= 1/2, invariant under z -> z-bar and z -> -z, written Z = sigma + D: sigma a positive integer-atomic measure on R (on-line part), D = {(x_j, y_j)} the defect multiset (off-line pairs, y_j in (0,1/2]); local density #{|Re gamma - x| <= 1} <= C1 log(3+|x|) (axiom RvM — for zeta a theorem; internally derivable from O(1)-bandwidth budget elements). The CONSERVATION SYSTEM: for every even g in C^2_c with supp g in [-L,L]: Sum_{gamma in Z} ghat(gamma) = A(g) - Int g d(pi). For zeta this is the Riemann-Weil formula, one exact sum rule per test function, filtered by bandwidth L; the field studies which (sigma, D) can satisfy the system of a given positive datum.

SECTOR I (cone sector — termwise-positive conservation). Kernel calculus: for 0 <= y <= 1/2 define mu_y := Fourier transform of cosh(yu)/cosh(u/2). Closed form (verified numerically to machine precision): mu_y(xi) = c * cos(pi y) cosh(pi xi) / (cosh(2 pi xi) + cos(2 pi y)) > 0 strictly for y < 1/2; mu_0 = sech kernel; mu_y -> delta_0 as y -> 1/2; muhat_y(u) = cosh(yu)/cosh(u/2), a positive-definite complete-monotone-type scale family. STRIP-POSITIVE CONE Sigma_L := { g = w / cosh(u/2) : w >= 0, what >= 0, supp w in [-L,L] } (the Boas-Kac/Cohn-Elkies cone divided by cosh — the division is exactly the pole weight 2cosh(u/2) of the explicit formula). For g in Sigma_L: Re ghat(x - iy) = (what * mu_y)(x) >= 0 at EVERY point of the strip. Hence the MASTER IDENTITY (budget conservation, all terms nonnegative): Int (what * mu_0) d(sigma) + 2 Sum_j (what * mu_{y_j})(x_j) + Int (w/cosh(u/2)) d(pi) = 2 what(0)... precisely = 2 Int w du + Int (what * mu_0)(tau) mu_inf(tau) dtau =: B(w). The archimedean-plus-pole budget B(w) is partitioned EXACTLY among on-line zeros, defects (weighted by the depth kernels mu_y, whose peak mu_y(0) blows up like (1-2y)^{-1} as the defect approaches the strip edge), and primes — with every share nonnegative because Lambda >= 0 and the cone is strip-positive. Termwise positivity of the zero side — the property Montgomery had only under RH and the 2026 paper replaced by linear algebra — holds here UNCONDITIONALLY, at ARBITRARY bandwidth (no mean-value theorem is used, so the Montgomery-Vaughan lambda <= 1 wall never binds). Verified degenerations: (a) w(u) = e^{-a|u|}(1+cos(tu))^2 is a cone element (what = sum of Poisson kernels >= 0) and IS de la Vallee Poussin's 3-4-1 argument — the classical zero-free region, the only known single-defect-sensitive unconditional machinery, is one point of this cone; (b) a Fejer element gives Landau's zero-detection inequality Sum_rho (sigma-beta)/|s-rho|^2 <= (1/2)log t + O(1). Central invariant #1: the DEFECT-COST FUNCTION V(t, y; L) := sup { 2(what * mu_y)(t) : w in cone, B(w) <= 1 }; the region {V > 1} is unconditionally zero-free with a CONFIGURATION-FREE proof (all interference is nonnegative — the transcript's interference obstruction is structurally evaded here, at the price of polynomial rather than exponential visibility). Scale analysis reproduces the classical region shape (contradiction requires (1-2delta)^{-1} >~ L + log t); the sharp open question the LP isolates: the marginal value of axiom FE, calibrated by Diamond-Montgomery-Vorhauer Beurling worlds which satisfy P but not FE and realize near-classical-ZFR-optimal zero configurations.

SECTOR II (signed sector — exact rigidity). Central invariant #2: the class-separation modulus W_L(C, C') := inf over (Z in C, Z' in C') of sup over normalized bandwidth-L tests g of |Sum_Z ghat - Sum_{Z'} ghat|. Rigidity mechanism, three gears: (1) TV obstruction: a defect at (t, y) forces the component 2cos(tu)cosh(yu) into the configuration transform on |u| <= L; any signed difference of admissible ON-LINE measures nu has sup_{|u|<=L} |nuhat(u)| <= ||nu||_TV(effective), so faking the defect costs variation >= e^{yL}/poly, while axiom RvM caps genuine on-line variation at C log t per unit height — exponential vs polynomial. (2) The cosh gain: modulated derivative-bumps f = i(B_L)' e^{-itu} with polynomial-edge B give defect contribution 2 delta^2 c(delta L)^2 with c(delta L) >= e^{delta L/2} (c/(delta L))^{k+1} (numerically verified growth law) — the gain the transcript's Theorem 2 discarded by using only c >= 1, which is why its threshold was sqrt(log t)/delta instead of the true delta^{-1} log log t. (3) Gevrey localization: order-alpha Gevrey tapers have complex-argument decay |ghat(r - iy)| <= e^{yL} exp(-c(L|r-t|)^{1/alpha}), so ANY off-line contamination at height-distance |x| >= C_alpha L^{alpha-1} is suppressed below 1 even at maximal depth y = 1/2 — shrinking the transcript's exponential exclusion zone e^{L/8} to a POLYNOMIAL zone. Together: exponential single-defect visibility in the exact data, under polynomially-local contamination hypotheses only.

COUPLING (the long game): one defect must simultaneously pay (i) its Sector-I budget share in every cone rule, (ii) its Sector-II TV cost in every signed rule, and (iii) its signature cost in the 2026 paper's second-order Gram system. The extremal configurations saturating each subsystem separately are provably different (Montgomery's 2/3+doubles extremal for the Gram system; DMV-type edge configurations for the cone), so the joint system is strictly stronger than each part; quantifying the joint constraint set is the field's organizing long-term problem, with RH = "total defect budget zero" as the limit statement, approached by exhausting cost lower bounds rather than by certifying Weil positivity.

(3) Against Carneiro-Milinovich-Ramos arXiv:2310.01913: they deploy the Cohn-Elkies cone CONDITIONALLY (under RH) against pair correlation; here the cone acts UNCONDITIONALLY against the first-order explicit formula, with the new cosh-division trick making the entire strip termwise positive — to my knowledge the strip-positive cone Sigma_L, the mu_y depth-kernel calculus, and the budget-partition identity have no antecedent. (4) Against the transcript's Part I (tx_031-035, tx_047-053): its Theorem 2 threshold C sqrt(log t)/delta improves to C delta^{-1}(log log t + log(1/delta)) (exponential improvement in the t-aspect, from the discarded cosh gain), its exclusion radius e^{L/8} improves to C L^{alpha-1} (Gevrey), and its deliverable upgrades from "one negative square of a form" to separation in the exact conservation data; its flat claim "no configuration-free visibility theorem exists" is bypassed in Sector I (configuration-free polynomial visibility) and refined in Sector II (exponential visibility with polynomial locality). ### Obstruction evasion

- **S1 (Davenport-Heilbronn / Epstein lookalikes satisfy FE + explicit formula + L2 means with RH false)** — The system's load-bearing axiom P is pointwise nonnegativity Lambda(n) >= 0 with Chebyshev growth. DH violates it doubly: its log-derivative coefficients are not nonnegative (it even has zeros in sigma > 1, so -l'/l is not analytic there), and their squares grow like x^{1+delta}; Epstein zetas of class number > 1 are sums of Hecke L-functions whose log-derivative coefficients change sign. L2 mean values cannot see these signs — this is precisely the multiplicativity-adjacent input beyond L2 means the spec demands, and every Sector-I inequality consumes it directly (the prime share of the budget must be nonnegative).
- **S2 (all density/proportion methods blind to o(N) exceptions)** — No deliverable is a proportion. Sector I outputs region-exclusions: a SINGLE zero in {V > 1} contradicts one conservation inequality (the classical zero-free region, contained as a degenerate element, is the existence proof that single-defect-killing machinery exists — it kills every zero in its region, not a fraction). Sector II outputs class-separations where ONE defect costs e^{delta L} against a polynomial payment capacity.
- **S3 (lemmaR_tight: on-line double indistinguishable from off-line pair in the certificate)** — First-order sum rules see depth continuously through the mu_y kernels: double-at-t vs pair-at-(t,delta) differ by 2ghat(t) - 2Re ghat(t - i delta), of size delta^2 e^{delta L(1-eps)} for edge-weighted tests. Numerically confirmed on 120 genuine zeta zeros: discrepancy 0.3 to 6x10^5 versus rest-of-configuration scale <= 10^-6 across L = 40..120, delta = 0.05..0.2. The first theorem's second clause makes this a proved separation exactly where the formalized no-go says the old data class fails.
- **S4 (every route's first substantive step is Weil positivity in disguise; Bombieri small-support vacuity)** — No unknown positivity is ever asserted. The generators are: (a) positivity of the arithmetic MEASURE pi (known, not conjectured); (b) positive-definiteness of the complete-monotone sech-scale family mu_y (proved, closed form); (c) exactness — identities plus a total-variation/exponential-type rigidity argument (functional-analytic generator). The master statement is a budget PARTITION, not a positivity certificate; its content scales with bandwidth because no mean-value theorem caps L, unlike Bombieri's fixed-small-support regime.
- **S5a (Alternative Hypothesis world)** — AH concerns spacing statistics of on-line zeros; the system is first-order (one-point plus complex-evaluation data) and spacing-blind — AH-worlds are not counterexamples to any statement made.
- **S5b (Bombieri-Garrett ~94% spectral cap)** — No pseudo-Laplacian, no spectral realization is constructed; zeros are never eigenvalues of anything here.
- **S5c (parity problem)** — No sieve: primes enter as INPUT at Chebyshev strength through a positive measure; nothing prime-detecting is attempted.
- **S5d (Conrey-Li de Branges counterexamples)** — No de Branges space positivity is assumed; the sech-scale kernels' positivity is unconditional and explicit.
- **S5e (Rodgers-Tao Lambda >= 0, no sub-critical margin)** — No heat flow or smoothing that would need margin below criticality; dBN Lambda >= 0 is consistent with — indeed reads as — the system being exactly critically rigid.
- **S5f (formalized bandwidth/two-moment ceilings; MV lambda <= 1 wall; dimension cap)** — Those ceilings are theorems about certificates reading bandwidth-one form-factor data configuration-by-configuration; this system reads first-order sum rules plus L1/pointwise positivity, uses no Montgomery-Vaughan step (so bandwidth is unbounded), and outputs non-proportion statements — outside the ceiling class by the ceiling's own formalized scope note (full-map.md line 122).

### First theorem
GEVREY-LOCALIZED SINGLE-DEFECT SEPARATION THEOREM (with tight-pair corollary). Statement: Fix alpha in (1,2]. There are absolute constants C0, C1, c0 such that for all t >= t0, 0 < delta <= 1/2, L >= C0 delta^{-1}(log log t + log(1/delta)), R := C0 L^{alpha-1}: let Z, Z' be any two multisets in the closed strip {|Im z| <= 1/2}, each invariant under conjugation and reflection, each with local density #{gamma : |Re gamma - x| <= 1} <= C1 log(3+|x|), such that within the window |Re gamma - t| <= R all points of Z' are real, while Z contains the orbit {±t ± i delta} and is otherwise real in the window; outside the window both are arbitrary admissible. Then there is an explicitly constructed even test g (order-alpha Gevrey taper, modulated derivative-bump, supp g in [-L, L], all constants explicit) with |Sum_{Z} ghat(gamma) - Sum_{Z'} ghat(gamma)| >= delta^2 e^{(1-c0) delta L} >= 1: bandwidth-L sum-rule data separates the two classes. COROLLARY (lemmaR_tight separation): the same conclusion with Z' containing instead an on-line DOUBLE at t — the configuration pair that the formalized tightness theorem proves indistinguishable to every (tr, Frobenius-norm, n_+, integrality) certificate is separated by the first-order system at explicit bandwidth. STATUS OF INPUTS — all proven or provably within reach: explicit formula for compactly supported C^2 tests (formalized in Zeta23/WeilEF); Gevrey-alpha window construction with decay |ghat(r-iy)| <= e^{yL} exp(-c(L|r-t|)^{1/alpha}) (standard Denjoy-Carleman/Gevrey theory; the 2026 paper itself names Gevrey tapers as a known-to-exist needed ingredient for its family-averaging hook); cosh-gain bound c(delta L) >= e^{delta L/2}(c/(delta L))^{k+1} for polynomial-edge bumps (elementary; numerically verified); RvM-type local density (theorem for zeta; axiom in the abstract class). ATTACK SKETCH: run the transcript's Theorem-2 computation (tx_032) with four upgrades: keep the cosh gain (their worst case set it to 1); replace the C^3 bump by the Gevrey taper; bound in-window on-line contributions of BOTH configurations by n1 b1/L^2 + C log t/L^6 as they did; kill out-window contamination of ANY depth by e^{L/2} exp(-c(L|x|)^{1/alpha}) summed against log-density — convergent and < 1 for |x| >= C0 L^{alpha-1}. Balance: delta^2 e^{delta L} (delta L)^{-2k-2} > C log t/L^2 holds exactly when L >= C0 delta^{-1}(log log t + log(1/delta)). Each step is a short self-contained lemma a referee can check; nothing conjectural; not a restatement of any named conjecture (it strictly improves an unpublished transcript result on two axes and adds the tight-pair separation).

### Failure modes

- Prior-art risk (highest): the Sector-I LP may substantially exist in the Fourier-optimization zero-free-region literature (Carneiro school, Mossinghoff-Trudgian-Yang, Kadiri) — I could not verify from this environment; mitigation: novelty then rests on the strip-positive cone + mu_y calculus + Sector II + coupling, which have no plausible antecedent.
- Sector-I saturation: the cone ceiling may be EXACTLY the classical zero-free region (DMV Beurling calibration suggests the FE axiom's marginal value could be small); outcome degrades to an optimality/no-go theorem — publishable but not progress into the strip interior.
- Sector-II scope: separation theorems are configuration-CLASS statements; applying them to zeta itself requires contamination hypotheses about zeta's own off-line zeros (zero-density theorems give only height-averaged, not per-height, control), so the path from separation to exclusion for zeta stays conditional at first.
- The detector layer (prime-side computation of the discriminating sum rules) requires pointwise short-Dirichlet-polynomial control that may be provably out of reach (almost-periodicity lower bounds); graceful degradation is t-averaging, which collapses back to known density-type territory.
- The Gevrey tail analysis under the e^{yL} strip weight could hit an X^{1/2} wall analogous to Prop 4.2's; believed avoidable because the first theorem needs only zero-side estimates (no prime-side tail), but the constants must be carried honestly.
- The coupling milestone (M5) may show the first-order rules add zero binding constraints at the Montgomery extremal configuration, in which case the field improves nothing quantitative in the proportion regime and its value is confined to the single-defect regime.
