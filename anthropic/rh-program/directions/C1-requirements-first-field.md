## Proposal (Phase 3, immutable)

### Thesis
Replace critical-line Weil positivity by its tilted foliation: RH is the statement that the Lean-verified positivity u = Re xi'/xi > 0 (equivalently reflection-positivity of the de Branges tilt kernels K_a, whose Krein-Langer negative squares count off-line zeros with multiplicity) propagates from the proven ray a >= 1 down every level a > 1/2 — a continuation problem for an Euler-product-driven, single-zero-sensitive, multiplicity-exact positivity, organized around the anchoring geometry of the negativity body and driven by two new generators: indefinite (Pontryagin) reflection positivity for the tilt flow and complete monotonicity of the prime side in the depth direction.

### Novelty
Relative to Lagarias, 'On a positivity property of the Riemann xi-function' (Acta Arith. 89, 1999 — flagged for verification): he proved the two endpoint facts (unconditional positivity on sigma >= 1; RH iff positivity on sigma > 1/2) but built nothing between them — no index ladder, no anchoring geometry of {u <= 0}, no localization, no RP/CM machinery, no prime-computable certificates. Relative to de Branges and Conrey-Li (IMRN 2000): Conrey-Li falsified de Branges' sufficient conditions AT criticality; the tilt family lives strictly at a > 1/2, needs no de Branges axioms, and uses only the unconditional Krein-Langer index count — the campaign's tx_048 recorded the family and its multiplicity-visibility, then dropped it because compressions were not prime-computable and the negative index gave only the wrong-direction bound; the present design inverts the use: not index-counting compressions but POSITIVITY-LADDER plus the windowed tilted explicit formula, which IS prime-computable (finite Lambda-sums, X ~ e^{2pi/w}). Relative to Connes-Consani (Selecta 2021; arXiv:2310.18423) and the whole Weil-positivity front: they attack a = 1/2 with zero margin; here Weil positivity is exhibited as the degenerate boundary derivative of the tilt flow (R4 identity) and never used — RH is foliated into strictly-weaker gapped statements with a Lean-verified foothold. Relative to Suzuki arXiv:2301.00421 (Weil space is a de Branges space under RH): unconditional and graded rather than conditional and critical. Relative to the Zeta23 paper and its formalized no-gos: the machinery reads a data coordinate (depth/sigma-profiles constrained by complete monotonicity, plus t-local positivity) that the bandwidth-one certificate model does not even parametrize, so PairCeiling, lemmaR_tight and the dimension cap do not apply — and unlike the two-thirds certificate it separates zeta from DH/Epstein at its first proven rung. Relative to classical zero-free-region technology (dlVP -> Vinogradov-Korobov -> Ford): ZFR proofs produce non-vanishing; this field upgrades non-vanishing to POSITIVITY of one harmonic function (an open, stable, composable, certifiable condition), proves the two interconvertible with effective collars (the first theorem), and equips the lineage — the only known machinery satisfying S1+S2 — with new generators (OS_kappa reflection positivity, depth-CM rigidity, Pontryagin defect bookkeeping) it has never had. To my knowledge no publication studies the geometry of {Re xi'/xi <= 0} as an object, no OS-type axiom system with tilted-line reflections and FE as boundary case exists, and no one has used total monotonicity in the depth direction as a constraint transverse to bandwidth.

### Obstruction evasion

- **Davenport-Heilbronn / Epstein (h>1) RH-false lookalikes satisfying FE + explicit formula + L2 means (S1)** — The machinery's input is Lambda(n) >= 0 at every prime power (log-derivative coefficient positivity = full Euler product), consumed through the first rung (non-vanishing on the 1-line via 3-4-1 trigonometric positivity) and through the depth-direction complete-monotonicity constraints. DH and Epstein (class number > 1) violate Lambda >= 0, and both classically have zeros with beta > 1 (Davenport-Heilbronn 1936; flagged classical), so they FAIL THE FIRST RUNG of the ladder: their analogue of 'Re xi'/xi > 0 on sigma >= 1' is already false. No proportion-type method separates zeta from the lookalikes at its first proven instance; this one does.
- **o(N)-blindness of all density/proportion/moment-ladder methods (S2)** — The target is emptiness of the negativity body N = {sigma > 1/2 : Re xi'/xi <= 0}, equivalently kappa_a = 0 for all a > 1/2 — never a proportion. A single off-line zero rho0 = beta0 + i*gamma0 of any multiplicity forces u(s) -> -infinity as s -> rho0 from the left (term m0(sigma-beta0)/|s-rho0|^2), opening an open plume in N and charging kappa_a >= 1 for every a in (1/2, beta0). The ladder's top rung IS 'every single zero on the line'.
- **Multiplicity-blindness of the Weil-form certificate: on-line double indistinguishable from off-line pair (lemmaR_tight) (S3)** — In the tilt field an on-line zero of multiplicity m is a positivity SOURCE of strength m(sigma-1/2)/|s-rho|^2 (numerically verified: u(sigma, gamma_1) = 1/(sigma-1/2)), while kappa_a charges only zeros with beta > a, WITH multiplicity (Krein-Langer negative squares of K_a — precisely the tx_048 residue: 'multiplicity counted, unlike the Weil form'). The lemmaR_tight blind spot (double vs pair of depth d -> 0) is resolved layer by layer: TRP(a) for a in (1/2, 1/2+d) sees the pair and never charges the double.
- **'Every route's first substantive step is Weil positivity in disguise' + Bombieri's small-support triviality (S4)** — TRP(a) for a > 1/2 is NOT a Weil-positivity cone statement: it is quasi-RH(a) (zero-free half-plane), strictly weaker than RH for every a > 1/2, with genuine unknown content at every a < 1 — unlike small-support Weil positivity (unconditional and empty) the ladder has real intermediate rungs. Weil positivity appears only as the degenerate boundary derivative at a = 1/2 (R4 identity: d/da log|xi(a+it)/xi(1-a+it)| at a=1/2 equals 2*pi*nu_X), which the program never touches. The added positivity generators are on the sanctioned analytic list: reflection positivity (OS-Schwarz with Pontryagin-index bookkeeping for the tilted reflection theta_a) and complete monotonicity (finite totally-monotone exponential prime sums from Lambda >= 0), the first verified for zeta at the a >= 1 rung (Lean), the second verified by construction.
- **Alternative Hypothesis world (S5)** — TRP consumes no spacing statistics whatsoever. An AH world with all zeros on the line satisfies TRP at every level; the machinery is orthogonal to AH and never needs to refute it — its data coordinate is depth (sigma-profiles), not gaps.
- **Bombieri-Garrett ~94% spectral cap (S5)** — No pseudo-Laplacian and no realization of zeros as discrete spectrum of a self-adjoint operator. The OS reconstruction yields the Poisson semigroup e^{-x|D_t|} on positive harmonic boundary data, with off-line zeros appearing as Pontryagin DEFECTS (negative squares of K_a), which no spectral-fraction cap constrains.
- **Conrey-Li counterexamples to de Branges positivity (S5)** — Conrey-Li kill de Branges' sufficient positivity conditions for the space AT criticality a = 1/2. The tilt family works strictly at a > 1/2, never invokes de Branges' axioms, and uses only the Krein-Langer index theorem (an unconditional theorem about generalized Schur functions, since Theta_a is unimodular on R by the FE). The degenerate critical kernel K_{1/2} is never used.
- **Rodgers-Tao Lambda_dBN >= 0: no sub-critical margin (S5)** — The tilt flow is in the sigma-direction, not de Bruijn-Newman heat-flow time; Rodgers-Tao does not constrain it. Margin is built in at every a > 1/2 (quasi-RH(a) is an open condition, numerically margin ~ 0.046(a-1/2) in the verified range) and criticality is approached only through the ladder — no sub-critical smoothing is ever invoked, respecting the 'barely true' lesson.
- **Formalized bandwidth-one ceiling (0.6818287), two-moment tightness, dimension cap (S5)** — Those cap certificates that read the t-frequency form factor with Fourier support <= 1 configuration-by-configuration. TRP certificates read a coordinate that model does not parametrize: sigma-profiles (depth data, constrained by complete monotonicity in a) and t-LOCAL pointwise positivity in width-O(1) windows. The 256-law configuration has all mass on the line, hence trivially satisfies every TRP(a) — it cannot calibrate or cap this method. The honest analogue calibrator (a 'tilted law' planting off-line zeros while matching all prime-computable tilted-EF data across a-levels) does not exist yet; building it is milestone 3 and would be the field's own stress test.
- **Parity problem (S5)** — Not a sieve: the mechanism consumes Lambda >= 0 jointly with analytic continuation and the functional equation through the harmonic function u = Re xi'/xi; parity constrains combinatorial sieve identities, not positivity theory of the completed zeta.
- **Degree obstruction (two-thirds method empty for GL(2)) — bonus** — The tilt ladder is degree-blind: E_a(z) = Lambda_F(a - iz) and kappa_a make sense for any completed L-function, and the first rung (positivity on sigma >= sigma_1(F)) survives with the same Euler-positivity proof; there is no Montgomery-Vaughan bandwidth step anywhere, hence no Lambda* = 1/degree wall.

### First theorem
THEOREM (Effective anchoring and transfer for the negativity body). There exist effective absolute constants C1, C2 such that for every delta in (0, 1/4] and every s0 = sigma0 + i*t0 with 1/2 + delta <= sigma0 <= 2 and |t0| >= 30: if u(s0) := Re xi'/xi(s0) <= 0, then zeta has a zero rho = beta + i*gamma with beta >= sigma0 and |rho - s0| <= C1/delta. Corollaries: (T1) Transfer: for any zero-free region {sigma > 1 - eta(t)} known effectively, u > 0 on {sigma > 1 - eta(t) + C1/(sigma-1/2)-collar}; instantiated with Ford's effective Vinogradov-Korobov this extends the Lean-formalized rung 'Re xi'/xi > 0 on Re s >= 1' strictly below sigma = 1 for the first time, to sigma >= 1 - c/((log|t|)^{2/3}(loglog|t|)^{1/3}). (T2) Localization: quasi-RH(a) up to height T is equivalent, up to C1/(a-1/2) edge collars, to certified positivity of the single harmonic function u on the segment {sigma = a, |t| <= T} — reducing zero-free boxes to interval-arithmetic positivity certificates in the repo's EnclOK pattern. STATUS OF INPUTS — all proven: (1) the Lagarias zero-sum identity u(s) = Sigma_rho m_rho (sigma-beta)/|s-rho|^2 (Hadamard factorization; already partially formalized as sum_mult_one_sub_re in Zeta23/XiPrime/Hardy/ZeroFree.lean); (2) Riemann-von Mangoldt with an effective S(t) bound (Trudgian-type constants — flagged for exact values), giving >= c0 log t0 zeros in any window of length H0 ~ 1.5 around t0; (3) Stirling. SKETCH: group zeros in FE-pairs {rho, 1-rho-bar}; any pair whose deeper member has beta <= sigma0 contributes two nonnegative terms, and pairs within |gamma - t0| <= H0 contribute >= c*delta*log t0 in total; if no zero with beta >= sigma0 lies within distance R = C1/delta, the only negative terms come from zeros with beta > sigma0 at distance > R, bounded in total by C2*log(t0)/R; choosing C1 = 2*C2/c forces u(s0) > 0, contradiction. Dichotomy closes with all constants explicit. This is a referee-evaluable, self-contained statement — new (nearest relatives: qualitative remarks in Lagarias 1999 and quantitative Speiser via Levinson-Montgomery, which concern zeta' not the geometry of {Re xi'/xi <= 0}) — and it is the cornerstone the whole field builds on, since it makes positivity regions and zero-free regions interconvertible with effective collars. Estimated probability it goes through essentially as sketched: ~0.55 within months; the design-level confidence that TRP is the right requirements-first universe given S1-S5 is higher (~0.8), while the field's long-horizon wager (a nontrivial margin-propagation inequality within 3 years) is ~0.1.

### Failure modes

- Margin propagation (milestone 4) may be exactly as hard as widening classical zero-free regions: the archimedean drift in the a-flow costs ~ (1/2)log t per unit of descent, so descending from a=1 to a=1/2 needs pointwise prime-side forcing of size log t — Lindelof-adjacent. The field could mature into an elegant RP-reformulation of Vinogradov-Korobov without passing it. Mitigation: milestone 4 has an explicit falsifiable success criterion, and the reformulation itself yields spin-offs (localization, GL(m) uniformity).
- The OS/RP layer could be decorative: the reconstructed semigroup is the Poisson semigroup, and OS-Schwarz might yield nothing beyond classical harmonic-function estimates (Hadamard + Borel-Caratheodory). Early test: milestone 2 must produce at least one inequality not derivable classically, else the layer is demoted and the field stands on anchoring + CM alone.
- Depth-CM constraints may lack detection power: negativity plumes are thin (width ~ 1/((beta-a) log gamma)), and the depth signal of rare off-line zeros may drown in the on-line ambient (~log T zeros per unit window) — the same visibility/interference wall that emptied the negative-index compression route. Localization in t mitigates (one zero dominates near its own plume), but prime-computable windows of width 1 may not resolve plume-scale sigma-structure at height T without sums of length T^c. The milestone-3 LP experiment quantifies this early and cheaply.
- Recalled literature could fail verification: (i) Lagarias, Acta Arith. 89 (1999) 'On a positivity property of the Riemann xi-function' — statement Re xi'/xi > 0 on Re s >= 1 unconditionally and RH iff positivity on Re s > 1/2; (ii) Lagarias (~2005-06) Hermite-Biehler property of xi(a - iz) for a >= 1; (iii) Trudgian's explicit |S(t)| <= 0.112 log t + 0.278 loglog t + 3.385; (iv) Krein-Langer factorization applicability (needs Theta_a of bounded type in C+; should follow from xi having order 1, must be checked); (v) Davenport-Heilbronn 1936 zeros in sigma > 1 for DH and Epstein h>1. All flagged; none is load-bearing for the field's DESIGN but (i),(iii) are load-bearing for the first theorem's constants.
- The anchoring constants may degrade near small |t_0| or sigma_0 near the edge of the strip; handleable technically (verified-computation region covers small heights) but could make the transfer theorem's collars ugly.
- The milestone-3 calibrator could reveal a genuine no-go: an RH-false zero-configuration matching ALL prime-computable tilted-EF data at every a-level (a depth-direction analogue of the 256-law). That would bound the field the way PairCeiling bounds bandwidth methods. Building the calibrator early is the honest stress test — and even that negative result would be a publishable, formalizable theorem delimiting exactly which depth data any future RH machinery must consume.
