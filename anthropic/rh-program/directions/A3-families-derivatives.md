## Proposal (Phase 3, immutable)

### Thesis
Because the rank-trace certificate is linear in the two matrix moments, it commutes with family averaging as a direct sum, and character orthogonality then replaces Montgomery-Vaughan as the mean-value engine - restoring full bandwidth at conductor scale and yielding unconditional two-thirds (and, with q-averaging, eleven-twelfths) theorems at polylogarithmic height, once one classical missing lemma (a Gevrey/Ingham-class taper with near-exponential Fourier decay) is supplied.

### Mechanism
THE CORE OBSERVATION. The entire Zeta23 certificate is linear in the two matrix moments: Lemma R at c=2 gives, per L-function, s1 >= 4 tr Ahat - ||Ahat||_F^2 - 2N, and tr and ||.||_F^2 are additive over block-diagonal direct sums. So for ANY finite family F of L-functions the certificate applies verbatim to the direct sum matrix DirectSum_{chi in F} Ghat_chi: sum_chi s1(chi) >= 4 sum_chi tr Ghat_chi - sum_chi ||Ghat_chi||_F^2 - 2 sum_chi N_chi. Averaging is therefore free on the zero side and the linear algebra; ALL new work is on the prime side, where the family average is computed by orthogonality instead of the Montgomery-Vaughan absolute-value bound. This changes which off-diagonal prime pairs must be controlled, and that is the entire game.

SUB-DIRECTION (i), TIER 1 - chi-AVERAGE AT FIXED PRIME q, T = polylog(q). Setup: for each chi != chi0 mod q (q prime, so all nonprincipal chi primitive), the paper's Theorem-E machinery gives the Weil form W_chi(f,g) = sum_rho m_rho h_f(gamma_rho) h_g(gamma_rho) with density nu_{X,chi} = mu_chi + P_{X,chi}, P_{X,chi}(tau) = -(1/pi) Re sum_{n<=X} Lambda(n) chi(n) n^{-1/2-i tau}. Gabor grid tau_k = T + 2 pi k/L, k < d = floor(LT/2pi), L = Lambda * l_q, l_q := log(qT/2pi), X = e^L = (qT/2pi)^Lambda. Per-character zero side (formalized, arithmetic-free): Ahat_chi = P_chi + Q_chi, rank P_chi <= s1+s2, tr P_chi <= N_on, n_+(Q_chi) <= p, regardless of q,T.

First moment averaged: sum_{chi != chi0} tr Gtilde_chi = phi(q) a L Nbar (1+o(1)) with Nbar = (T/2pi) l_q, because (a) the mu_chi term is deterministic (Stirling, uniform in q), (b) sum_chi chi(n) = phi(q) 1_{n == 1 mod q} and n <= X < q forces n=1 (no prime-power terms AT ALL), (c) removing chi0 costs one character's trivial bound << L sqrt(X) = o(phi(q) T l_q). Second moment averaged: expanding P_{X,chi}(tau) P_{X,chi}(tau') produces chi(n)chibar(m)-terms and chi(n)chi(m)-terms. Orthogonality: sum_chi chi(n)chibar(m) = phi(q) 1_{n == m mod q}; for n,m <= X < q this forces n = m - THE OFF-DIAGONAL VANISHES IDENTICALLY. No Montgomery-Vaughan, no cancellation argument, nothing: the binding constraint X <= T^{1-eps} of the t-aspect is replaced by X < q, i.e. normalized bandwidth Lambda* = log q/log(qT) -> 1 when T = q^{o(1)}. The dual terms (nm == 1 mod q, nm = 1 + jq <= X^2) are bounded trivially by L^2 X/q = L^2 (qT)^Lambda / q -> 0 for Lambda <= 1 - 1/loglog q (even this close to 1, X/q <= e^{-log q/loglog q} T beats every polylog). Hence sum_{chi != chi0} tr Gtilde_chi^2 = phi(q) (T L/2pi)(l_q^2 + L^2/3)(1+o(1)), i.e. the averaged kappa is EXACTLY the paper's kappa(Lambda) = 1/Lambda + Lambda/3 -> 4/3, and the averaged certificate gives H(Lambda) -> 2/3 simple-and-on-line, 5/6 distinct, on average over the family - with T only polylog(q).

THE NAMED MISSING PIECE - THE TAPER (designed here). At T = polylog(q) the paper's Prop 4.2 dies: theta_0 ~ X^{1/2} log(4T)/D0^2 with X^{1/2} = (qT)^{Lambda/2} = q^{Lambda/2 + o(1)} while D0 <= T = polylog(q). The X^{1/2} = e^{L sup|Im gamma|} factor is unavoidable (zeros can be anywhere in the strip); what must improve is the REAL-AXIS decay of phihat: the C^3 ramp's r^{-2} is hopeless; we need near-exponential decay from a compactly supported window. Paley-Wiener forbids exp(-c|r|); Gevrey-s tapers give exp(-c|r|^{1/s}); the OPTIMAL choice is an Ingham taper: phi = 1_{[-(L-w)/2,(L-w)/2]} star psi, psi = star-product over j >= 1 of (2w_j)^{-1} 1_{[-w_j, w_j]}, with widths w_j = c_delta * w/(j (log(j+2))^{1+delta}), sum w_j = w/2, w = 1 fixed. Then phihat(z) = 2 sin((L-w)z/2)/z * prod_j sinc(w_j z), supp phi = [-L/2, L/2] exactly, phi = 1 on the middle, and the elementary bounds |sin(w(r-iy))| <= e^{w|y|}, |sinc(w_j(r-iy))| <= e^{w_j|y|} min(1, 1/(w_j|r|)) multiply to TAPER LEMMA T0: |phihat(r-iy)| <= C_delta e^{|y|L/2} exp(-|r|/(C_delta (1+log_+|r|)^{1+delta})) for all real r, |y| <= 1/2 (Ingham's criterion integral of eps(t)/t dt < infinity is exactly what permits eps(r) = c/(log r)^{1+delta}). Tail estimate with T0: with D0 = T/loglog q, ||E_chi|| <= sum_{gamma notin I'} m_rho ||u_rho||^2 <= C e^{L/2} * log(q) * exp(-2 c D0/(log D0)^{1+delta}) * (zero-density factor), which is o(1/q) as soon as T/(log T)^{1+delta} >= C' Lambda log q loglog q, i.e. T >= (log q)^{1+delta'} for any delta' > delta. The same near-exponential decay also repairs every window-restriction error in the Section 5 reduction (the sqrt(X)-pointwise bound on P_X now meets exp(-cD0/(log D0)^{1+delta}) instead of D0^{-2}), which is why the whole prime side re-runs at tiny T. This T0 is the single new analytic lemma; everything else is bookkeeping against the existing formalized pipeline.

Boundary bookkeeping: N_II (zeros in the D0-collars) << phi(q) D0 l_q = o(phi(q) T l_q) since D0 = T/loglog q; per-character RvM error O(log qT) sums to phi(q) O(log q) = o(phi(q) T l_q) since T -> infinity. Grid dimension d = floor(LT/2pi) = Lambda Nbar (1+o(1)) since log T/l_q -> 0. All checks pass; assembly gives F1 below.

Now sum over q of phi(q) 1_{n == m mod q} converts the off-diagonal n - m = jq into averaged shifted convolutions sum_j sum_q w(q/Q) Lambda(n) Lambda(n+jq) - evaluable UNCONDITIONALLY by dispersion/asymptotic-large-sieve technology (Conrey-Iwaniec-Soundararajan) for X up to Q^{2-eps}: effective bandwidth Lambda* = 2. SUB-DIRECTION (ii) - DERIVATIVE ESCALATION (designed, subordinate). Per-derivative transfer: Gauss-Lucas for genus-1 real entire functions puts all zeros of xi^{(k)} in the closed strip unconditionally (induction from xi; the paper's k=1 open-strip and Re xi'/xi > 0 arguments extend by the same Laguerre-type induction). The xi'-VARIATIONAL PROBLEM (replacing the ad hoc quartic window): the functional is c^{(1)}(v) = lambda (int v)^2/(int v^2 + lambda^2 int int K^{(1)}(s-s') v v) with K^{(1)} the xi'-two-point kernel = |x| plus explicit lower-order polynomial corrections from the FGL density; differentiating the Euler-Lagrange identity twice yields a constant-coefficient linear ODE of order 2 or 4 (v'' + 2 lambda^2 (a_1 v + b_1 v'') = const), whose solutions are cos/cosh combinations - a closed-form optimizer replacing 0.86864 by an exact constant (predicted 0.870-0.875, below the RH-conditional 0.8825). k -> infinity: kappa_k -> 1, so unconditionally 'for every eps > 0 there is k(eps) with proportion >= 1 - eps of zeros of xi^{(k)} simple AND on the line' - de-conditionalizing the Farmer-Gonek program. HONEST LIMIT: this yields nothing for zeta itself: differentiation is smoothing, Rodgers-Tao Lambda >= 0 says there is no margin for any un-smoothing bootstrap, interlacing arguments require realness of the zeros being descended from, and Conrey (1983) already gives on-line proportion -> 1 for xi^{(k)} without RH content. The new content is per-k simplicity constants, k=1 optimality, and the family-of-derivatives ladder; it is a proportions machine for derivative zeros, not a zeta channel.

### Obstruction evasion

- **DH/Epstein filter (functional equation + explicit formula + L2 means hold, RH false)** — Out of scope, stated honestly: this route claims improved proportions on average over families, never full RH. It nonetheless interacts correctly with the filter: Davenport-Heilbronn-type objects have -F'/F coefficients with sum |c(n)|^2 ~ x^{1+delta}, so the averaged diagonal (T/pi) sum Lambda(n)^2 n^{-1} g(log n) blows up and the certificate is empty for them (paper Remark 7.2(iii) last sentence); and DH functions do not form an orthogonal family {chi mod q}, so the orthogonality step that replaces Montgomery-Vaughan has no DH analogue. The mechanism consumes Euler-product-derived coefficients Lambda(n)chi(n) with |coefficients| = Lambda(n) (Ramanujan trivially) plus family orthogonality - inputs the RH-false controls violate - but the output is still a lower-bound certificate insensitive to o(N) off-line zeros, so no RH claim is made.
- **Alternative Hypothesis (bandwidth-1 pair data cannot distinguish GUE from half-integer spacings)** — Tier 1 (chi-average at fixed q, bandwidth Lambda < 1) is a lower-bound certificate, indifferent to AH. Tier 2 (Ozluk regime, q-and-chi double average, effective bandwidth up to 2) consumes form-factor information on (1,2), strictly outside the AH-consistency window: an unconditional evaluation of the family form factor F_fam(alpha) = 1 + o(1) on any subinterval of (1,2) refutes AH on average over the Dirichlet family - a concrete refutation channel this program produces as a by-product (Ozluk proved it under GRH; milestone M5 removes GRH).
- **Formalized bandwidth-one certificate ceiling 0.6818287 (PairCeiling)** — Tier 1 stays below the ceiling (targets 2/3 -> 0.6725 averaged), so no violation is claimed. Tier 2 escapes it legitimately: the ceiling binds certificates reading only the form-factor measure on [0,1] configuration-by-configuration, and the repo's own scope note lists 'family averaging' and 'bandwidth > 1' as explicitly OUTSIDE the ceiling. q-averaging converts the off-diagonal n = m + hq prime pairs into unconditionally evaluable averaged shifted convolutions, i.e. genuine form-factor data on (1,2) - the one currency the ceiling's price list says buys 0.70/0.80/0.90 at supports 1.04/1.26/1.70.
- **lemmaR_tight (two-moment rank-trace certificate exhausted)** — No improvement of the linear algebra is claimed. The certificate used is exactly the mass-linear Lemma R at c = 2 and c = 3, applied to the block-diagonal direct sum over the family; tightness concerns the invariant list (tr, Frobenius, integer atoms, n_+), which we keep. What changes is the arithmetic INPUT (the value of kappa fed to the parametric count_certificate), via orthogonality replacing Montgomery-Vaughan - the only resource the campaign identified as non-exhausted.
- **Dimension cap d = lambda*N (nothing below lambda = 1/2, 100% only at lambda = 1)** — The cap is per-L-function and remains respected: Tier 1 operates at Lambda -> 1^- where the cap allows 100%, and certifies 2/3 < 1. In Tier 2 (Lambda in (1,2)) the grid has d ~ Lambda*N_bar > N_bar per character, so the rank/index cap is slack, not binding. The cap's vacuity threshold Lambda > 1/2 is exactly why individual GL(2) gives nothing (Lambda* = 1/2) and why the Petersson-averaged GL(2) family (Lambda* restored to 1) is the right object - the design works with the cap, not against it.
- **kappa(lambda) = 1/lambda + lambda/3 >= 2/sqrt(3), certificates capped at 0.8453 at any bandwidth** — That cap presumes the second-moment constant 1/lambda + lambda/3, i.e. form factor |alpha| at ALL frequencies (Poissonian growth). The family-averaged second moment at Lambda in (1,2) has the strictly smaller plateau value: kappa_fam(Lambda) = 1/Lambda + (Lambda - 2/3 + (Lambda-1)^2)/Lambda^2, giving 2 - kappa_fam(2) = 11/12 = 0.9167 > 0.8453 at Lambda = 2, and kappa_fam -> 1 as Lambda grows. The formalized cap is a statement about the |alpha|-form-factor formula, not about certificates fed a proven smaller kappa; the Lean assembly (count_certificate) is deliberately parametric in kappa, so the smaller family constant plugs in with zero new formal work. No proportion-1 claim is made at any finite Lambda.
- **Bombieri small-support Weil positivity is unconditional (small cones prove nothing)** — No Weil-positivity cone is used anywhere. The first substantive step is not positivity of W on a cone but an EVALUATION of two averaged moments of compressions of W_chi, converted to counts by inertia + rank-trace - the same non-positivity-based mechanism as the anchor paper. The 'new leverage' demanded by the campaign residue ('every route's first step was Weil positivity in disguise') is new unconditional arithmetic evaluations (character orthogonality now, dispersion/asymptotic-large-sieve later), not any new positivity.
- **Bombieri-Garrett ~94% pseudo-Laplacian spectral cap** — Out of scope: no self-adjoint operator or pseudo-Laplacian is invoked; zeros are never embedded in a discrete spectrum. The method is finite-dimensional compression + inertia, which the Bombieri-Garrett argument does not address. (Numerically our horizon 11/12 = 91.7% also sits below 94%, so even a spectral reading would not collide.)
- **Parity problem for sieve-only routes** — Not a sieve. The prime side only ever uses Lambda(n)^2-diagonal evaluations (Chebyshev-Mertens strength) plus orthogonality of characters; no sifting of integers, no parity-sensitive weights. Milestone M5's averaged shifted convolutions sum Lambda*Lambda over arithmetic progressions ON AVERAGE over many moduli, which is large-sieve territory, not parity-blocked sieve territory.
- **Conrey-Li counterexamples to de Branges positivity** — Out of scope: no de Branges space, no K_a kernel, no positivity condition of that type appears. The route stays within the Hermitian-compression certificate class whose formal properties are already machine-checked.
- **Rodgers-Tao Lambda >= 0 (no sub-critical margin)** — Binding for sub-direction (ii) and honestly absorbed: differentiation of xi is a smoothing (heat-flow-like) operation, so Lambda >= 0 means results about xi^(k) can NEVER be pulled back to xi/zeta by any un-smoothing bootstrap; moreover Conrey (1983) already gets on-line proportion -> 1 for xi^(k) unconditionally, confirming that 'proportion -> 1 as k -> infinity' carries no RH content. The derivative-escalation deliverables are therefore claimed only as per-fixed-k quantitative SIMPLICITY constants (new even for k = 1 via the solved variational problem), with the k -> infinity limit stated as a de-conditionalization of Farmer-Gonek-Lee, explicitly not a zeta statement.

### First theorem
THEOREM F1 (family-averaged two-thirds at polylogarithmic height). (Corollary via Markov: for every x in [0,1], the fraction of characters chi with N0s_chi >= x*N_chi is at least (2/3 - o(1) - x)/(1-x); e.g. half of all characters have a third of their zeros simple-and-on-line at height polylog q.) ### Failure modes

- Taper strip-uniformity: the Ingham product taper's bound |phihat(r-iy)| <= e^{|y|L/2} exp(-c|r|/(log|r|)^{1+delta}) may only kick in beyond a crossover radius r0 growing with L; if r0 exceeds (log q)^{1+delta} the theorem degrades to T >= (log q)^{A} for a larger explicit A(delta). This weakens the headline ('polylog height' survives, 'nearly log q' may not).
- Uniform error bookkeeping at tiny T: the paper's Section 5 error field E_T was engineered for T -> infinity at fixed lambda; re-deriving every cross/end/restriction term uniformly in (q,T) with T = polylog(q) may surface a term (e.g. from the mu_chi^2 integral or window ends N_II) forcing T >= (log q)^{2+delta} or an extra (loglog q)-loss in the o(1). Manageable but could add months.
- Tier 2 dispersion wall: the unconditional evaluation of the (1,2)-band family form factor (averaged shifted convolutions sum_q sum_j Lambda(n)Lambda(n+jq)) may stall at Lambda = 1 + c for small c rather than reaching 2, if the required distribution of Lambda*Lambda in progressions exceeds Bombieri-Vinogradov strength in the needed ranges; fallback is a partial-range theorem (e.g. averaged 0.70-0.75 instead of 11/12), still past the bandwidth-one ceiling. Ozluk's original argument used GRH; how much of it the asymptotic large sieve replaces must be verified against Conrey-Iwaniec-Soundararajan's actual reach.
- Literature verification risk (flagged): the exact statements/constants recalled from memory - Ozluk 1996 (GRH, F_Q on |alpha|<2, 11/12 simple), Conrey-Iwaniec-Soundararajan asymptotic large sieve critical-zeros proportion (~56-58%), Conrey 1983 xi^(k) on-line proportions, Farmer-Gonek-Lee xi^(k) second-moment constants - must be checked before claims of 'first'/'beats X' are published. If CIS-type technology already yields a polylog-height statement somewhere, novelty of F1 narrows to the 2/3-simple constant and the tiny-T regime.
- GL(2) family subtleties: Petersson averaging introduces harmonic weights; converting weighted zero-proportions to natural ones, and controlling the Kloosterman/Bessel off-diagonal uniformly at T = polylog(kN), may cost a constant or restrict to weight/level ranges; the M4 constant could land below 2/3.
- Aggregation weakness of the deliverable itself: an average lower bound over chi gives per-character statements only via Markov (e.g. half the characters have >= 1/3 on line at polylog height); a referee may discount 'on average' claims. Mitigation: state per-character corollaries explicitly and honestly.
- Numerical falsification risk (which is also a feature): the Part-B end-to-end experiment at q = 997 could reveal that the averaged finite-size certificate converges more slowly than the t-aspect factor a^2(1+Lambda^2/3)/(b+Lambda^2 J) predicts; that would not kill the asymptotic theorem but would remove the 'numerically visible' selling point and might expose a missed error term.
