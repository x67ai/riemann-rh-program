## Thesis

Rodgers–Tao Λ ≥ 0 (arXiv:1801.05914, program-verified) together with de Bruijn–Newman (RH ⟺ Λ ≤ 0) makes **RH ⟺ Λ = 0**, so a certified strictly positive lower bound Λ ≥ 10^{−N} is a well-defined, finite-witness DISPROOF of RH — and per the program's own findings log and the sweep scout's targeted arXiv sweep, **no serious counterexample-search program exists anywhere** (Farmer arXiv:2211.11671, abstract-verified; no 2022–2026 paper proposes or executes a computational off-line-zero search). D1 stands this channel up as an engineering program with the one asset no prior verification effort had: the zeta-23 formal-verification DNA. The arm builds (1) machine-checked **witness formats** that convert any future find — by this program or anyone — into a kernel-checked disproof of RH (the DRAT-certificate analog for analytic number theory), (2) a **formal Λ-bracket** (Lean-verified instance of the Polymath15 pipeline), and (3) a **targeted certified search** whose search space is pruned by B2's wanted poster and pointed by two calibrated detection statistics this program now owns (the conductor-Fuchs anomaly baseline; the KKT partial-sum repulsion row). It is a refutation channel ONLY: by Dobner's theorem (below) it contains no Euler-product-sensitive generator and can never be upgraded into proof machinery — which is exactly why it can be kept honestly separate from Tracks A/B/C.

## Mechanism — the certified-search architecture

### (a) The Λ>0 channel (verdict-grade core)

**Logic chain (verified twice in the Phase-4 cycle; wording repaired per D-R8, 2026-08-26):** Λ ≥ 0 (Rodgers–Tao) + (RH ⟺ Λ ≤ 0) ⟹ RH ⟺ Λ = 0. By the definition of Λ (de Bruijn monotonicity + Hurwitz closure give {t : H_t has only real zeros} = [Λ, ∞) exactly), **Λ > t₀ ⟺ H_{t₀} has a non-real zero** — re-derived correct by both killers, including the t₀ = Λ edge case. The arm uses the WITNESS direction only: a certified non-real zero of H_{t₀} ⟹ Λ > t₀ ≥ 10^{−N} (the converse "exactly" fails in the degenerate case Λ = 10^{−N}, which the channel never needs; and W3/W4 certify Λ > 0 with no zero exhibited, so exhibition is sufficient, not necessary). This is a **finite argument-principle/interval-arithmetic computation** (Polymath15's effective A_t + B_t machinery run in reverse: as evaluator inside a winding-number certificate rather than as a barrier). The t₀ = 0 endpoint is the direct route: a certified off-line zero of ζ gives Λ > 0 immediately — by the classical de Bruijn–Newman equivalence (¬RH ⟹ Λ > 0 is the contrapositive of RH ⟺ Λ ≤ 0; Rodgers–Tao is the OTHER half, Λ ≥ 0 — attribution fixed per D-R8). B2 (line: "no independent shortcut exists") records why this is the whole channel: backward heat flow from bracket data is exponentially ill-posed, and forward flow of an RH-true configuration keeps all zeros real for every t > 0. M5's t₀ selection needs no B2-M6 bridge for correctness (binary search over t₀ suffices; the bridge buys efficiency only).

**Witness formats** (the arm's product line; W1 is the core, the rest are cheap complements):

- **W1 — argument-principle box** (from the sweep scout's Certified Disproof Interface, half A): R = [σ₁,σ₂]×[T₁,T₂] with ½ < σ₁ ≤ σ₂ < 1 (so the pole at s = 1 is outside R). A ball-arithmetic enclosure of (1/2πi)∮_{∂R} ζ′/ζ ds of width < 1/2 containing an integer m ≥ 1, plus a certified nonvanishing enclosure of ζ on ∂R, certifies m zeros in R with Re s ≥ σ₁ > 1/2 — hence ¬RH, hence Λ > 0 (classical de Bruijn–Newman; attribution fixed per D-R8). The identical format with the Polymath15 evaluator certifies a non-real zero of H_{t₀}, i.e. Λ > t₀ quantitatively.
- **W2 — KKT certified large partial sum** (the wave-2 closure's row (vii); arXiv:2408.03938, abstract-verified): large partial sums of Dirichlet coefficients λ_π(n) strongly repel low-lying zeros of L(s,π) away from the critical line — so a **certified partial-sum evaluation past the KKT threshold is a machine-checkable GRH-disproof witness**, complementary to Λ>0: it is an *arithmetic/coefficient-side* witness where W1 is *analytic/zero-side*, and a certified sum is a vastly cheaper computation than a zero hunt. (Body-read of 2408.03938 required before the format is specified — the exact thresholds, uniformity, and family scope are not yet on disk; see Risks.)
- **W3 — Weil-form negativity witness** [format spec repaired per D-R7, 2026-08-26]: by the witness direction of Weil's criterion (RH ⟹ W(g,g) ≥ 0 on autocorrelation tests g = f ⋆ f̃ — this direction needs only the explicit formula, re-derived in-cycle), a certified W(f,f) < 0 for one explicit test function f is a disproof certificate consisting of a single certified evaluation — **which MUST include the pole contribution h(±i/2) alongside the archimedean term and the finite prime sum** (routing caveat 12's sign/normalization trap), and MUST be evaluated on the autocorrelation/PSD class only (v†Wv in the Gram convention, as the `results/ccm-dh-test/` weilform stack correctly does — negativity of a non-autocorrelation functional certifies nothing). This is the ζ-side twin of the DH variational witness in M0 below; the CCM experiment supplies the calibrated machinery and shows exactly how such a witness localizes at the off-line height.
- **W4 — Robin integer witness** (twin channel, absurdly cheap to keep warm) [wording repaired per D-R7]: RH ⟺ σ(n) < e^γ n log log n for all n > 5040 (Robin 1984; the witness direction — one certified violator ⟹ ¬RH — is the easy direction); the verified structure theorems are "the LEAST counterexample is superabundant" (Akbary–Friggstad 2009) and "if any counterexample exists, a colossally abundant one exists" (arXiv:2510.23889) — so the CA-restricted cron search remains complete for the existence question; a certified violating n is an elementary-arithmetic disproof witness.

### (b) Wanted-poster-guided search-space pruning (B2's constraints as certified filters)

The search is **targeted, never exhaustive** — B2's outputs define where any counterexample can hide:

1. **Target selection.** B2 M2's certified profile: any counterexample is *shallow* (δ ≲ 1/log γ — deep zeros are o(N)-rare by zero-density theorems), *locally sparse at every polynomial scale* γ^θ, θ > 3−√6 (M1(b): off-line local density ≤ 1/θ + θ/3 − 1; ≤ 0.334 at θ = 1), *mirror-paired*, and immersed in a certified ≥ 2/3-simple on-line environment. Operational consequence (B2 payoff, verbatim): hunt **near-degenerate LOCAL configurations, not deep excursions** — i.e. Lehmer-pair-like near-degeneracies. Empirical prior: Stopple arXiv:1508.05870 (verified): 855 strong Lehmer pairs among 114,661 zero pairs near t = 10^6; isolated windows at heights 10^20–10^36 via Bober–Hiary-style multi-evaluation (arXiv:1607.00709, verified-to-exist; claimed heights NOT confirmed from the abstract) rather than systematic sweeps.
2. **Claim screening.** Every claimed disproof (numerical or theoretical, by anyone) must match the poster profile — D1 runs the screen and, when a claim survives it, aims W1 at it. This turns B2's "citable counterexample profile" into an executable service.
3. **Exclusion ledger.** Every search-negative window becomes a **certified exclusion region** (RH verified in that box, machine-checked at the transcript level) — recorded in a durable ledger under `results/`. The poster tells us these negatives are exactly where they carry information (the only regime a counterexample could occupy).

Honesty inherited from B2's verdicts: the poster constrains only Ω(N)-dense off-line ensembles at scales ≥ γ^{0.5505}; isolated Lehmer-type configurations pass every poster constraint vacuously. So the poster prunes and prioritizes; it never guarantees the needle is findable. That is the correct division: the filters are certified, the prior is not.

## Honesty section — expected value if RH is true

If RH is true — the program's working assumption everywhere else — every search returns negative forever. The arm is commissioned anyway because each product is priced under RH-true:

- **Witness formats (M1) buy:** the permanent conversion of ANY future find, by anyone, into a machine-checked disproof — an insurance policy that costs once and holds forever; plus the first Lean-checkable certificate format for analytic-number-theory computations (the DRAT analog), reusable by A4's LP rows, B2 M4's edge-row enclosures, and B4's H_t numerics. The zeta-23 `NumericCert` pattern generalized from form-factor grids to contour transcripts is program infrastructure, not search overhead.
- **Formal Λ-bracket (M2) buys:** the first *formalized* statement about Λ (a machine-checked 0 ≤ Λ ≤ 0.22 instance); any bracket narrowing (Λ ≤ 0.15-class) shrinks the "RH barely true" window — a genuine constraint on the space of RH-true worlds — and directly stress-tests B4's flowed certificates (B4's R(σ) family lives at the diffusive scale inside this bracket).
- **Exclusion regions (M3) buy:** extension of the rigorous verification record (stuck at 3·10^{12} since 2021, program-verified) in the *targeted* windows where verification carries maximal information per the poster; every classical result conditional on "RH verified to height X" inherits the improvement.
- **Detector baselines (M0/M4) buy:** the conductor-Fuchs law as calibrated RH-true baseline is a permanent instrument for auditing ANY future spectral-positivity claim (it is already the S1 audit that reclassified CCM); the claim-screening service ends ad-hoc evaluation of circulating "disproofs".
- **Search-negatives as science:** per the program charter, refutations and dead ends are first-class results; a decade of certified negatives in exactly the poster-predicted regime is the strongest computational statement about RH's truth that is honestly obtainable (Farmer's essay is the design document for saying this without over-claiming).

What the arm does NOT buy under RH-true: any progress toward *proving* RH — by construction and by theorem (see barrier self-check, item 5). Its budget must be justified by the list above alone.

## Barrier-zoo self-check — what this arm CANNOT decide

1. **RH-true is undecidable by this arm, structurally.** RH's truth has no finite witness — only its falsity does (the scout's one-line summary of why certified computation was never applied to RH). The Λ-pipeline decays roughly logarithmically in verification height (0.22 near 3·10^{10}-scale input [RECALLED-UNVERIFIED anchor], 0.2-class at 3·10^{12}), so Λ = 0 is unreachable by any finite computation. M2 narrows brackets; it can never close them.
2. **The AH world is untouched.** The Alternative Hypothesis survives every certified constraint the program owns (B2's own assessment), and B2 M5's no-go applies to D1's detectors with full force: no statistic of zeros below finite height decides anything. D1's discipline — detectors are pointers, W1–W4 are the only verdicts — is the design response, not an evasion: a statistics anomaly triggers a box computation; it is never itself evidence against (or for) RH.
3. **The depth ≪ 1/log resolution wall (the e^{c/δ} prime-sum wall from B2).** Statistics and Weil-form detectors see a zero of depth δ only through prime sums of length e^{c/δ} — computationally void for δ ≪ 1. The CCM experiment is the calibrated instance: depth 0.3085 at height 85.7 already demands support μ ≳ 120. Sharpened for search design: in the poster's shallow regime δ ≍ a/log γ the wall e^{c/δ} = γ^{c/a} is *polynomial in height* — that sliver is the searchable regime — while for δ = o(1/log γ) it is superpolynomial and pointers go blind. Crucially, the wall is a FINDING wall, not a certifying wall: box certification at a *known* location costs only ~log(1/δ) digits of precision. D1 can certify any needle someone points at; it cannot see needles below the 1/log resolution.
4. **The Farmer carrier-wave ceiling.** [Corrected Session 6 per D-R9 — the "~10^{10000}" figure does NOT appear in the on-disk full text (p2-12) and is STRUCK; flag resolved.] The citable anchors: Odlyzko's criterion that the true nature of ζ is unlikely to be revealed before S(t) is routinely larger than 100 — with S(t) typically √(½ loglog t), that is t ~ exp(exp(2·10⁴)), beyond any evaluation algorithm (observed record |S(t)| ≈ 3.3455 at t ≈ 7.75·10²⁷, Bober–Hiary). Honest prior, to headline every D1 publication: P(hit at accessible height) is materially nonzero ONLY if RH fails *structurally* rather than by fluctuation. The completeness critic's "nonzero probability of literally deciding RH" is real but small, and the arm's budget is justified by the honesty section, not by this probability.
5. **S1-emptiness — by theorem, and a feature.** Dobner (Acta Arith. 201 (2021), arXiv:2005.05142, verified): Λ ≥ 0 holds for the whole extended Selberg class from the functional equation alone, no Euler product. The entire channel is DH-blind at the machinery level: it consumes the true ζ pointwise but contains no multiplicativity-sensitive generator, and therefore can NEVER be repurposed as proof machinery (S4 empty by construction; the campaign post-mortem's "Weil positivity in disguise" failure mode is structurally impossible here). This is why Track D can be kept strictly separate from proof channels without leakage in either direction. Dobner's theorem doubles as an executable barrier-zoo entry: any proposed proof machinery whose core argument survives transplantation to the extended Selberg class is DH-blind.
6. **On-line degeneracies.** A genuine on-line multiple zero can be counted in a box (multiplicity m ≥ 2) but never certified exactly on-line at finite precision — D1 cannot decide simplicity questions (S3 limitation), and makes no claim on the multiplicity side of the program.
