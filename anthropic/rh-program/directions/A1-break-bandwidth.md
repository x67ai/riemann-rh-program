## Proposal (Phase 3, immutable)

### Thesis
The lambda = 1 wall falls to a one-sided attack: smoothing the height window annihilates the oscillatory part of the off-diagonal prime form O1 (a sharp-cutoff artifact, not arithmetic), and the surviving resonant block — prime-pair correlations at shifts h <= T^eta — needs only the classical Selberg-sieve UPPER bound (constant <= 4, not the Hardy-Littlewood asymptotic) to feed the rank-trace certificate at effective bandwidth 1 + eta, because the certificate consumes the second moment one-sidedly and the marginal value of bandwidth is first-order in eta while the price of sieve-ignorance is second-order.

### Mechanism
OBJECT. The wall is formalized at Zeta23/PrimeSideB/PPOffDiag.lean:40 (O1_bound): the off-diagonal prime form O1 = sum_{n != m <= X} a_n a_m A^-(log n, log m), a_n = Lambda(n)/sqrt(n), is BOUNDED by 16 C (int Phi^2) sum Lambda(n)^2 ~ L^2 X via Montgomery-Vaughan, with zero cancellation used; this forces X <= T (lambda <= 1). Reading the paper's Prop 5.6 proof shows A^- is built from BOUNDARY evaluations of the inner integral int_I (n/m)^{i tau'} d tau' over the SHARP height window I = [T, 2T]: the 1/(y_n - y_m) kernel with phases (n/m)^{iT}, (n/m)^{2iT} is a sharp-cutoff artifact. THE TWO-STEP MECHANISM. Step 1 (oscillation kill — analysis only): replace the sharp Gabor-grid truncation k < d by smooth grid weights omega(tau_k/T), omega in C_c^infinity((3/4,9/4)), omega = 1 on [1,2], ramp 1/log T. The weighted Gram matrix G_omega = Omega^{1/2} G Omega^{1/2} preserves the entire zero-side architecture: P_omega >= 0 with rank <= rank P, n_+(Q_omega) <= n_+(Q) by inertia pull-back (congruence by a PSD diagonal), and the sampling identity gives each zero the real weight omega(gamma_rho/T) — consumed by the k_c-form rank_trace_mult_k which is already stated for arbitrary real weights. On the prime side the inner integral becomes int omega(tau'/T) omega((tau'+x)/T) (n/m)^{i tau'} d tau' = T*(omega^2)-hat(T theta)(1+O(L/T)), theta = log(n/m), which decays faster than any power of (1+T|theta|). Hence: all off-diagonal pairs with |theta| > T^{epsilon-1} contribute O_A(T^{-A}) — the entire Montgomery-Vaughan-bounded oscillatory mass, the O2 sum-frequency terms, and (crucially) the sharp-truncation strip errors of Lemma 5.4 that carry the other X-sized losses, are all annihilated. This is verified numerically: at T = 4000, X = T^{1.1}, the shoulder 40/T < |theta| <= 60/T contributes 10^{-18} of the diagonal. Step 2 (sieve enclosure — the new arithmetic input): the surviving RESONANT block is R_omega = sum_{n != m, |theta| <= T^{epsilon-1}} a_n a_m * 2 pi g(y_n) * T (omega^2)-hat(T theta), i.e. prime pairs n, n+h with 1 <= |h| <= n T^{epsilon-1} <= T^{eta+epsilon} — exactly the Hardy-Littlewood band that encodes the form factor F(alpha) on alpha in (1, lambda]. Its evaluation is HL-hard (the paper says so, section 7.5(a)) — but the CERTIFICATE consumes ||G-hat||_F^2 only through an UPPER bound, and the correlations are termwise NONNEGATIVE in h: 0 <= C_X(h) := sum_{n <= X} Lambda(n) Lambda(n+h) <= (C_s + o(1)) S(h) X unconditionally and uniformly in h (Selberg upper sieve, C_s = 4; Wu 2004 gives 3.3996 — flagged). So for the real kernel w(n,h) of R_omega: R_omega <= C_s * X * sum_h [w]_+ (h) S(h) (1+o(1)) and >= -C_s * X * sum_h [w]_- S(h): a two-sided enclosure with NO correlation asymptotics. CALIBRATION (checked two independent ways): in form-factor language the diagonal prime sum extended to X = T^lambda contributes 1/lambda + lambda/3 (the alpha-triangle with F-diagonal reading |alpha| on ALL of [0, lambda]), and the resonant block carries the correction int_1^lambda (1 - alpha/lambda)(F(alpha) - alpha) d alpha. Under exact HL (F = 1 past 1) this is NEGATIVE: the certificate becomes H_1(lambda) = 2 - 1/lambda - lambda/3 + (lambda-1)^3/(3 lambda^2), and this closed form REPRODUCES THE PAPER'S PRICE LIST: H_1(1.043) = 0.6936, plus Montgomery-Taylor-type window re-optimization ~ +0.006 = 0.70; H_1(1.265) = 0.7917, + window ~ 0.80. Under the sieve enclosure the worst case in the band costs C_s A(omega) (lambda-1)^2/(2 lambda) with A(omega) = int |Re (omega^2)-hat| dv / (2 pi int omega^2) an explicit, optimizable window constant (the center-frequency phase e^{i c T theta} of the height window forces the absolute-envelope form; enlarging the height-window aspect ratio [T, KT] shrinks A like 1/(K-1)). CERTIFICATE VALUE: H_enc(lambda) = 2 - 1/lambda - lambda/3 - C_s A(omega) (lambda-1)^2/(2 lambda) = 2/3 + (2/3) eta - (C_eff/2) eta^2 + O(eta^3), C_eff = C_s A(omega): maximized at eta* = 2/(3 C_eff) with GAIN 2/(9 C_eff) > 0 for ANY finite C_eff — every finite sieve constant buys a strictly positive bandwidth extension, because the marginal value of bandwidth at the edge is first-order ((2/3) eta from the 1/lambda + lambda/3 diagonal) while the price of ignorance in the band is second-order (eta^2, from the vanishing triangle weight at the edge). Beating the formalized 0.6818287 ceiling from the flat window requires C_eff <= 14.6; C_s = 4 with A(omega) <= 3.65 (or C_s = 3.4 with A <= 4.3, or aspect-ratio-enlarged windows) achieves it, and the LP-optimal certificate weight r (Milestone 0) plus Montgomery-Taylor window re-optimization add ~ +0.006 on top. Numerics at toy scale confirm the resonant block is real, HL-sized (ratio 0.80 to the S(h)-weighted prediction at T = 4000) and 5x inside the sieve envelope. WHY THE PAPER MISSED IT: section 7.5(a) frames the off-diagonal as needing EVALUATION ('Hardy-Littlewood asymptotics, equivalently PCC for alpha > 1'); the certificate needs only a one-sided ENCLOSURE, and one-sided prime-pair information of the right order is a 60-year-old sieve theorem. The oscillatory obstruction that made even enclosure look hopeless is a sharp-window artifact removable by smoothing that the fixed Gabor architecture concealed.

### Novelty
Three genuinely new moves relative to the literature. (1) ONE-SIDEDNESS AS THE INTERFACE: everyone who has touched mean values of prime Dirichlet polynomials of length X > T (Goldston-Gonek, Mean values of long Dirichlet polynomials, 1998; Montgomery-Soundararajan 2004; the conditional moment computations in BGSTB24's orbit and the Zeta23 paper's own section 7.5(a)/(f)) treats the off-diagonal as something to EVALUATE, hence conditional on Hardy-Littlewood/PCC-past-1; the observation that a Weil-form POSITIVITY CERTIFICATE consumes the second moment only through an upper bound, so that a 1965-vintage Selberg sieve sandwich (0 <= correlation <= 4 * singular series) is already bandwidth-(1+eta) information, is new — it could not have been made before Aug 2026 because the certificate framework (rank-trace on the Gram compression) did not exist. (2) THE OSCILLATION-KILL DIAGNOSIS: identifying the Montgomery-Vaughan-bounded mass in O1 (Prop 5.6, Lean O1_bound) as a sharp-height-window artifact, removable entirely by smooth grid weights compatible with the inertia architecture (congruence by PSD diagonal + real-weight k_c-form) — the paper smooths in the SPECTRAL variable (taper phi) but never in the HEIGHT variable, and its lambda <= 1 wall is exactly the price. (3) PRICING BEFORE PROVING: the banded extension of the formalized 256-law LP — adding form-factor cap rows F <= C_s on (1, lambda] to the exact-rational LP and recomputing the ceiling — is a new use of the Lean ceiling artifact as a forward-design instrument, giving the exact worth of each candidate sieve constant before any analytic work. It goes beyond: BGSTB (arXiv:2306.04799 — unconditional F only for |alpha| < 1, sharp windows); GS/GLSS (arXiv:2503.15449, 2507.06823 — conditional on PCC); CGdL20/CCLM (F-positivity past 1 assumed under RH; we manufacture an unconditional band substitute); Radziwill's mollifier ceilings and Guth-Maynard zero-density (different currencies entirely); and the Zeta23 paper itself, whose section 7.5(a) states the off-diagonal 'would require' HL — true for evaluation, not for enclosure. Flagged-for-verification recalls: Selberg constant 4 (Halberstam-Richert Ch. 3-4), Wu 2004 constant 3.3996, Mikawa averaged-correlation threshold X^{1/3+eps}, Lavrik's average-HL, existence of a Lean Selberg-sieve development (Mellendijk).

### Obstruction evasion

- **DH/Epstein filter** — Out of scope, stated honestly: this is a proportion-improvement route (target 0.69-0.75, never RH). Bonus: the new input IS prime-specific anyway — the Selberg upper sieve for Lambda(n)Lambda(n+h) uses equidistribution of primes in arithmetic progressions (Euler-product/multiplicativity beyond L2-means). Davenport-Heilbronn coefficients violate it (their |c(n)|^2 grows like x^{1+delta} and no singular-series structure exists), so even the improved-proportion machinery does not extend to DH — consistent with the paper's Remark 7.2 observation that Prop 5.6 already fails for DH.
- **Alternative Hypothesis (bandwidth-1 pair correlation cannot distinguish GUE from half-integer spacing)** — Not evaded — irrelevant. The certificate is a counting lower bound valid against EVERY configuration consistent with the data; it never needs to distinguish GUE from AH. Moreover the new band constraint F restricted to (1,1+eta] in [0, C_s] is strictly more than bandwidth-1 data, so the AH-indistinguishability theorem (arXiv:2507.06823) does not apply to the enlarged data class; we make no claim of ruling AH out.
- **Bandwidth-one certificate ceiling 0.6818287 (formalized, PairCeiling)** — Evaded by consuming bandwidth-(1+eta) data, which the formalized ceiling explicitly excludes from its scope (the Lean theorem covers certificates reading only the form-factor measure on [0,1]; the paper's own scope caveat lists 'bandwidth > 1' as outside). Concretely: the 256-law hides an atom of mass D(1)=0.824 exactly AT alpha=1, invisible to any r with r(1)=0; our sieve enclosure caps the smoothed form-factor mass on (1, 1+eta] by C_s*eta + o(1), which the banded LP (Milestone 0) uses to price how much of that atom is killed. The certificate value 2/3 + 2/(9 C_eff) exceeds 0.6818287 whenever the effective constant C_eff = C_s * A(omega) < 14.6 — the design's central quantitative gate, computed numerically before any proof effort.
- **lemmaR_tight (two-moment rank-trace certificate exhausted)** — Not contradicted: we do not improve the linear algebra. lemmaR_tight says the certificate is optimal GIVEN tr and ||.||_F^2 at bandwidth-one values; we change the value of ||.||_F^2 by feeding a smaller (bandwidth-1+eta, sieve-enclosed) second moment into the SAME tight inequality. The Lean k_c-form rank_trace_mult_k is already stated for arbitrary real weights, which is exactly what the omega-weighted compression produces.
- **Dimension cap d = lambda*N (nothing below lambda=1/2, 100% only at lambda=1)** — Works in our favor: at lambda = 1.08-1.27 the cap is 108%-127% > our targets 0.69-0.75. The cap direction (certificate <= lambda*N) is slack throughout the proposed operating range.
- **kappa(lambda) = 1/lambda + lambda/3 >= 2/sqrt(3): trace+Frobenius certificates cap at 0.8453 at ANY bandwidth** — Respected: the horizon target 0.75 (full averaged-HL, C_s -> 1, lambda ~ 1.27) sits below 0.8453. The route's asymptote is the kappa-cap, honestly acknowledged; passing 0.8453 would need the fourth moment (HL* route, Milestone 5) or new invariants, not claimed here.
- **Bombieri small-support Weil positivity unconditional (small cones prove nothing)** — Out of scope: we never argue W >= 0 on any cone. The mechanism is a counting certificate (lower bound on simple on-line zeros); Bombieri's theorem concerns proving positivity, which we neither need nor claim.
- **Bombieri-Garrett ~94% pseudo-Laplacian spectral cap** — Different architecture: no self-adjoint operator whose spectrum is claimed to contain zeros; the Gram compression is read only through inertia/trace/Frobenius. Our ceilings (kappa-cap 0.8453) are below 94% anyway.
- **Parity problem for sieve-only routes** — Confronted head-on and priced: parity is exactly why C_s cannot be pushed to 1 by sieve alone (pointwise twin-prime upper bounds are stuck at 3.3996 >= 2, plausibly permanently >= 2). The design degrades gracefully in C_s: any finite C_s gives a positive bandwidth gain 2/(9 C_s A). Reaching the C_s -> 1 horizon requires h-averaged dispersion estimates (Lavrik/Mikawa-type, circle method, Deshouillers-Iwaniec), which are NOT sieve-only and hence not parity-blocked; the parity floor caps the sieve-only milestones at ~0.70-0.71, stated explicitly.
- **Conrey-Li counterexamples to de Branges positivity** — Out of scope: no de Branges space, no positivity condition of that type is used.
- **Rodgers-Tao Lambda >= 0 (no sub-critical margin)** — Out of scope: no heat-flow/de Bruijn-Newman deformation is used; the route makes no claim that would require a positive margin below criticality.
- **Campaign residue: 'every route's first substantive step was Weil positivity in disguise'; per-pair quadratic pricing FALSE; Pontryagin counting empty** — The first substantive step here is (a) an elementary Fourier-analytic identity (smooth height weights annihilate the oscillatory off-diagonal — pure analysis, no positivity), and (b) a classical sieve upper bound on prime-pair correlations. Neither is Weil positivity in any disguise; the positivity certificate is the EXISTING Zeta23 machine, reused as a consumer. The new leverage is precisely the currency the campaign identified as missing: arithmetic input beyond bandwidth one (paper section 7.5(a) names HL-type prime-pair information as the sole obstruction; the overlooked gap is that a positivity certificate needs only a one-sided ENCLOSURE of O1, not its evaluation, and enclosure is unconditionally available from sieves). No per-pair quadratic pricing is used (mass-linear Lemma R only), and no negative-index counting.

### First theorem
THEOREM (Sieve-enclosed smoothed second moment at bandwidth lambda = 1+eta). Fix lambda in (1, 6/5), a C^3 taper phi as in the Zeta23 paper with L = lambda*log(T/2pi), X = e^L = (T/2pi)^lambda, and a height weight omega in C_c^infinity((3/4, 9/4)), 0 <= omega <= 1, omega = 1 on [1,2], with ramp width 1/log T. Let G_omega := Omega^{1/2} G Omega^{1/2} be the omega-weighted Gabor-Gram matrix of the Weil form (Omega = diag(omega(tau_k/T))), in the paper's units. Then UNCONDITIONALLY, as T -> infinity: (a) [diagonal] tr G_omega-hat = N_omega (1+o(1)) with N_omega = sum_rho m_rho omega(gamma_rho/T); (b) [oscillation kill] the off-diagonal prime form restricted to |log(n/m)| > T^{epsilon}/T contributes O_A(T^{-A}) relative to the main term, for every A — a pure Fourier statement from omega-hat decay plus the aliasing-free Poisson identity (no arithmetic input beyond Chebyshev); (c) [sieve enclosure] the surviving resonant block R_omega = sum over prime-power pairs n != m <= X with |log(n/m)| <= T^{epsilon}/T of Lambda(n)Lambda(m)/sqrt(nm) * K_omega(n,m), K_omega(n,m) = 2*pi*g(log n)*T*(omega^2)-hat(T log(n/m)), satisfies |R_omega| <= (C_s + o(1)) * A(omega) * ((lambda-1)^2/(2 lambda)) * (main second-moment term), where C_s = 4 (Selberg upper sieve constant for sum_{n<=X} Lambda(n)Lambda(n+h) <= (C_s+o(1)) S(h) X, uniform in 1 <= |h| <= X^{1-epsilon}) and A(omega) = integral |Re (omega^2)-hat(v)| dv / (2*pi* integral omega^2) is an explicit window constant; (d) [certificate] consequently ||G_omega-hat||_F^2 <= (1/lambda_1 + lambda_1/3 + C_s A(omega) (lambda-1)^2/(2 lambda_1) + o(1)) N, and via the real-weight rank-trace inequality (Lean rank_trace_mult_k), N_0^s(T,2T) >= (H_enc(lambda) - o(1)) N(T,2T) with H_enc(lambda) = 2 - 1/lambda - lambda/3 - C_s A(omega) (lambda-1)^2/(2 lambda), whose maximum over lambda exceeds 2/3 by 2/(9 C_s A(omega)) > 0. STATUS OF INPUTS: the Selberg sieve bound is classical and proven (Halberstam-Richert; constant 4, improvable to 3.3996 by Wu 2004 — flagged for verification); the Poisson/aliasing infrastructure and the six-piece M[.,.] computation exist in the paper and Lean repo and need re-derivation with omega-weights (new but template-following); the weighted rank-trace assembly already exists leak-free for real weights in the Lean repo. ATTACK SKETCH: (1) prove the omega-weighted sampling identity with superpolynomially small aliasing (support of phi-hat*phi-hat is exactly [-L,L]; the omega(./T) modulation smears mass past the boundary only through T*omega-hat(T .)-convolution tails, giving O((wT)^{-1}-scale) leakage); (2) redo Prop 5.5-5.7 with omega-weights — all strip errors previously requiring pointwise |P_X| <= sqrt(X) bounds on sharp boundaries vanish because there is no sharp boundary; (3) split O1 at |theta| = T^{epsilon-1} and apply (b), then (c) with the termwise sandwich 0 <= sum_n Lambda(n)Lambda(n+h) <= C_s S(h) X against the absolute envelope of the kernel; (4) assemble via the k_c-form with real weights, paying O(N/log T) on the ramps. The referee-checkable core is (b)+(c): a statement about prime sums and explicit kernels, with no reference to zeta zeros.
