### MAJOR 1 — The pair-channel closure is overclaimed as covering "the gate's entire admissible class"

Locations and quotes:

* Abstract: "The pair-interference channel — the one gap in the atom-only model theorem — is closed for the gate's entire admissible class by an integrality theorem, with an unconditional 8/9-strength spectral backstop at all depths."
* Section 1.4, headline 8: "closed for the gate's entire admissible class: proved for all single-pair configurations to depth w <= 2.8 ... and all multi-pair configurations to w <= 0.82 unconditionally (w <= 1 modulo one certified crowding constant, ratio 0.9775)".
* Section 4.3, Consequence: "The model-level corner theorem extends from the atom-only class to the gate's entire admissible class: ..." and "Every remaining regime (deep multi-pair beyond the family, ...)".

The problem. The gate's admissible pair class, per the paper's own Section 2.4 and Section 4.3 ("the gate's pair family is w in (0, 1] plus deep probes w in {1.5, 2}"), includes multi-pair columns whose depths involve the deep probes. The proved coverage is: single-pair to w <= 2.8 (Theorem 4.6); multi-pair to w <= 0.82 unconditionally and to w <= 1 modulo the numerically certified crowding constant (Theorem 4.7); equal-depth pair-only at every depth (Theorem 4.8). A column with two or more pairs at mixed depths including w in {1.5, 2} — admissible under Section 2.4 — is covered by none of these; it is floored only by the 8/9 backstop plus generic search numerics. The companion note says this correctly (its Section 8.1 suggested wording, and O2: "multi-pair beyond the R5 family: covered by numerics + Theorem D only"), and the paper's own Section 8.3 (O2) repeats it — so the abstract and headline contradict the paper's own honest residue. Additionally, "deep multi-pair beyond the family" (Section 4.3, Consequence) misdescribes the deep probes as outside the family that Section 2.4 defines them into.

Why it matters. The upgrade of delta_0 = 0 from "exact LP equality on the certified grid" (true, witness-backed) to "exact over the admissible class" runs through P_base(class) = corner, i.e. through (MI) holding on the whole class. Where (MI) is not proved, class-level exactness rests on converged pricing, which Section 8.3 correctly labels heuristic. An undiscovered pair column below the corner would lower P_base only (the anti-absorption direction), so an absorption claim is exactly the claim that needs the closure to be complete. The gap regimes are small and backstopped at 20/27, and nothing in the LP record points at them — but the abstract's "closed for the gate's entire admissible class" states more than is proved.

Required fix. Replace the three quoted passages with the companion note's own scoped phrasing, e.g.: "closed for the gate's pair class throughout the R5 depth family — proved unconditionally for single-pair columns at every gate depth (w <= 2.8) and multi-pair columns at w <= 0.82, modulo one numerically certified crowding constant on the deepest ~20% of the family; multi-pair columns involving the deep probes w in {1.5, 2} are covered by adversarial numerics plus the unconditional 8/9 backstop." In Section 4.3 change "deep multi-pair beyond the family" to "multi-pair columns at the deep-probe depths (inside the gate class but beyond the theorems' range)". One added sentence in 4.3 or 8.3 should state explicitly that class-level exactness of delta_0 = 0 is proved on the closure's regimes and heuristic (pricing-backed, backstopped) elsewhere.
