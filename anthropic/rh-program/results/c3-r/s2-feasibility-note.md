## 0. VERDICT (stated first): **TRANSFERS-WITH-WORK**

The two short exact sequences (1.3.1)/(1.3.2) of [ALKL] — the load-bearing frame of the two-cohomology Lefschetz-distribution design that survives Deninger–Singhof (ledger §7) — **survive the passage from a codimension-one submanifold of a closed manifold to a preserved solenoidal leaf in a compact foliated space with totally disconnected transversal factor, at the price of named, well-posed work and no identified obstruction.** The three findings that carry the verdict:

1. **The conormal direction never was the transversal direction.** Conormality in [ALKL] is measured in the one-dimensional defining-function direction x, which in their own geometry is the FLOW-transverse direction, not the foliation-chart transversal; in the solenoid transplant that direction remains a genuine real line. The totally disconnected factor T enters every definition and every proof only as a passive parameter. In ALKL's own setting the directions along the preserved leaves are leaf directions (their M⁰ is a union of leaves), and the algebra Diff(M, M⁰) is spanned near M⁰ by the b-field x∂ₓ together with leafwise fields ([ALKL] (4.1.1) and p. 101: "X(M, F) spans X(M, M⁰) as C^∞(M)-module by (4.1.1)"). A totally disconnected transversal therefore removes **no generator that the manifold case used**: the absence of transversal derivatives is a **feature** (there is literally nothing to lose), not a gap. §4 row I-1.
2. **Every analytic workhorse in the exactness proofs is one-dimensional in x with all other variables passive.** The surjectivity of R is produced by the partial extension maps E_m, whose construction ([ALKL] Prop. 2.5.1, proof given in the memoir) is: inclusion for m ≥ 0, and for m < 0 the composition ∂ₓᴺ ∘ E₀ ∘ Jᴺ with Ju(x, y) = ∫₁ˣ u(ξ, y)dξ — integration, extension, and differentiation in x alone, with y a spectator. A C(T)-valued spectator changes none of these formulas. §4 rows I-6/I-7.
3. **The functional-analytic frame degrades exactly where the inventory says it can afford to.** In the parameterized spaces, the Montel, Schwartz, and reflexivity properties of [ÁLKL23] FAIL (an infinite-dimensional C(T)-factor kills them; §4 row I-10, honest). But a proof-level inventory of [ÁLKL23]'s exactness arguments (Cor. 7.30, Prop. 6.42 + Claim 6.43, Prop. 8.8) shows the exactness of BOTH sequences consumes only: (a) the partial extension maps; (b) acyclicity of the step spectra — proved there by symbol-seminorm topology-coincidence (Cor. 4.5), an interpolation argument pointwise in the base variable and hence pointwise in T; (c) category-level machinery (Hahn–Banach, De Wilde open mapping for webbed spaces, [Wen03] duality and proj-limit lemmas) that is indifferent to the geometric origin of the spaces. Montel/reflexivity are consumed **nowhere in the sequences' construction or exactness**. §4 rows I-8/I-9/I-10, §5.

**Consequences for the ledger:** S2 stays TRANSFERS-WITH-WORK; Route 2 still has **no OBSTRUCTED row**; the kill-criterion does **not** fire from this leg. The remaining Route-2 blocker is unchanged: the S4 existence problem. The work items W1–W7 (§5) are a concrete, arithmetic-free, publishable analysis program.

**What would have counted as an obstruction, and did not materialize** (§6): a definitional ingredient of the sequences requiring transversal differentiability (none does — I-1); an exactness proof requiring compact Sobolev embeddings or Montel-ness (none does — I-8/I-9); a boundary/cutting construction requiring the ambient space to be a manifold near M⁰ (the constructions are collar-local products in x — I-4, modulo the collar axiom A3, which is honestly a geometric axiom shifted to S4's account).

## 2. The manifold-case machinery, verbatim (task item 1)

### 2.1 The two sequences and the spaces they live on ([ALKL] §1.3.2, pp. 4–5; verified against the rendered page)

The geometric frame: M a closed manifold, F a transversely oriented smooth codimension-one foliation, φ a transversely simple foliated flow, M⁰ the (compact, finite) union of leaves preserved by φ, M¹ = M ∖ M⁰ ([ALKL] §4.1.2, pp. 99–101).

Verbatim ([ALKL] §1.3.2, printed pp. 4–5; the notation "(M, L)" in some displays is the memoir's Chapter-2 notation, where the submanifold is called L; in the Introduction L := M⁰):

> "Let Diff(M, M⁰) be the filtered algebra of differential operators on C^∞(M) generated by C^∞(M) and the vector fields on M tangent to M⁰, and let H^s(M) be the Sobolev space of order s ∈ R. A distribution u ∈ C^{−∞}(M) is said to be conormal at M⁰ of Sobolev order s if Diff(M, M⁰)u ⊂ H^s(M). These distributions form a Fréchet space I^{(s)} = I^{(s)}(M, M⁰) endowed with the projective topology given by the maps P : I^{(s)} → H^s(M) (P ∈ Diff(M, M⁰)). The spaces I^{(s)} form an inductive spectrum defining an LF-space I = I(M, M⁰) = ⋃_s I^{(s)}, with continuous inclusions C^∞(M) ⊂ I ⊂ C^{−∞}(M). (…) In particular, for the density bundle Ω = ΩM, we get the strong dual I′(M, L) = I(M, L; Ω)′, simply denoted by I′. The elements of I′ are called dual-conormal distributions; in fact, C^∞(M) ⊂ I′ ⊂ C^{−∞}(M) with I ∩ I′ = C^∞(M).
> Let also K = K(M, M⁰) ⊂ I be the closed subspace consisting of elements supported in M⁰. On the other hand, via the lift to M̄ [the manifold with boundary obtained by cutting M along M⁰], we get another space, J = J(M, M⁰), which is isomorphic to the space of extendable distributions on M̄ conormal at the boundary [Mel96, Chapter 4]. There are canonical injections C^∞(M) ⊂ J ⊂ C^∞(M¹). Let K′ = K′(M, L) and J′ = J′(M, L) be defined like I′. We get J′ ⊂ C^{−∞}(M). Moreover there are short exact sequences in the category of continuous linear maps between locally convex spaces [Wen03, Chapter 2],
>
> (1.3.1)  0 → K →^ι I →^R J → 0 ,
> (1.3.2)  0 ← K′ ←^{R′} I′ ←^{ι′} J′ ← 0 ,
>
> where ι is the inclusion map and R is defined by restriction to M¹, and (1.3.2) is the transpose of the version of (1.3.1) with Ω (R′ = ι^t and ι′ = R^t). These sequences are relevant because K, J, K′ and J′ have better descriptions than I and I′. So (1.3.1) and (1.3.2) will play an important role."

The leafwise versions ([ALKL] §1.3.2 p. 5 and §5.1 p. 118, (5.1.5); §6.1 p. 131): with coefficients in ΛF, the spaces I(F) = I(M, M⁰; ΛF), I′(F), K(F), J(F), K′(F), J′(F) are topological complexes with d_F carrying R-actions φ* = {φ^{t\,*}}, and (1.3.1)/(1.3.2) hold with ι, R, ι′, R′ cochain maps; the induced sequences in cohomology and reduced cohomology (0 → H•K(F) → H̄•I(F) → H̄•J(F) → 0 and its dual) are Theorems 1.3.3 and 1.3.6 of the memoir (pp. 5–6), proved in §5.5 and §6.5.

## 3. The transplant target, defined (task item 2)

### 3.1 Ambient category (from [Den05] §7.1–7.2, following [MS88] Ch. II)

Verbatim [Den05] §7.1 (p. 28): a **foliated space** with d-dimensional leaves is a separable metrizable space X with charts φᵢ: Uᵢ → Fᵢ × Tᵢ, Fᵢ ⊂ R^d open, such that (a) transitions have the form φⱼ∘φᵢ^{-1}(x, y) = (f_{ij}(x, y), g_{ij}(y)), and (b) "All partial derivatives D_x^α f_{ij}(x, y) exist and are continuous as functions of x and y." A **(generalized) solenoid** of dimension a is such an X locally homeomorphic to Lᵢ × Tᵢ with Tᵢ **totally disconnected** (condition (a) then automatic: "continuous functions from connected subsets of R^a into a totally disconnected space are constant"); smooth structure = an atlas satisfying (b). Tangential objects: T F with fibers the leaf tangent spaces ([Den05] p. 29, after [MS88] p. 43); sections "are by definition continuous and smooth on the leaves"; morphisms = continuous maps, smooth leafwise. [Den05]'s Warning (p. 29): on a foliated manifold the foliated-space sheaves differ from the smooth-foliation sheaves — "in the second one only wants continuity" transversally. A **codimension-one foliation F inside an a-dimensional solenoid (X, L)**: a second foliated-space structure with leaves of dimension d = a − 1, each L-leaf foliated by F-leaves ([Den05] §7.2, pp. 29–30). A **flow**: a continuous R-action, smooth on L-leaves, F-compatible if each φᵗ maps F-leaves to F-leaves ([Den05] §7.3, p. 30).

This is the same uniformity used in the one published trace formula at a totally disconnected transversal: [Lei07a] Definitions 1–2 (pp. 13–14) take charts D × Z_p^m and sections "smooth along the leaves and continuous globally," meaning every leafwise derivative ∂^{r₁}∂^{r₂}a(z, θ) is jointly continuous in (z, θ) ∈ Uᵢ × Z_p^m. The transplant below adopts exactly this design (call it the **uniform model**).

### 3.2 The S2 geometric axioms (the transplant's standing hypotheses)

Let a = 3, d = 2 (the ledger's target dimension). **Axioms:**

- **(A-i)** X a compact smooth solenoid of dimension 3 with a codimension-one foliation F (leaves = surfaces) and an F-compatible flow φ, as in §3.1.
- **(A-ii)** X⁰ ⊂ X a nonempty closed φ-invariant union of F-leaves ("the preserved solenoidal leaf"; it may be a single compact surface leaf, a surface lamination with totally disconnected transversal T₀, or a finite union of such), with X¹ := X ∖ X⁰ and φ transverse to the F-leaves in X¹.
- **(A-iii) (collar + defining function)** There is an open T ⊇ X⁰ and a homeomorphism T ≅ (−ε, ε)ₓ × X⁰ of foliated spaces (tangentially smooth with tangentially smooth inverse) under which X⁰ = {x = 0}, x is tangentially smooth with leafwise differential nonvanishing on X⁰, and the F-plaques of T are the level sets {x = const} × (plaques of X⁰). (Transverse orientability of F along X⁰ is included; the non-orientable variant is handled by the double-cover trick exactly as in [ALKL] §2.6.16, which transfers verbatim since it is a finite covering construction.)
- **(A-iv) (linearized flow at X⁰)** For each component L of X⁰ there is κ_L ∈ R^× with φ̄ᵗ(x) = e^{κ_L t}x on the local transversal in the collar coordinate — the transplant of [ALKL] (4.1.1) (p. 100, after [ÁLKL22, Lemma 3.2]); equivalently Z = κ_L x∂ₓ + (leafwise) near X⁰.
- **(A-v) (invariant transverse measure, full support)** X carries a φ-invariant transverse measure Λ for F of full support, restricting on the collar to (Lebesgue-type in x) — none: Λ is transverse to F, and in the collar it is carried by the x-line times the transversal factor of X⁰; full support is required so that the Λ-duality below is faithful. (For the arithmetic wish-list this is the same transverse measure the counting problem already demands — ledger row R15; in [Den05] Thm. 7.8 it is the "canonical transverse measure μ" with χ_Co(F, μ) = χ(M)·l, pp. 34–35.)
- **(A-vi) (bounded geometry leafwise)** A tangentially smooth leafwise metric on TF (a "Riemannian metric" in [Den05]'s sense, p. 29); X compact makes the leafwise geometry uniformly bounded across the transversal — the foliated-space replacement of [ALKL] H6.

Axioms (A-iii)/(A-iv) are theorems in the manifold case (tubular neighborhood theorem; [ÁLKL22, Lemma 3.2]) and are AXIOMS here; whether the arithmetic object satisfies them is part of the S4 existence problem, not of S2. This is the honest bookkeeping split: **S2 = analysis GIVEN the axioms; S4 = existence of an arithmetic object satisfying them** (see §6.2 for what happens if (A-iii) fails).

### 3.3 The transplanted function spaces (task item 2, the definitions)

Fix a finite atlas {Uᵢ ≅ R³-chart × Tᵢ} of X adapted to X⁰ where Uᵢ meets X⁰ (i.e., collar charts (x, y, t) with y leafwise on X⁰ and t ∈ Tᵢ), and a partition of unity; since each Tᵢ is compact totally disconnected, **partitions by characteristic functions of clopen sets are available in the transversal factor and are tangentially smooth** (locally constant transversally, hence all conditions (b) trivial) — localization is STRICTLY easier than on manifolds. [Derived this session; it is the standard clopen-partition property of totally disconnected compacta, and is the mechanism behind [Den05]'s remark that condition (a) is automatic for solenoids.]

- **C^∞_τ(X)**: tangentially smooth functions, all leafwise derivatives continuous on X (the uniform model, §3.1). A Fréchet space (sup-seminorms of chart derivatives; countable because the atlas is finite and orders are countable).
