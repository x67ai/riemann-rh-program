§ 1Executive summary

The program has completed its foundational question. Sponsor standing order 6 — Grossmann before Riemann — required an exhaustive check of existing mathematics for a ready-made branch fitting the specification an RH-deciding machinery must satisfy, before building new mathematics from scratch. That sweep is now closed: 35 branches scouted across two waves, and no Grossmann exists.

The sweep's one-line verdict: "the generator exists, but the geometry does not." The only positivity mechanism that has ever proved a Riemann Hypothesis — the Hodge index theorem applied to the square of a curve (Weil's proof over function fields) — is published, understood, and available. But the object it needs over the rational numbers (a "square of Spec Z" carrying an intersection theory) exists in no branch of mathematics. The two programs building toward it (Connes–Consani; Deninger) are each stalled at exactly that step, one for eight years. Wave 2 corroborated this three independent ways.

Consequently the program's headline is now a construction order (direction C3): build — or refute the possibility of — the missing doubled arithmetic object. Alongside it: a certified computational refutation arm (D1) exploiting the program's formal-verification DNA; surviving directions with mandatory repairs; adjudicated refutations recorded as first-class results; and an executable barrier zoo that every future proposal must pass before design cost is spent.

Session 6 — every open verdict resolved The whole portfolio has now been through adversarial review. Three decisions landed this session, each computationally verified and independently audited. (1) C3 survives reduced. Two blind-replicated killer fatals were upheld on re-derivation: the novel Tate-curve seed (Ep × Eq) is provably empty — the cross-prime correspondence groups vanish — and the "provable today" S1 lemma is not a theorem under any reading of its own definition. What survives is the honest spine: the Deninger-leg trace-formula transplant as primary leg, the positivity engine as instrument, and the S1 axiom statement with exact witnesses. (2) D1 is verified. Three convergent survives-with-repairs verdicts, zero fatals; its calibration detector confirmed on a fifth conductor (χ₃, +1.2% from the predicted 4π/3); and its cycle caught a program-wide record error — the de Bruijn–Newman bracket of record is 0 ≤ Λ ≤ 0.2 (Platt–Trudgian, Cor. 2), not 0.22. (3) A4's decision gate fired — on the closing branch. The corrected cubic-certificate LP, run against the full adversary class and independently audited, returns separation exactly zero: the cubic route is closed at the proven operating point, and the formalized two-moment wall (lemmaR_tight) stands strengthened, now with a hand-checkable witness. A refutation this clean is a first-class deliverable — it is the program's own barrier-zoo doctrine applied to itself.

§ 2The starting point and its walls

The parent theorem (v5, 2026 — now the primary citation) proves ≥ 2/3 of zeta zeros are simple and on the line, in Lean-formalized form, via a positive-semidefinite "Weil–Gabor" matrix certificate at bandwidth one. What makes it a foundation rather than a stepping stone is that the repo formalizes its own impossibility results:

  • Bandwidth-one ceiling 0.6818287 — the certificate class is exhausted just above the achieved 0.6725.
  • Two-moment cap 0.8453 at any bandwidth — κ(λ) = 1/λ + λ/3 ≥ 2/√3: trace + Frobenius certificates can never reach proportion 1, regardless of prime-correlation progress.
  • lemmaR_tight — an on-line double zero and an off-line pair are indistinguishable to every two-moment functional. This degeneracy, not a lack of analytic strength, is the structural wall.
  • o(N)-blindness — pair correlation alone already gives 100% simple-on-line without giving RH: density methods cannot see o(N) exceptional zeros, and a single off-line zero refutes RH.
  • The DH/Epstein filter — Davenport–Heilbronn and Epstein zeta functions satisfy the functional equation, explicit formula, and L² mean values, yet violate RH. Any full-RH route must consume an input they violate (Euler product / Λ(n) ≥ 0 / Ramanujan) — at the axiom level, not the estimate level.

From these walls the program distilled the specification S1–S6 that any RH-deciding machinery must satisfy: Euler-product sensitivity (S1), sensitivity to a single off-line zero (S2), multiplicity visibility (S3), a genuinely new positivity generator — not a reparametrized Weil-positivity cone (S4), survival of all named no-gos (S5), and — added by the sweep's comparative anatomy of every RH ever proven — a finite-rank/tower-rational trace calculus on a doubled object (S6).

§ 4The Grossmann sweep: closed, negative, productive

Wave 1 (24 branch scouts + synthesizer, ~2.1M tokens, 631 online verification calls) found one marginal candidate (Arakelov/Hodge-index, 0.55), five certified dead ends, and the rest instruments. Wave 2 (11 unswept corners) returned 9 instruments, 2 further dead-end certificates, 0 candidates — and no displacement of the wave-1 shortlist. Three remaining corners are justified watch-only. The grid is closed.

Wave 2's independent corroborations of "the generator exists but the geometry does not":

  • The function-field all-moments theorem (BDPW) is a textbook S6 success — finite-rank trace calculus on a doubled object — running solely on Deligne purity: exactly the missing geometry.
  • BZSV relative-Langlands period positivity is justified, in the authors' own footnote, by the Riemann Hypothesis: positivity flows from RH into the framework, so it cannot be an engine for it. Its number-field theory is explicitly unbuilt.
  • Yuan–Zhang's arithmetic Hodge index is a proven, RH-independent positivity calculus — whose axioms consume only the product formula, so an RH-false lookalike can wear it. Substrate-blind generator; no geometry.

The sweep's negative is a positive deliverable: it fixes the de novo construction's axioms (Rankin–Selberg nonnegativity as the only S1 = 5 in 35 branches; Λ-ring descent; weight purity), its target inequality (Castelnuovo on the square), the S6 spec amendment, calibration theorems, and the substrate candidates — and it certified, with sources, where seven whole branches end.

§ 5Decisive test: the CCM strategy is arithmetic-blind

The Connes–Consani–Moscovici "Zeta Spectral Triples" strategy (arXiv:2511.22755) — finite-rank operators built from Euler products whose spectra match low zeros, with a convergence conjecture that would establish RH — was the sweep's only immediately executable decisive test. The program built the Davenport–Heilbronn analogue and ran it. Verdict: arithmetic-blind (confidence 0.85).

  • DH — which violates RH — passes every probed part of the mechanism: simple even ground state, positive form, and a spectrum reproducing DH's own genuine low zeros to 1.05e−5.
  • The tempting rescue ("zeta's ground eigenvalue collapses many orders faster") was refuted by controls: the collapse rate obeys a clean conductor law c = 4π/q across four systems. RH-true χ₅ and RH-false DH (both conductor 5) collapse at the same rate to 2.4%. The rate measures the conductor, not arithmetic truth.
  • DH's actual off-line zero needs truncation size μ ≳ 120 to become spectrally visible; CCM's own computations reach μ ≤ 17. All S1 content defers to the unproven convergence conjecture — which therefore inherits no DH-filter defense.

Consequences: CCM reclassifies from proof-route candidate to instrument; a circularity no-go is publishable; and the calibrated ε ~ poly·e^(−4πμ/q) baseline becomes a detection statistic for the refutation arm. The by-product lesson is program-wide: S1 input must be consumed at the axiom level; spectral phenomena alone cannot certify it. Session 6 added the predicted fifth calibration point: χ₃ (conductor 3, odd parity) fits c = 4.2397 against the predicted 4π/3 = 4.1888 — 1.2%, the tightest Dirichlet point — with its spectrum reproducing the true first zero of L(s, χ₃) to 4·10⁻¹⁰, and its prefactor showing no arithmetic signature (RH-false DH sits between two RH-true systems in the prefactor band). The law now stands on five systems across both parities.

§ 7The barrier zoo

Three of four early refutations died after design cost to checks that could have run in an afternoon. The zoo (BARRIER-ZOO.md) institutionalizes those checks: 46 barriers in 5 groups — model worlds (DH/Epstein with the one-line ΛDH(12) = −0.76 witness, Alternative Hypothesis, the Beurling counterexample factory…), formalized ceilings, structural no-gos, program-discovered barriers (A2's window-product law; A4's garnish-absorption barrier, now closed by the audited exact-zero gate; C1's cosh ghost; Session 6's IV.10 — Tate-curve products carry no correspondence calculus), and process barriers. Each entry carries an executable test; the brief-time protocol returns any proposal that fails one without an explicit evasion argument — before design cost. Session 6 sharpened the process group with its own lessons: a scout's verification of what a source says is not verification of what can be proved; referee silence is not rebuttal; and self-instrumented briefs convert instead of dying — C3's own gate rules are why it survived reduced where its predecessor was refuted outright. A seven-item formalization queue extends the Lean no-go library; item 5 now has its concrete witness (the gate's grid law is algebraic — Lean-ready), and item 6 was honestly repriced from theorem to axiom statement.
